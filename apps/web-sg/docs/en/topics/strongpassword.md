<p>Creating a strong password is one of the most important steps in preventing unauthorized access to your trade account and portfolio. It helps safeguard your personal information, investments, and financial assets.</p><p>Follow these tips to set a strong password:</p><h3>1. How do I set a strong password? </h3><h4>Account login password</h4><ol><li>Long: At least 12 characters.</li><li>Complex: Include a mix of uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers and symbols.</li><li>Unique: Avoid using common passwords such as “123456”, “password”, or “qwerty”.</li><li>Difficult to guess: Avoid your name, birthdate or phone number. </li><li>Use passphrases: Combine random words into a memorable phrase, such as “Long! bridge$#2025”.</li><li>Substitute letters with symbols or numbers: Example: O becomes 0, A becomes @ - “L0ng! bridg3$#2025”.</li><li>Avoid reusing passwords: Use a unique password for each account.</li><li>Avoid words that can be found in a dictionary: Hackers often use software to guess passwords based on the dictionary</li></ol><p>Examples:</p><ul><li>Good: G@l4xy$Tr0ng! 2025</li><li>Bad: password123</li></ul><p> </p><h4>Trade account password (6-digit code)</h4><ol><li>Use random combinations of numbers, such as “482395”.</li><li>Avoid using personal information such as birthdates, phone numbers</li><li>Choose a password that’s easy for you to remember, but difficult for others to guess</li></ol><p>Examples:</p><ul><li>Good: 428154 (Random combination of numbers)</li><li>Bad: 246135 (Guessable pattern) </li></ul><p> </p><h3>2. How do I protect my password?</h3><ul><li>Never share your password: Keep your password private, even from friends or family.</li><li>Beware of phishing scams: Avoid entering your password on suspicious websites or links.</li><li>Use a password manager: Consider using a trusted password manager to generate and securely store strong passwords.</li></ul><p> </p><h3>3. How do I test my password’s strength?</h3><p>You can check how strong your password is by using the Cyber Security Agency (CSA) of Singapore’s <a href="https://ihp.csa.gov.sg/password-checker">Password Checker</a>. This tool evaluates your password’s strength and provides recommendations to make it stronger. </p><p> </p><h3>4. How often should I update my password? </h3><p>Make it a habit to update your passwords every 6 months, or immediately if you suspect your account has been compromised. Cybersecurity threats evolve, and so should your passwords. </p><p> </p><h3>5. How do I know if my password is strong enough?</h3><p>Use this checklist to confirm if you have a strong password:</p><p>✅ Include at least 12 characters</p><p>✅ Have a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols</p><p>✅ Unique and hard to guess</p><p>✅ Avoids any personal information</p><p>✅ Updated in the last 6 months</p>
