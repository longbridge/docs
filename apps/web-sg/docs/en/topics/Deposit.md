<p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">This article details the complete process of depositing funds to Longbridge. It covers supported currencies, deposit methods, arrival time, and related fees to help you efficiently complete fund transfer operations.</p><h4>1. How to deposit funds to bank account of Long Bridge Securities Pte. Ltd. (&#34;LBS&#34;)？</h4><p style="text-align: justify">Step 1: Transfer funds to bank account of LBS (You can deposit money through DDA, bank transfer, Paynow)</p><p style="text-align: justify">Step 2. Submit deposit notification (For DDA, deposit notification is not required, for bank transfer and Paynow, deposit notification would be required)</p><p style="text-align: justify">Step 3: Your funds will be reflected as deposited into your trading account at LBS and is available for usage as soon as the funds are credited into your account.</p><p> </p><h4>2. What currencies does LBS support for deposit?</h4><p>LBS supports SGD, USD and HKD for deposit.</p><p>You can also do instant conversion between currencies after your funds are successfully credited into your LBS' Trading account.</p><p> </p><h4>3. Does LBS charge deposit fees?</h4><p style="text-align: justify">LBS and its agent bank (DBS) do not charge any fee. However, the remittance bank and/or intermediary bank may impose charges. For any specific cost, please consult your remittance bank.</p><p style="text-align: justify">The exact amount received by LBS will be deposited into your trading account, any bank charges incurred by the remittance bank will be borne by you.</p><p> </p><h4>4. Please use the bank account with the same name as your LBS' account to initiate the deposit. Otherwise, such payments will be rejected. Refunds will be returned to the remitter's account in the same manner as they were transferred. It may take up to 2-4 weeks（The third-party platforms deposit refunds may take 6-8 weeks) and all the bank charges are to be borne by the client.</p><p style="text-align: justify">Banks may take time to process the remittance.
