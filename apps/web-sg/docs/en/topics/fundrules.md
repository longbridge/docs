<p>Fund transactions are processed based on the trading day (T day), with 12:00 as the cutoff. Units purchased before the cutoff will be confirmed within T+4. Proceeds from fund redemptions may take up to 7 trading days to be credited to your account. Specific rules and amount limits are subject to the product details.</p><h2>1. Subscription Rules</h2><h4>Subscription Process</h4><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/139359f27dd6935dd8102c08122d6550"/></figure><p><span style="color: #888888"><strong>Note</strong>: T day refers to the trading day. The cut-off time is 12:00 daily. T (not including 12:00) refers to the time before 12:00 (not inclusive), while T+1 refers to the time after 12:00 (inclusive). Weekends and statutory holidays (subject to the country in which the fund is located) are non-trading days.</span></p><h4>Trading Amount</h4><p>The<strong> minimum initial purchase amount</strong> and <strong>subsequent investment amount</strong> can be found in the Longbridge app &gt; &#34;Market&#34; &gt; &#34;Fund&#34; tab &gt; select your preferred Fund &gt; &#34;Trading Rules&#34; &gt; &#34;Buy&#34; tab.</p><h4>Rules for Canceling Orders</h4><ul><li>Existing orders can be cancelled before 12:00 noon on T day</li><li>Existing orders cannot be cancelled after 12:00 noon on T day. <br/>You must first wait for the units to be confirmed; whereupon, you may request to sell on the second trading day after confirmation of the purchase has been completed.</li></ul><p> </p><h2>2. Redemption Rules</h2><h4>Redemption Process</h4><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/a5c86407a7c4151eda479554255f8b29"/></figure><p><span style="color: #888888"><strong>Note</strong>: T day refers to the trading day. Weekends and statutory holidays (subject to the country in which the fund is located) are non-trading days.</span></p><h4>Trading Amount</h4><p>The<strong> minimum holding unit</strong> and <strong>minimum redemption unit (units to be sold)</strong> can be found in the Longbridge app &gt; &#34;Market&#34; &gt; &#34;Fund&#34; tab &gt; select your preferred Fund &gt; &#34;Trading Rules&#34; &gt; &#34;Sell&#34; tab.</p><h4>Rules for Canceling Orders</h4><p>Order withdrawal requests may be made on the Fund Trading History page in the app.</p><p> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Key takeaways</strong>:</p><ul><li>Purchase rules<ul><li><strong>Cut-off time</strong>: Place an order before 12:00 (exclusive) on the trading day (T day) .</li><li><strong>Unit confirmation: </strong>Units are confirmed on T+4 (postponed for non-trading days).</li><li><strong>View returns</strong>: Estimated T+5</li><li><strong>Cancellation</strong>: Existing orders can be cancelled before 12:00 noon on T day.</li></ul></li><li>Selling rules<ul><li><strong>Cut-off time: </strong>Place the sell order before 12:00 on T day.</li><li><strong>Funds arrival</strong>: Funds arrive within 7 trading days after net worth confirmation (net worth confirmed on T+1).</li><li><strong>Cancellation</strong>: Order withdrawal requests may be made on the Fund Trading History page in the app.</li></ul></li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
