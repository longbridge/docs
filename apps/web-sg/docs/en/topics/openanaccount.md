<p style="margin-left: 0cm">The following are the steps for opening a Longbridge account for users in Singapore.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Download the Longbridge SG App, sign up in the App, and tap <i>Portfolio</i>.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Select your account opening method.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/ea44f35c2628304b9a2a902a5fb6523e"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">If you choose Myinfo, you need to log in with your Singpass, and some of your basic information will be automatically filled in. Please note that information imported from Myinfo cannot be edited.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 70.14%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/d33acabb862695547b6ef160f0cb701e"/></figure><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 80.16%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/a5a07d6307575581df88f385bd58df4b"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">If you select <i>Continue Manually</i>, please fill in your personal information and residential address according to your ID document. Then, follow these steps:</p><p>(1) Take a photo of your ID document.</p><figure class="table" style="width: 84.19%"><table><colgroup><col style="width: 31.86%"/><col style="width: 42.53%"/><col style="width: 25.61%"/></colgroup><thead><tr><th colspan="1" rowspan="1">ID document</th><th colspan="1" rowspan="1">Singapore, Malaysia, and Indonesia</th><th colspan="1" rowspan="1">Other Countries</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td colspan="1" rowspan="1">Passport</td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1">✅</td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1">✅</td></tr><tr><td colspan="1" rowspan="1">ID Card</td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1">✅</td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1">❌</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p>(2) Follow the instructions to complete facial recognition.</p><p>(3) Upload proof of residential address.</p><ul><li>Acceptable proof of residential address includes bank statements, government-issued letters, or utility bills.</li><li>Ensure the proof document shows your full name, address, and date.</li><li>The proof document must be issued within the last 90 days.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 3. Provide employment details and financial information.</p><p style="margin-left: 30pt">Note: Please provide your employer's name in English and Chinese.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 4. Review the information you have filled in.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 5.
