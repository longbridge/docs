<p>This article will quickly answer common questions about currency conversion.</p><p><strong>1. What is currency conversion?</strong></p><p>Currency conversion refers to the mutual conversion between multiple currencies which you may wish to perform in your Trading Account where the base currency is not denominated in Singapore Dollar (&#34;non-SGD&#34;).</p><p>Long Bridge Securities Pte. Ltd. offers currency conversion features to facilitate your trading in Singapore, Hong Kong stocks and U.S. stocks which are denominated in non-SGD currency.</p><p><strong>2. How are currency conversions performed?</strong></p><p>In the Longbridge App, go to &#34;Portfolio&#34; &gt; &#34;More&#34; &gt; select &#34;Conversion&#34;.</p><p>Here, you can view the current conversion rates. Select the currency and enter the amount you wish to convert. </p><p><strong>3. Which currencies does the currency conversion support?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0px">To facilitate your trading in the various markets, LBS supports the exchange between the following major currencies:</p><ul><li>SGD</li><li>USD</li><li>HKD</li></ul><p><strong>4. How much does it cost to perform conversion currency?</strong></p><p>There is no service fee for currency conversion.</p><p>Exchange rates used are subject to the usual foreign exchange fluctuations in the market.</p><p><strong>5. How is the currency conversion rate calculated?</strong></p><p>Since currency conversion rates fluctuate in real-time, the conversions you perform are based on rates charged by LBS, which are calculated using rates obtained from banks.</p><p> </p><p><strong>Notice</strong></p><ol style="list-style-type: decimal"><li><strong>Conversion rate:</strong> The conversion rate displayed on the conversion page is for your reference only. The actual conversion rate which LBS uses to process your currency conversion request may differ.</li><li><strong>Reminder:</strong> The currency conversion feature is only offered to facilitate your transaction settlement purposes. Please refrain from using this feature for currency speculation. Requests that are not related to trade-related transactions may be rejected.</li></ol><p> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
