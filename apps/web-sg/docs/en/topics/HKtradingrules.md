<h2>Hong Kong Stock Trading Hours</h2><p>Except for public holidays and the market closure due to bad weather, the Hong Kong stock market generally trades from Monday to Friday. The trading hours are as follows:</p><h4>1. Closing Auction Session</h4><figure class="table" style="width: 100%"><table><colgroup><col style="width: 24.37%"/><col style="width: 18.57%"/><col style="width: 20.38%"/><col style="width: 36.68%"/></colgroup><tbody><tr><td><strong>Periods</strong></td><td><strong>Full Day Trading</strong></td><td><strong>Half Day Trading*</strong></td><td><strong>Trading Rules</strong></td></tr><tr><td>Reference Price Fixing Period</td><td>16:00 - 16:01</td><td>12:00-12:01</td><td>The Reference Price is calculated and set; no orders can be entered.</td></tr><tr><td>Order Input Period</td><td>16:01 - 16:06</td><td>12:01-12:06</td><td>At-auction limit orders (ALO) and at-auction orders (AO) within the ±5 percent price limit can be entered, amended, or canceled.</td></tr><tr><td>No-cancellation Period</td><td>16:06-16:08</td><td>12:06-12:08</td><td>At-auction limit orders (ALO) and at-auction orders (AO) can be entered but cannot be canceled or amended; the prices must be between the lowest ask and highest bid.</td></tr><tr><td>Random Closing Period</td><td>16:08-16:10</td><td>12:08-12:10</td><td>At-auction limit orders (ALO) and at-auction orders (AO) can still be entered but cannot be canceled or amended; the prices must be between the lowest ask and highest bid.</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p>*Eves of Christmas, New Year, and Lunar New Year.</p><p> </p><h2>Hong Kong Stock Trading and Settlement Mechanism</h2><h4>1. Day trading</h4><p>Day trading is a mode of trading where a client buys or sells a certain stock or stock option position on the same trading day. Day trading is often referred to as T+0 trading. Longbridge accounts can engage in unlimited day trading.</p><h4>2. Short-Selling Rules</h4><p>Short-selling of Hong Kong stocks is currently not supported</p><h4>3. Clearing Rules</h4><p>Hong Kong stocks are subject to a T+2 closing system under which the clearing and closing process are<span style="color: rgb(216,57,49)"> </span>not completed until the second business day after the transaction.</p><h4>4. Minimum Board Lot Size and Price Limits for Hong Kong Stocks</h4><p>The minimum board lot size for Hong Kong stocks is 1 lot, while stocks with less than one lot are odd lots, and there is no single-day price limit.</p><h4>5.
