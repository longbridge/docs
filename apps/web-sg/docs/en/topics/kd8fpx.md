<p>This article will introduce the steps on transferring stocks from your CDP account.</p><p> </p><p>1. Please submit your application according to the <a href="https://longbridge.com/sg/en/support/topics/stocktransfer/GuidanceOfShareTransferIn">Share Transfer guideline</a>, select &#34;Central Depository (CDP)&#34; in &#34;Delivery Broker&#34;, and enter your CDP account number starting with 1681XXXXXXXX into &#34;Account Number&#34;.</p><p>2. An auto-generated email with the CDP form attached will be sent to your registered email. (Please note that the form in the email is the old version. For your reference, the latest version of the form can be downloaded here: <a href="https://api2.sgx.com/sites/default/files/2026-01/Request%20for%20Transfer%20of%20Securities%2012012026.pdf">Request for Transfer of Securities</a>.)</p><p>3. Please reply the email with following files:</p><ul><li>A copy of NRIC (Front &amp; Back)</li><li>CDP Form “<a href="https://api2.sgx.com/sites/default/files/2026-01/Request%20for%20Transfer%20of%20Securities%2012012026.pdf">Request for Transfer of Securities</a>” — ensure Sections 1, 2, 3, and 4 are completed, and Section 6 is signed.</li></ul><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/d0aaf19aa33dd59f73761cc1d3428e03"/></figure><p> </p><p>We will process your application after receiving your email, and the stocks will be allocated into your Longbridge account in 3 to 15 business days.</p>
