<p style="margin-left: 0px"><strong>The account cancellation process is as follows:</strong><br/><strong>Before proceeding with the cancellation, please take note of the following:</strong></p><ol><li><strong>Asset Settlement:</strong> Ensure that there are no pending assets to be settled, no outstanding liabilities, and that all third-party bindings have been removed.</li><li><strong>Data Deletion:</strong> Upon cancellation, your personal information, account details, and transaction records will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.</li><li><strong>Rights Forfeiture:</strong> Cancellation is considered a waiver of any future entitlements that may arise (e.g., IPO refunds, etc.).</li><li><strong>Registration Restriction:</strong> Within 90 days after account cancellation, the current phone number cannot be used to register a new account.</li></ol><p><strong>The account cancellation process:</strong></p><ul><li><strong>Account Opening Completed:</strong> Please send an email from the contact email address you registered with Longbridge to <a href="mailto:contact@longbridge.sg">contact@longbridge.sg</a>, and provide the following information: Your Longbridge Singapore securities account number; indicate whether you wish to “close only the securities account” or “close the securities account and delete login information”; and state the reason for closing the account. We will process your request after verifying your identity, and the process will be completed within 7 business days.</li><li><strong>Account Opening Not Completed:</strong> Open Longbridge &gt; tap the profile icon in the top-left corner &gt; select <i>Help and Customer Service</i>, then contact customer service for assistance.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0px"><i>This article is for reference only.</i></p>
