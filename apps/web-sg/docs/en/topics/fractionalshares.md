<p>A fractional share order of US stocks refers to an order in the US securities market with a trade quantity that includes a decimal. Fractional shares trading typically offers lower costs because you can purchase only the desired amount of stock instead of using extra funds to buy a whole share, thereby lowering the investment threshold and enabling flexible portfolio allocation.</p><h3>1. Stocks eligible for fractional shares trading</h3><p>You can find the stocks that support fractional shares trading under Market &gt; Classify &gt; Fractional Shares. Additionally, the details page for individual stocks supporting fractional shares trading will display a fractional share icon.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_3622_mi87xj8zzkf4l2hpxt8_.jpeg"/></figure><h3>2. Process of fractional shares trading</h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Select a US stock that supports fractional shares trading.</p><ul><li>Enter a decimal number in the <i>Qty</i> field (up to 4 decimal places).</li><li>Enter the desired amount in the <i>Amount</i> field. The system will automatically calculate and convert it into the corresponding number of shares based on the current stock price.</li></ul><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_3622_mi87xrthmjxw3nwnt3_.jpeg"/></figure><h3>3. Corporate actions for fractional shares</h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Mandatory corporate actions, such as dividends and stock splits, are supported. Other voluntary corporate actions, such as rights issues and offers, are not allowed.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The operation process remains unchanged. For corporate actions involving stock splits or reverse stock splits, you may receive fractional shares. For example, if a stock undergoes a 1-for-10 reverse split and you hold 5 shares before the reverse split, you will receive 0.5 shares after the reverse split.</p><h3>4. Fees for fractional shares trading in US stocks</h3><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/5689c075ca9fa4aba82c3643aff908a9"/></figure><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm">FAQs</h3><p><strong>Q1: What are the trading hours supported for U.S. fractional share orders?</strong></p><p>Fractional shares are only tradable during regular market hours, from 9:30 AM to 4:00 PM (Eastern Time). If there is an early closing, trading hours will be from 9:30 AM to 1:00 PM (Eastern Time).</p><p><strong>Q2: What are the order types supported for U.S. fractional share trading?</strong></p><p>Fractional share orders only support limit orders and market orders. Conditional orders or grid orders are not currently supported.</p><p><strong>Q3: Does fractional share trading in U.S. stocks support margin or short selling?</strong></p><p>Fractional shares cannot be shorted. Fractional shares support unified buying power, and the margin interest rate is the same as for whole shares.</p><p><strong>Q4: Does fractional share trading in U.S. stocks support order modifications?</strong></p><p>Fractional share orders cannot be modified. If you need to adjust an order, you must first cancel it and then place a new order.</p><p><strong>Q5: Is there a minimum order requirement for fractional shares?</strong></p><p>The minimum order size for fractional trading is 0.0001 shares, and a minimum buy order amount of USD 1.</p><p> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
