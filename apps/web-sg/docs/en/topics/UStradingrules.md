US Stock Trading and Settlement Mechanism</h2><p><strong>Day Trading</strong><br/>Day trading means buying and selling a batch of securities within a day. Day trading is often referred to as T+0 trading. Longbridge accounts can engage in unlimited day trading.</p><p><strong>Clearing Rules</strong><br/>US stocks are subject to a T+1 closing system under which the clearing and closing process is not completed until the next business day after the transaction.</p><h2>3. Minimum Board Lot Size and Price Limits for US Stocks</h2><p>The minimum board lot size for US stocks is 1 share.</p><h2>4. Minimum Stock Tick Size</h2><p>Minimum stock tick size: USD 0.0001 for stocks trading under USD 1 and USD 0.01 for stocks trading above USD 1.</p><h2>5.
