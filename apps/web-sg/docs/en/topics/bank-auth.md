<p>This article details the complete process for adding and verifying a bank account, including applicable scenarios, step-by-step instructions, required documents, and FAQs, to help you successfully complete bank account binding and ensure smooth fund withdrawals.</p><h2>1. Under what circumstances do I need to verify my bank account?</h2><p>Only verified bank accounts can be used for withdrawals.</p><ul><li>If the bank account is added through DDA linkage, it will be automatically verified.</li><li>If the bank account is added manually, verification is required by submitting valid proof of bank account.</li></ul><p> </p><h2>2. How do I register a bank account?</h2><p>Step1. Login to LB apps &gt; Portfolio &gt; More &gt; Bank Accounts.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/0fd3483b0da61a512aa9a65f98a9065c"/></figure><p>Step2. Select「+」and enter your bank account information. </p><h5><span style="color: rgb(216,57,49)">Note:</span></h5><ul><li><span style="color: rgb(216,57,49)">Registering bank accounts belonging to another person, third-party payment platforms, or joint bank accounts is not permitted.</span></li><li><span style="color: rgb(216,57,49)">Bank account number is not the same as debit/credit card number.</span></li><li><span style="color: rgb(216,57,49)">A valid proof of account must clearly display the beneficiary name, bank account number and bank name.</span></li></ul><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/a72f059ef498fd249f4ae3464f231ba5"/></figure><p>Once submitted, the status will be automatically updated to &#34;Under Review&#34; and is typically processed within 1-2 business days.</p><p>Once the review is successful, the bank account status will be updated to &#34;Verified&#34;, and you will receive a notification via push notification and email.</p><p>If the review fails, the status will be reverted to &#34;Verify Now&#34; and you will need to re-upload the correct supported documents to proceed with the verification. A detailed rejection notice will also be sent to your registered email address.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 80.16%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/0051d59d9815b931302abe9fa5a7269e"/></figure><h2>3. failed?</strong></p><p>Common reasons for rejection include:</p><ul><li>The provided proof of account document does not include the client's name, bank account number, or bank name/logo.</li><li>The account information (e.g., client name, bank name, or account number) entered by the client does not match the information in the provided proof of account. Please note that the bank account must be in the client's name; accounts held by others, joint accounts, or third-party payment platforms are not accepted.</li><li>The provided proof of account document is blurry and cannot be clearly recognized.</li><li>Credit/debit card numbers are provided instead of a bank account number. Please note that credit/debit card numbers are not the same as bank account numbers and are not interchangeable.</li></ul><p><strong>Q2:How long do I need to wait for bank account verification?</strong></p><p>The verification is typically completed within 1-2 business days. Once verified successfully, the bank account can be used for fund withdrawals.</p><p> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Key takeaways:</strong></p><ul><li>Verification requirements: Only verified bank accounts can be used for withdrawals. Accounts added via DDA authorization will be automatically verified, while accounts added manually require submitting bank proof for verification.</li><li>Operation path: Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>More</i> &gt; <i>Bank Accounts</i> &gt; Tap [+] to fill in the information.</li><li>Review time: It usually takes 1–2 working days, and the result will be notified via push notification and email.</li><li>A valid bank proof document must clearly show your name, bank account number, and bank name.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0.0px"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0.0px"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0.0px"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
