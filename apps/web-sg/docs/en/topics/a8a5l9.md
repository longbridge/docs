<p style="margin-left: 0px">US Order Book provides 60 levels Bid and Offer, covering pre-market, regular, post-market, and overnight trading sessions.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/e69dd5349f5693700a3b52c10682971d"/></figure><h2 style="margin-left: 0px">1. Feature Overview</h2><h3 style="margin-left: 0px">1.1 Depth Chart</h3><p style="margin-left: 0px">The Depth Chart displays the number of buy and sell orders at various price levels. It is based on the top 60 levels of buy and sell orders.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/5c9a13eddc26d2559c268653f04e9b65"/></figure><ul><li>Horizontal Axis: It displays order prices. From left to right, the four prices are: the low price of the 60 bid levels, the high price of the 60 bid levels (best bid price), the low price of the 60 ask levels (best ask price), and the high price of the 60 ask levels.</li><li>Vertical Axis: It displays the order quantity required to be filled when reaching this price level. The bottom value is the smaller of the best bid and best ask quantity, while the top value is the larger of the total buy quantity and total sell quantity of the 60 levels.</li><li>Crosshair: It displays the selected price level and the order quantity required to be filled to reach that price.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0px">With the Depth Chart, you can easily see the quantity of buy and sell orders at different price levels and understand the current market's buying and selling demand:</p><ul><li>Width: It reflects the distribution of order prices.</li><li>Height: It displays the trend of total buy and sell order quantity.</li><li>Steepness: It shows the price levels where orders are concentrated and their respective quantities.</li></ul><h3 style="margin-left: 0px">1.2 Market Depth</h3><p style="margin-left: 0px">Market Depth provides detailed bid and ask quotes, covering up to 60 levels. Users can select from different depths, including 1, 5, 10, 20, 40, or 60 levels.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/b788c9a6ed012eafc0f11e52408fdea6"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0px"><strong>Bid/Ask Ratio:</strong></p><ul><li>Bid Ratio: This refers to the ratio of the &#34;total buy quantity&#34; to the &#34;total order quantity&#34; for a security on Nasdaq. The &#34;total order quantity&#34; is the sum of the &#34;total buy quantity&#34; and the &#34;total sell quantity&#34;.</li><li>Ask Ratio: This refers to the ratio of the &#34;total sell quantity&#34; to the &#34;total order quantity&#34; for a security on Nasdaq.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0px">Note: The Bid/Ask Ratio<strong> </strong>provides an insight into total order quantity distribution of a security on <span style="background-color: rgb(255,255,255); color: rgb(0,0,0)">exchange</span>, not limited to the top 60 levels.</p><p style="margin-left: 0px"><strong>BBO Line:</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0px">It identifies the orders within the best bid and offer (BBO) in the order book.</p><ul><li>Bid Side: Orders with prices higher than or equal to the best bid price are displayed above the bid BBO line.</li><li>Ask Side: Orders with prices lower than or equal to the best ask price are displayed above the ask BBO line.</li></ul><figure class="table" style="width: 896px"><table><colgroup><col style="width: 50%"/><col style="width: 50%"/></colgroup><tbody><tr><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><strong>BBO Type</strong></td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1"><strong>Explanation</strong></td></tr><tr><td colspan="1" rowspan="1">NSDQ BBO</td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1">Nasdaq TotalView and NSDQ BBO both represent data from Nasdaq Stock Exchange, so the best bid and ask prices in Nasdaq TotalView are the same as those in NSDQ BBO.</td></tr><tr><td colspan="1" rowspan="1">National BBO</td><td colspan="1" rowspan="1">National BBO aggregates the best quotes from 17 exchanges across the United States, representing the highest bid and the lowest offer from all quotes, excluding odd-lot orders. Therefore, when it is NBBO, the BBO line may span multiple quote levels.</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p style="margin-left: 0px"><strong>Order Size Bar:</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0px">The colored order size bar visually shows the distribution of bid/ask order quantities.</p><p style="margin-left: 0px">The area of bar is drawn on the basis of current maximum order quantity, and the bar on each level is displayed proportionally according to its order quantity compared to the maximum.</p><p style="margin-left: 0px">Note: On the Quote page, it takes the selected maximum order quantity as the benchmark, while under full screen mode, it takes the maximum order quantity of 60 levels as the benchmark.</p><h3 style="margin-left: 0px">1.3 Order Depth</h3><p style="margin-left: 0px">Order Depth provides detailed information for each bid/ask order across all 60 levels.</p><ul><li>MPID (Market Participants ID) Indicates the ID of a market participant. If a MPID is not provided by participants, it is shown as NSDQ, indicating that the order is from the Nasdaq exchange. BLUE indicates orders from Blue Ocean Exchange during overnight trading.</li><li>The bid/ask ratios and BBO line rules adhere to the standards set by Market Depth.</li></ul><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/fe8b2997130541470c9c7899e1ec60bc"/></figure><h2 style="margin-left: 0px">2.
