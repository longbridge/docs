<p style="margin-left: 0cm">Here you'll find quick answers to common questions about fractional share trading.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>1. What is fractional share trading?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The standard trading unit is one board lot of 100 shares. However, some products, such as Special Purpose Acquisition Company (SPAC) Warrants, exchange-traded funds (ETFs), American Depositary Receipts (ADRs), and fixed income instruments, may have different board lot sizes.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Board lot size for different products</strong></p><figure class="table"><table><tbody><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Product</strong></p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Board lot size</strong></p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">SPAC Warrant</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">1</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">ETF</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">1</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">ADR</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">10</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Fixed income instrument</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">10, 100, or 1,000</p></td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">* Since 17 January 2022, all ETFs listed on the Singapore Exchange (SGX) trade with a board lot size of 1 unit.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">SGX also has a market for fractional share trading, which allows for trading for quantities smaller than a standard board lot of the underlying security.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>2. Does Longbridge provide fractional share trading services?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Yes, Longbridge provides fractional share trading services. You can go to the Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>Trade</i> &gt; <i>Choose Symbol</i>, select a stock and the trading direction (buy/sell), tap <i>Odd Lot Order</i> under <i>Type</i>, enter the price and quantity, and submit the order.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>3. Why might the buy price for a fractional share be higher than for 1 lot?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Due to lower liquidity and trading volume in the fractional share market, the buy price for fractional shares might be higher than for a full lot. When buying fractional shares, you can enter a price slightly above the market price to increase the chance of filling the order.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>4. Why might the sell price for a fractional share be lower than for 1 lot?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Due to lower liquidity and trading volume in the fractional share market, the sell price for fractional shares might be lower than for a full lot. When selling fractional shares, you can enter a price slightly below the market price to increase the chance of filling the order.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>5. What are the fees for fractional share trading?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The fee for fractional share trading is the same as for standard lot trading. For details, please see the Longbridge pricing table.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>6. Can I buy/sell fractional shares and full lots in a single order?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">No, fractional shares and full lots require separate orders. The number of shares for a fractional share order must be less than one board lot.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>7. When can I trade fractional shares?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Fractional shares can only be traded during the continuous trading session of the Singapore stock market (9:00–12:00 and 13:00–17:00 on trading days). There is no pre-market session or closing auction session for fractional shares.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>8. What types of orders are supported for fractional shares? Can I modify my order after submitting it?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Fractional share trading only supports limit orders. Once placed, these orders cannot be modified but can be canceled.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>9. Why hasn't my fractional share order been filled after submission?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Due to lower liquidity in the fractional share market, your order might not be filled immediately if there are no matching buy/sell orders from other participants.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>10. Does Longbridge support the transfer of fractional shares?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Yes, Longbridge supports both transfer-in and transfer-out of fractional shares. For details, please see <a href="https://support.longbridge.sg/topics/Sharetransfer/GuidanceOfShareTransferIn?locale=zh-CN">the guidelines for share transfer-in</a> and <a href="https://support.longbridge.sg/topics/Sharetransfer/sharetransferout?locale=zh-CN">the guidelines for share transfer-out</a>.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
