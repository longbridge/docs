<p style="margin-left: 0cm">Below is a detailed introduction and example regarding margin calls.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>A margin call occurs when your account equity falls below the maintenance margin (MM) requirement. If this happens, you must address the margin call within 3 trading days upon receiving the margin call notice (including the date of receipt).</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The amount specified in the issued margin call notice will be slightly higher than the difference between the MM and the equity value, to provide a buffer for price fluctuations.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Example of a margin call:</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">A client deposits SGD 10,000 in cash and SGD 5,000 worth of Stock A (initial margin (IM): 30%, MM: 25%), and purchases SGD 25,000 worth of Stock B (IM: 50%, MM: 45%). Assume that the market value of Stock B falls to SGD 19,500.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">After the client buys the stocks and the value of Stock B decreases, the client's holdings are as follows:</p><figure class="table"><table><tbody><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Cash arrears</strong></p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">SGD 15,000 (SGD 10,000 - SGD 25,000)</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Total market value of the portfolio</strong></p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">SGD 24,500 (SGD 5,000 + SGD 19,500)</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Equity</strong></p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">SGD 9,500</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Holdings IM</strong></p></td><td>SGD 11,250 (SGD 5,000 × 30% + SGD 19,500 × 50%)</td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Holdings MM</strong></p></td><td>SGD 10,025 (SGD 5,000 × 25% + SGD 19,500 × 45%)</td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Margin call</strong></p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">SGD 1,015 (The difference between the MM and the equity balance is SGD 525).</p></td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The client needs to ensure that the equity in their account meets the margin requirement on or before the specified date.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">If the client chooses to sell stocks, the minimum market value that needs to be sold is equal to the margin call amount divided by the <strong>IM ratio</strong>.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>If the equity falls below the forced liquidation margin, the client must immediately cover the margin shortfall; otherwise, their positions may be forcibly liquidated.</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>Note: If you need any explanation or help with the margin trading mechanism, please contact customer service.</i></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Key takeaways:</strong></p><ul><li>Triggering condition: The value of equity falls below the MM level.</li><li>Margin call amount: The margin call amount is generally higher than the difference between the MM and equity.</li><li>Processing deadline: You must make up the shortfall or reduce your positions within 3 trading days upon receiving the notice.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
