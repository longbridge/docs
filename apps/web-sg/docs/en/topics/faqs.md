<p>Here you'll find quick answers to common questions about about funds.</p><p><strong>1. What is the deadline for subscribing to funds?</strong></p><p>Orders submitted before 12:00 noon on each trading day will be processed on the same day. Orders submitted after 12:00 noon or on non-trading days (e.g., fund holidays and  Singapore public holidays) will be processed on the next trading day.</p><p><strong>2. Is financing supported for fund subscriptions?</strong></p><p>No. Fund subscriptions must be paid using cash and are not eligible for financing. However, you can use the funds in your Cash Plus account to subscribe to Unit Trusts.</p><p><strong>3. Is there a specific order for fund redemption?</strong></p><p>There is no specific order for fund redemption. You can go to the corresponding fund page &gt; Salable Fund Units to view redeemable funds. After successful redemption, the funds will be returned via the original payment method.</p><p><strong>4. When will the funds arrive after redemption?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">If the fund redemption request is submitted before 12:00 noon on T day, the fund company will calculate the asset value based on the net worth on T day. The funds will arrive within 7 trading days.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</p>
