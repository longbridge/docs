It activates the day after your Cash Plus subscription is confirmed.</p><p>The coupon becomes active when its status updates to &#34;Active,&#34; which occurs the day after the interest boost takes effect. Can multiple Interest Boost coupons take effect at the same time? </h4><p>Yes, they can. When there are multiple interest boost coupons in the account, each coupon will take effect simultaneously according to its own rules.</p><p><strong>Example:</strong> Client holds SGD 300 in funds that is eligible for interest boost and has two interest boost coupons in their account that take effect on the same day:</p><ul><li>One coupon offers a 3% interest boost for 30 days on a maximum principal of SGD 100.</li><li>The other coupon offers a 5% interest boost for 60 days on a maximum principal of SGD 200.</li></ul><p>The maximum additional interest earnings over 60 days can be calculated as follows:</p><ul><li><strong>Day 1 to Day 30:</strong> SGD 100 * (3% / 365) * 30 + SGD 200 * (5% / 365) * 30</li><li><strong>Day 31 to Day 60:</strong> SGD 200 * (5% / 365) * 30</li></ul><p> </p><p>Long Bridge Securities Pte.
