<p>This article will quickly answer frequently asked questions about Cash Plus.</p><h4>1. Is Cash Plus suitable for me?</h4><h5>Trade stocks as you please</h5><p>Stock trading is unrestricted and there is no need to worry about missing out on market opportunities.</p><h5>Disposable income added value</h5><p>This offers clients cash value-added services which not only meet their needs to buy stocks at any time, but also generate returns from their idle funds. (Professional investment management capabilities are used to select high-quality and robust investment products.)</p><h5>Get Set Once and For All</h5><p>Sign a one-time agreement for automatic subscriptions on daily basis.</p><h5>Low Risk</h5><p>The excess funds facility mainly invests in highly secure high liquidity funds with low risk and low volatility, meeting the liquidity needs of investors on the basis of generating returns.</p><p> </p><h4>2. Can Cash Plus be used for currency conversion and withdrawal?</h4><p>No. Generally, funds in Cash Plus must be manually redeemed before currency conversion or withdrawal requests can be processed.</p><p>For withdrawals, our system will automatically initiate the redemption of Cash Plus funds on behalf of clients. Once the redemption is successful, the withdrawal will be processed automatically. This process typically takes 1–2 business days.</p><p>For currency conversion, funds in Cash Plus must be manually redeemed before a conversion request can be submitted. However, for clients with the auto-conversion facility, the system will automatically redeem sufficient funds from Cash Plus whenever there is a debit balance in another currency. Once the redemption is completed, the proceeds will be automatically converted to offset the debit balance</p><p> </p><h4>3. Can Cash Plus be redeemed manually?</h4><p>Yes, redemption requests can be submitted at any time on the Cash Plus page. Once it is successfully redeemed, the cash can directly be used for stock trading, currency conversion as well as withdrawal. The redemption process may take approximately 1 business day. Do note that idle cash from Cash Plus redemption will only be placed back 3 days after successful redemption.</p><p>In the case of trading, Cash Plus will automatically subscribe or redeem according to the balance in your securities account:</p><h5>Auto-Subscription:</h5><p>Once a sales trade has been settled, idle cash will be automatically invested into Cash Plus underlying funds at a fixed time daily with minimum subscription amount of 1 and subscription fee of 0.</p><h5>Auto-Redemption:</h5><p>Once a buy trade has been successfully done (T+1), funds will be automatically redeemed into cash for settlement at a fixed time daily with minimum holdings of 1 and redemption fee of 0.</p><p><strong>Disclaimer</strong>: <i>Although the Cash Plus facility is aimed at maximizing the return on your idle funds, it is important to note that clients are responsible for ensuring there are sufficient funds in their accounts for settlement purposes in the event that the funds from the Cash Plus redemption are not credited in time for trade settlement.</i></p><p> </p><h4>4. Does Cash Plus offer a fixed return？And is it safe?</h4><p>No, the return is not principal guaranteed. However, the investment mandate is strictly to preserve the fund’s principal value and maintain a high degree of liquidity while producing returns comparable to savings deposits. The high liquidity funds invested are <span style="background-color: rgb(250,250,250); color: rgba(0,0,0,0.85)">iFAST SGD Enhanced Liquidity A SGD</span> for SGD, and <span style="background-color: rgb(250,250,250); color: rgba(0,0,0,0.85)">iFAST USD Enhanced Liquidity USD A</span> for USD. The funds invest in high quality, short-term instruments such as high quality government and corporate bonds, commercial bills and fixed deposits that mature in 397 days or less.</p><p> </p><h4>5. How to calculate the return？</h4><p>Idle cash that is received before 08:00 AM on SG trading day will be automatically placed into the Cash Plus facility on the same day (T). Otherwise, it will only be placed into Cash Plus facility on the next trading day (T+1).</p><p><strong>Examples:</strong></p><p>Scenario 1: The cash was received at 7:30 AM on Monday, then the fund will be automatically placed into Cash Plus on the same day while the subscription unit will be confirmed on Monday. Cash Plus return will be computed from Tuesday onwards.</p><p>Scenario 2: The cash was received at 3:30 PM on Monday, then the fund will be automatically placed into Cash Plus on Tuesday while the subscription unit will be confirmed on Tuesday. Cash Plus return will be computed from Wednesday onwards.</p><p> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
