<p>This article will introduce the steps to withdrawal to your Wise account.</p><h4>Step 1: Retrieve a Valid Wise Proof of Account</h4><p>1. <strong>Log in</strong> to your Wise account.</p><p>2. Open the <strong>currency account </strong>(Note: If the currency account is not available, please add it by selecting the<span style="color: #555555"> </span>(+) button before continuing further). </p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 80.36%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/f5c6ca3f8405476655022986177a5284"/></figure><p>3. Tap the <strong>three-dot menu</strong> (⋯) in the top-right corner.</p><p>4. Select <strong>&#34;Get proof of account details&#34;</strong> (Note: If the selection is unavailable, it indicates that your account has not been verified. Please complete the account verification process or contact a WISE representative for further assistance)</p><p><span style="color: #888888"><i>Sample of a Valid Proof of Account:</i></span></p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 82.57%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/8e851d5c01be46cc2d80bd22f106338a"/></figure><h4>Step 2: Add WISE Account as Bank Account</h4><p>1. <strong>Login </strong>to Longbridge app and navigate to<strong> Portfolio </strong>&gt;<strong> More </strong>&gt; <strong>Bank Accounts</strong>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/56d343cae337c22d89dab83180a7317c"/></figure><p>2. Click the <strong>(+)</strong> button, fill in your personal account information as per the WISE proof of account, and upload the proof of account. (Note: If the bank name is not listed in the dropdown menu, select &#34;Other Banks&#34; and enter the bank name exactly as per the WISE proof of account.)</p><p> </p><p><i>Note:</i></p><p><i>Please be reminded to refer to the WISE proof of account, </i></p><ul><li><i>For <strong>SWIFT Code = TRWISGSGXXX</strong>  Can be found under: </i><br/><i>Bank Account Region </i>→<i> <strong>Banks in Singapore</strong> </i>→<i> <strong>Wise Asia-Pacific Pte.Ltd.</strong></i></li><li><i>For SWIFT Code = <strong>TRWIUS35XXX</strong> ---&gt; Can be found under:</i><br/><i>Bank Account Region </i>→<i> <strong>Banks in US</strong> </i>→<i> <strong>Wise Payments Ltd.</strong></i></li><li><i>For other SWIFT Code, please select &#34;Other Banks&#34; and enter the bank name exactly as per the WISE proof of account.)</i></li></ul><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/150b70dcd17aeb13dd015660eda27ffc"/></figure><p>3. Once submitted, the status will update to <strong>&#34;Under Review&#34;</strong> and is typically processed within one business day.</p><p>4. If the review is successful, the bank account status will be updated to <strong>&#34;Verified&#34;</strong>, and you will receive a notification via push notification and email.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 80.36%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/b0fde4b9bbb800b07a3a05f3dea5c123"/></figure><h4>Step 3: Submit Withdrawal Request</h4><p>Once your WISE account is verified, you may initiate withdrawal by navigating to the Longbridge app &gt; <strong>Portfolio </strong>&gt;<strong> More </strong>&gt;<strong> Withdrawal</strong> and select your WISE account as the withdrawal destination.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/dad1058fab0df3fa612672aa767c3ec0"/></figure><p><strong>Notes:</strong></p><ul><li>Please transfer funds to a bank account under the same name as your LBS Account. Funds cannot be withdrawn to other persons' bank accounts, third-party payment platforms or a joint bank account.</li><li>LBS supports SGD, USD and HKD for withdrawals. Please ensure that your bank supports the withdrawal currency.</li><li>If you do not have a verified bank account with LBS, please add one first before initiating a withdrawal.*</li><li>Only settled amounts can be withdrawn, and your net total cash balance must be greater than 0.</li><li>Remittance fees are subject to change without prior notice.</li><li>You will be responsible for any charges imposed by the remitting banks, receiving banks and intermediary banks.</li><li>An email and push notification will be sent to you once the withdrawal is complete.</li></ul><p> </p><p style="margin-left: 0.0px"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0.0px"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
