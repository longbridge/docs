<p>All deposits must originate from a bank account under your personal name. You won’t be able to deposit funds into your Longbridge Singapore account if they come from:</p><ul><li>Accounts that belong to a third party</li><li>Third-party payment platforms</li><li>Cheques or cash</li></ul><p>If your deposit falls under any of these categories, it will be unsuccessful.</p><p> </p><h3>1.  How do I get a refund for an unsuccessful deposit?</h3><p>If your deposit was unsuccessful, you can request a refund through these methods:</p><ul><li>Go to the <strong>Longbridge App &gt;</strong> <i><strong>Me &gt; Help and Customer Service &gt; Online Service</strong></i></li><li>Call us at <strong>+65 6330 3030</strong></li><li>WhatsApp us at <strong>+65 6330 3033</strong></li><li>Email us at <a href="mailto:contact@longbridge.sg"><span style="color: #304ffe">contact@longbridge.sg</span></a></li></ul><p> </p><h3>2. When will I receive my refund?</h3><p>You’ll receive your refund through your original transfer method.</p><ul><li>For bank accounts: Refunds typically take 2-4 weeks to process.</li><li>For third-party payment platforms: Refunds may take 6-8 weeks to process.
