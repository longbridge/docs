<h3 style="margin-left: 0px">Intraday P/L</h3><p style="margin-left: 0px">Intraday P/L refers to the cumulative P/L from the previous trading day’s closing price to the current trading day’s session.</p><p style="margin-left: 0px"><strong>Calculation formula: </strong>Intraday P/L = (current price * qty) – (previous closing price * <span style="background-color: rgb(255,255,255); color: rgb(0,0,0)">previous closing qty</span>) + current selling volume - current buying volume</p><p style="margin-left: 0px"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0px"><strong>Case 1:</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0px">Xiaoming holds 100 shares of Alibaba (BABA), and the holdings are as follows.</p><figure class="table"><table><tbody><tr><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Current price</strong></p></td><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Qty</strong></p></td><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Previous</strong></p><p style="text-align: center"><strong>closing price</strong></p></td><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Previous</strong></p><p style="text-align: center"><strong>closing qty</strong></p></td><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Current selling volume</strong></p></td><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Current buying volume</strong></p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="text-align: center">200 HKD</p></td><td><p style="text-align: center">100 shares</p></td><td><p style="text-align: center">190 HKD</p></td><td><p style="text-align: center">100 shares</p></td><td><p style="text-align: center">0</p></td><td><p style="text-align: center">0</p></td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p style="margin-left: 0px">According to the formula, the intraday P/L is (200 *100) – (190*100) + 0 -0 = 1000 HKD</p><p style="margin-left: 0px">Intraday P/L: +1000HKD</p><p style="margin-left: 0px"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0px"><strong>Case 2:</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0px">Xiao Ming holds 100 shares of Alibaba (BABA) at the opening. He first sells 50 shares at the price of 202/share and then buys 50 shares at the price of 198/share. The holdings are as follows.</p><figure class="table" style="width: 100%"><table><colgroup><col style="width: 16.78%"/><col style="width: 13.51%"/><col style="width: 16.51%"/><col style="width: 17.22%"/><col style="width: 18.13%"/><col style="width: 17.85%"/></colgroup><tbody><tr><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Current price</strong></p></td><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Qty</strong></p></td><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Previous</strong></p><p style="text-align: center"><strong>closing price</strong></p></td><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Previous</strong></p><p style="text-align: center"><strong>closing qty</strong></p></td><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Current selling volume</strong></p></td><td><p style="text-align: center"><strong>Current buying volume</strong></p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="text-align: center">200 HKD</p></td><td><p style="text-align: center">100 shares</p></td><td><p style="text-align: center">190 HKD</p></td><td><p style="text-align: center">100 shares</p></td><td><p style="text-align: center">10100 HKD</p></td><td><p style="text-align: center">9900 HKD</p></td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p style="margin-left: 0px">According to the formula, the intraday P/L is (200 * 100) – (190 * 100) + 202 * 50 – 198 * 50 = 1200 HKD</p><p style="margin-left: 0px">Intraday P/L: +1200 HKD</p><p style="margin-left: 0px"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0px"><strong>Note:</strong></p><ol><li>The intraday P/L only calculates the actual holdings, excluding transaction-incurred fees such as handling fees.</li><li>If you liquidate and open a position within the same day, the intraday P/L will continue to be calculated using the data at the time of liquidation.</li></ol><p> </p><p style="margin-left: 0.0px"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0.0px"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
