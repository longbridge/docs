<h2>What Is IPO Auction?</h2><p>Before a U.S.-listed stock officially opens for trading on its first day, the exchange conducts a centralized auction to determine the opening price — <br/> this is called the <strong>IPO auction</strong>. Unlike a standard opening auction, the IPO auction is specifically designed to establish a new stock's very first    <br/> opening price. The exchange publishes real-time auction updates to help market participants gauge the supply and demand dynamics of the new listing.   <br/>  <br/> IPO auction data is displayed only during the auction phase on a stock's first day of listing, and is automatically hidden once the auction concludes. <br/> Data updates in real time; the reference price and matched shares may change continuously. The final opening price is determined by the result at the<br/> moment the auction ends.</p><h2>Where to View?</h2><p>Go to the individual stock quote page. An <strong>IPO auction</strong> data bar will appear above the price chart, displaying live auction progress.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/bcb633df961eb4ed5c342d67150f3a70"/></figure><figure class="table" style="width: 100%"><table><colgroup><col style="width: 24.32%"/><col style="width: 75.68%"/></colgroup><tbody><tr><td><strong>Field</strong></td><td><strong>Description</strong></td></tr><tr><td>IPO auction price</td><td>The indicative reference price during the current auction phase, representing the expected opening price.</td></tr><tr><td>Matched shares</td><td><p>The number of shares that can be matched between buyers and sellers in the current auction. A higher value indicates a greater degree</p><p>  of order matching at the current price level.</p></td></tr><tr><td>Unmatched shares</td><td>The number of remaining orders after matching, reflecting the supply-demand imbalance in the current auction book.</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><h2>How to Interpret the Data? </h2><p>Using FRBT shown above as an example:</p><ul><li><strong>IPO auction price 19.000 </strong>— higher than the IPO offer price of 18.000 (+5.56%), indicating strong buying interest and an expected opening price above the offer price.</li><li><strong>Matched shares 262.70K</strong> — a significant volume of buy and sell orders can be matched at the current price level.</li><li><strong>Unmatched shares 76.83K</strong> — a portion of buy orders have yet to find a matching seller, indicating that buyer demand slightly exceeds supply.</li></ul><h2>Permission Requirement</h2><p>This feature requires users to hold <strong>LV2 US Stocks Level 2 Quote</strong> permission to access.</p>
