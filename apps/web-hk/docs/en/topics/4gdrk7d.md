<h2>Hong Kong Stock Trading Hours</h2><p>Except for public holidays and the market closure due to bad weather, the Hong Kong stock market generally trades from Monday to Friday. The trading hours are as follows:</p><h4>1. Pre-opening Session</h4><figure class="table" style="width: 100%"><table><colgroup><col style="width: 26.31%"/><col style="width: 26.22%"/><col style="width: 47.47%"/></colgroup><thead><tr><th>Periods</th><th>Full Day Trading &amp; Half Day Trading*</th><th>Trading Rules</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Order Input Period</td><td>9:00am - 9:15am</td><td>Orders can be entered, amended, or canceled.</td></tr><tr><td>No-cancellation Period</td><td>9:15am - 9:20am</td><td>Only at-auction limit orders (ALO) and at-auction orders (AO) can be entered, and no orders can be amended or canceled.</td></tr><tr><td>Random Matching Period</td><td>9:20am - up to 9:22am</td><td>Matching orders during the auction period. (orders can be entered, but not guaranteed to be <strong>matched</strong> and cannot be canceled or amended)</td></tr><tr><td>Blocking Period</td><td>After matching - 9:30am</td><td>Oders can no longer be entered, amended, or canceled.</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p>*Eves of Christmas, New Year, and Lunar New Year.</p><h4>2. Continuous Trading Session</h4><figure class="table"><table><colgroup><col style="width: 19.73%"/><col style="width: 18.95%"/><col style="width: 20.18%"/><col style="width: 41.14%"/></colgroup><thead><tr><th>Periods</th><th>Full Day Trading</th><th>Half Day Trading*</th><th>Trading Rules</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Morning Session</td><td>9:30 - 12:00</td><td>9:30 - 12:00</td><td>Orders can be entered, amended, or canceled. Enhanced limit orders (ELO) are supported.</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p>*Eves of Christmas, New Year, and Lunar New Year.</p><h4>3. Closing Auction Session</h4><figure class="table"><table><colgroup><col style="width: 24.7%"/><col style="width: 20.02%"/><col style="width: 18.22%"/><col style="width: 37.06%"/></colgroup><thead><tr><th>Periods</th><th>Full Day Trading</th><th>Half Day Trading*</th><th>Trading Rules</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Reference Price Fixing Period</td><td>16:00 - 16:01</td><td>12:00-12:01</td><td>The Reference Price is calculated and set; no orders can be entered.</td></tr><tr><td>Order Input Period</td><td>16:01 - 16:06</td><td>12:01-12:06</td><td>At-auction limit orders (ALO) and at-auction orders (AO) within the ±5 percent price limit can be entered, amended, or canceled.</td></tr><tr><td>No-cancellation Period</td><td>16:06-16:08</td><td>12:06-12:08</td><td>At-auction limit orders (ALO) and at-auction orders (AO) can be entered but cannot be canceled or amended; the prices must be between the lowest ask and highest bid.</td></tr><tr><td>Random Closing Period</td><td>16:08-16:10</td><td>12:08-12:10</td><td>At-auction limit orders (ALO) and at-auction orders (AO) can still be entered but cannot be canceled or amended; the prices must be between the lowest ask and highest bid.</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p>*Eves of Christmas, New Year, and Lunar New Year.</p><h2> </h2><h2><strong>Hong Kong Stock Trading and Settlement Mechanism</strong></h2><h4><strong>1. Day trading</strong></h4><p>Day trading is a mode of trading where a client buys or sells a certain stock or stock option position on the same trading day. Day trading is often referred to as T+0 trading. Longbridge accounts can engage in unlimited day trading.</p><h4>2. Short-Selling Rules</h4><p>Short-selling of Hong Kong stocks is currently not supported</p><h4>3. Clearing Rules</h4><p>Hong Kong stocks are subject to a T+2 closing system under which the clearing and closing process is not completed until the second business day after the transaction.</p><h4><strong>4. Minimum Board Lot Size and Price Limits for Hong Kong Stocks</strong></h4><p>The minimum board lot size for Hong Kong stocks is 1 lot, while stocks with less than one lot are odd lots, and there is no single-day price limit.</p><h4><strong>5.
