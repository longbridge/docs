Short Straddle </h2><ul><li><strong>Overview</strong></li></ul><p>Short straddle is an options strategy where you sell a call and a put with the same strike price and expiration date. If your account has insufficient buying power, a margin call will be issued, which may lead to forced liquidation. If you hold an options strategy, Longbridge may liquidate the entire strategy at once, including all option contracts and the corresponding stock positions. Please plan your funds and positions in advance.</p>
