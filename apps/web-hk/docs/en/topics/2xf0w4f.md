<p style="margin-left: 0cm">The process of transferring stocks from Futu Securities to Longbridge mainly involves two steps: submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge and submitting a transfer-out request to Futu Securities. Below are the detailed descriptions for these two steps.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>1. Submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Go to the Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>Transfer</i> to submit a transfer-in application. If you have already deposited funds, select <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>More</i> &gt; <i>Share Transfer-In</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4mimlm1q07lcuomzd_.jpeg"/></figure><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4mihpwdtqqlgfaydu_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Fill in stock transfer details.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(1) Fill in the information of the transfer-out broker and your account information at that broker, then click <i>Next.</i></p><figure class="table"><table><thead><tr><th><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: center"><strong>Transfer-out broker</strong></p></th><th><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: center"><strong>Futu Securities International (Hong Kong) Limited</strong></p></th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Account number</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">16-digit Futu Securities account number starting with 10. You can check it on the Futu daily statement and select the corresponding securities account according to the stock market.</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Futu daily statement</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>Go to Trading</i> &gt; <i>Securities Account</i> &gt; <i>My Statement </i>and<i> </i>select the corresponding account for query.</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Account name</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Enter your account name registered with the transfer-out broker, which must be the same as your Longbridge account name.</p></td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(2) Enter the details of the stocks to be transferred into Longbridge. After confirming the details are correct, click <i>Next.</i></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Please note: Longbridge now supports entering the cost price per share (optional).</p><ul><li>If not entered: The cost price per share will be calculated based on the closing price on the day the transfer is completed.</li><li>If you need to enter: Please enter the cost price you actually want to display. After the transfer is completed, profits and losses will be calculated based on the cost price you entered.</li><li>The cost price cannot be modified once it is entered. If you have any questions, please contact customer service.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 3. Confirm the information and submit the application. Check the information such as the transfer-out broker, stock code, and number of shares, and submit the application if correct. If there are any errors or omissions, please go back to modify.</p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">2. Submitting a transfer-out request to Futu Securities</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Select <i>Trading</i> &gt; <i>Securities Account</i> &gt; <i>All</i> &gt; <i>Transfer out stocks</i>.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Choose the market corresponding to your transfer-out stock and fill in the transfer information (the example below shows the US stock transfer process).</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Select <i>transfer out US stocks</i>, choose Long Bridge HK Limited as the receiving broker, enter Longbridge information, and click <i>Next</i> after confirmation.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Longbridge information (US stocks):</strong></p><figure class="table"><table><thead><tr><th><p style="margin-left: 0cm;
