Introduction to the US stock overnight session</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The overnight session operates from 20:00 to 3:50 ET the next day.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The first overnight session of the week begins at 20:00 ET on Sunday and ends at 3:50 ET on Monday, while the last session runs from 20:00 ET on Thursday to 3:50 ET on Friday.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The overnight session marks the start of a trading day; the session beginning at 20:00 ET is attributed to the next trading day. For example, the overnight session from 20:00 ET on Monday to 3:50 ET on Tuesday is part of Tuesday's trading day. Underlying varieties eligible for overnight trading</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The varieties available for overnight trading include stocks and ETFs. You can identify whether a variety supports overnight trading by checking the <strong>24H</strong> label on its details page. The range of varieties eligible for overnight trading will continue to expand.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 80.16%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/2fc41f6fcc935097febc546cb4faa890"/></figure><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">3. How to access overnight trading market data</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">When overnight trading begins, you can view the overnight trading snapshots, intraday chart, trade details, trade statistics, and top bid/ask quotes.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 80.16%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/ef0e794496544509fbb7b8f9444d6ef1"/></figure><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">4. Order rules and types for overnight trading</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Overnight trading only supports limit orders, which are valid for the current trading day only.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Currently, there are two ways to place overnight trading orders:</p><ul><li>On the <strong>trading floor</strong>, select the overnight session and proceed with order placement.</li><li>In the <strong>quick trade</strong> interface, enable the overnight trading icon and proceed with order placement.</li></ul><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 80.16%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/a4a2c127cf40a3e5c0f2e0938ac4748e"/></figure><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">5.Calculation for positions and profits/losses (P&amp;L) during overnight trading</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">You can choose whether to use overnight prices by navigating to <i>Settings</i> &gt; <i>Portfolio Settings </i>&gt; <i>Asset calculation method</i>. Sessions are attributed to the next trading day.</li><li>Eligible varieties: Available for select stocks and ETFs—identified by the <strong>24H</strong> label on the details page. Coverage is continuously expanding.</li><li>Market data: After overnight trading begins, you can access intraday charts, trade details, top bid/ask quotes, and more.</li><li>Trading method: Only limit orders are supported, with day-only validity.
