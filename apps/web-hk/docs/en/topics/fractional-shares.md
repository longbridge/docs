<p style="margin-left: 0cm">A fractional share order of US stocks refers to an order in the US securities market with a trade quantity that includes a decimal. Fractional shares trading typically offers lower costs because you can purchase only the desired amount of stock instead of using extra funds to buy a whole share, thereby lowering the investment threshold and enabling flexible portfolio allocation.</p><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>1. Stocks eligible for fractional shares trading</strong></h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm">You can find the stocks that support fractional shares trading under <i>Market</i> &gt; <i>Classify</i> &gt; <i>Fractional Shares</i>. Additionally, the details page for individual stocks supporting fractional shares trading will display a fractional share icon.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/73bc3e6e77f938294842f6e3db255588"/></figure><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>2. Process of fractional shares trading</strong></h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Select a US stock that supports fractional shares trading.</p><ul><li>Enter a decimal number in the <i>Qty</i> field (up to 4 decimal places).</li><li>Enter the desired amount in the <i>Amount</i> field. The system will automatically calculate and convert it into the corresponding number of shares based on the current stock price.</li></ul><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/93b7f30ec2a646b19b6f0190ee912e6e"/></figure><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>3. Corporate actions for fractional shares</strong></h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Mandatory corporate actions, such as dividends and stock splits, are supported. Other voluntary corporate actions, such as rights issues and offers, are not allowed.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The operation process remains unchanged. For corporate actions involving stock splits or reverse stock splits, you may receive fractional shares. For example, if a stock undergoes a 1-for-10 reverse split and you hold 5 shares before the reverse split, you will receive 0.5 shares after the reverse split.</p><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>4. On early close days, the hours are ET 09:30–13:00.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Q2: What order types are supported for fractional shares trading?</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Fractional share orders currently support limit orders and market orders. Conditional orders or grid orders are not yet supported.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Q3: Does fractional shares trading support margin financing and short selling?</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Fractional shares cannot be sold short. Fractional shares trading supports unified buying power, and the margin rate is the same as for whole shares.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Q4: Can I modify fractional share orders?</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Modifying fractional share orders is currently not supported. To adjust an order, you must first cancel it and then place a new order.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="background-color: rgb(248,249,250); color: rgb(31,31,31)">Q5: What is the minimum amount for fractional share orders in the US stock market? </span></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="background-color: rgb(248,249,250); color: rgb(31,31,31)">Fractional share orders must have a minimum transaction amount of USD 1.</span></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
