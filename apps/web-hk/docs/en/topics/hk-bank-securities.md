<p>Bank-securities transfers allow you to conveniently, quickly, and securely move funds between your bank account and your securities account, all without the need for online banking remittances. This guide provides a detailed introduction to bank-securities transfers, the activation and transfer process via the Minsheng Hong Kong App and online banking, and important notes to help you complete relevant operations smoothly.</p><h2><strong>1. Activation process</strong></h2><ul><li><strong>Applying for activation in the Minsheng Hong Kong App</strong></li></ul><p>Step 1. Go to Minsheng Hong Kong App &gt; <i>Bank and Securities</i>. Click <i>Search</i> to search for Long Bridge HK Limited, and select <i>Associate</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/ae562cbda3223bc5a588a9add84ce077"/></figure><p>Step 2. For merchant name, enter <strong>Long Bridge HK Limited</strong>, and for associated account, enter your <strong>Longbridge account number</strong>. Click <i>submit</i>. A message will be displayed, indicating that your application for association has been submitted and you need to wait for approval.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/d7b57423fac953d73801f4a721e45966"/></figure><ul><li><strong>Applying for activation via Minsheng Hong Kong online banking</strong></li></ul><p>Step 1. Log in to your Minsheng Bank Hong Kong online banking account.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/eebdd733b99e9f882e6aa6e6efd85949"/></figure><p>Step 2. After successful login, click <i>Bank-Securities Transfer</i> &gt; <i>Associated Account Maintenance</i> &gt; <i>Add Associated Account</i> to activate the bank-securities transfer service.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/0c1cc11641d4f68d18390f498b44e536"/></figure><p>Step 3. For merchant name, select <i>Long Bridge HK Limited</i>, and for associated account, enter your <strong>Longbridge integrated account number</strong> (e.g., H12345678).</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/14a40fd7c9e2fb4a55e6b000cc103f80"/></figure><p>Step 4. Insert UKEY and enter your password for verification. After the application for association is submitted, wait for review.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/7bb0607f77215786a0dc1af310e225e8"/></figure><h2><strong>2. Transfer process</strong></h2><ul><li><strong>Mobile banking transfer</strong></li></ul><p>Step 1. Go to the Minsheng Hong Kong App &gt; <i>Banking and Securities</i>.</p><p>Step 2. Select the associated <strong>Long Bridge HK Limited</strong> and click <i>Transfer</i> &gt; <i>Bank to Merchant</i> on the right.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/cecc135c95cccab9396552f96c26a246"/></figure><p>Step 3. Enter your <strong>debit account</strong> and <strong>transfer amount</strong>, confirm the information, submit the transfer, and wait for the funds to be credited.</p><blockquote><p>Note: The Minsheng online banking transfer service is available from 09:00 to 18:00 on Hong Kong working days. Transfer applications are not accepted outside these hours or on non-working days (Saturdays, Sundays, and Hong Kong public holidays).</p></blockquote><ul><li><strong>Online banking transfer</strong></li></ul><p>Step 1. Log in to online banking, click <i>Bank-Securities Transfer</i> &gt; <i>Transfer Out and Transfer In</i> &gt; <i>Bank to Merchant</i>, and make the transfer.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/44cd27be09b791198137e94e048c3024"/></figure><p>Step 2. Enter your <strong>debit account</strong> and <strong>transfer amount</strong>, confirm the information, <strong>submit</strong> the transfer, and wait for the funds to be credited.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/e29da7b7080180686beafb666df5e2ee"/></figure><h2><strong>Notes</strong></h2><ol><li>You need to register important information such as a valid mobile phone number, email address, and up-to-date identity documents with your banks.</li><li>The name on the bank account used for the transfer must match the name on your Longbridge account. You cannot use another person's bank account (or a joint account) for transfers. Any refund fees incurred due to non-compliance will be borne by you.</li><li>It takes time for the bank to process the transfer. Bank notifications stating &#34;transfer initiated&#34; do not indicate immediate receipt by Longbridge. Settlement and approval are required after the funds arrive in Longbridge. Banks and Longbridge do not process transfers during Hong Kong public holidays. Please allow sufficient time for processing.</li><li>Longbridge currently does not accept cash deposits.</li></ol><h2><strong>Disclosures</strong></h2><p><i>The above guide screenshots are provided by users and are for reference only.</i></p><p><i>If there are any discrepancies between the screenshots and the actual interfaces, the actual bank interfaces shall prevail.
