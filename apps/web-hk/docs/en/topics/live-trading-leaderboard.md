<p><strong>Real trading results, out in the open, measured side by side — that's the Live Trading Leaderboard.</strong></p><p>The leaderboard is generated from the real transaction data that participating users have authorized for public display, and ranks by time-weighted rate of return (the true level of return after the effect of deposits and withdrawals is stripped out). There are two tracks: the <strong>Monthly Leaderboard</strong> runs by calendar month, while the <strong>Annual Leaderboard</strong> is a year-long marathon held only once a year.</p><h3>Who can appear on the leaderboard?</h3><p>To be ranked, you must meet all of the following:</p><ul><li>Total account assets of no less than HKD 100,000</li><li>A positive rate of return for the statistical period</li><li>Live Trading authorization enabled, and kept enabled for no fewer than 30% of the days in the period (see the notes below)</li></ul><h3>I've just enabled Live Trading authorization — when can I compete?</h3><ul><li><strong>Monthly Leaderboard:</strong> if you enable authorization only within the last 10 days of the month, you will not be ranked for that month (the leaderboard will show &#34;Not ranked yet&#34;); you will compete normally from the next period onward. (The Monthly Leaderboard program takes effect from the date of this announcement.)</li><li><strong>Annual Leaderboard:</strong> requires more than 180 cumulative days of authorization, with authorization kept enabled throughout the most recent 90 days. (The Annual Leaderboard program starts on 1 January 2027.)</li><li>In addition, users with any record of community content violations during the statistical period are disqualified.</li></ul><h3>How are rankings determined?</h3><ul><li>There is only one core metric: the account's overall time-weighted rate of return for the statistical period, ranked from highest to lowest</li><li>If returns are tied, the profit amount is compared, and the higher amount ranks first</li><li>The leaderboard is updated once a day and shows data as at the previous trading day</li></ul><h3>When are results announced?</h3><ul><li><strong>Monthly Leaderboard:</strong> final rankings for the previous month are published within the first 3 days of each month</li><li><strong>Annual Leaderboard:</strong> final rankings for the previous year are published within the first 3 days of each year</li></ul><h3>What do you get for ranking?</h3><p><strong>Monthly Leaderboard:</strong></p><ul><li>1st–3rd place: 600,000 Task Coins each</li><li>4th–10th place: 300,000 Task Coins each</li><li>11th–50th place: 50,000 Task Coins each</li><li>All top 50 receive the exclusive badge for that month's Monthly Leaderboard</li></ul><p><strong>Annual Leaderboard:</strong></p><ul><li>1st–3rd place: 3,000,000 Task Coins each</li><li>4th–10th place: 1,200,000 Task Coins each</li><li>11th–50th place: 100,000 Task Coins each</li><li>All top 50 receive the Annual Badge — the highest honour of the year in the Live Trading system</li></ul><p><i>Task Coins can be redeemed for physical gifts in the </i><a href="https://activity.lbkrs.com/opa/points/mall"><i><strong>Redemption Mall</strong></i></a><i>; redemption rules are as stated on the store page.</i></p><h3>On fairness</h3><ul><li>Users who do not meet the Live Trading authorization duration requirement for a period will have both their ranking and their badge for that period revoked</li><li>To keep the competition fair, please be sure to comply with the community rules, program rules and other applicable rules.
