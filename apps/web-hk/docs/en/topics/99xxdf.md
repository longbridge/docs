<p style="margin-left: 0cm">The following presents the core indicators of virtual assets, including 24-hour price/volume, the Committee, and market cap performance and supply. This information enables you to swiftly grasp an asset's activity level, volatility, and supply-demand situation.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>24-hour highest</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The highest price generated by an asset in the past 24 hours.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>24-hour lowest</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The lowest price generated by an asset in the past 24 hours.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>24-hour quantity</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The total number of trades of an asset in the past 24 hours.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>24-hour volume</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The total trading volume of an asset in the past 24 hours.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>24-hour range</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">24-hour range refers to the percentage between the difference of the day's highest and lowest prices relative to the previous closing price.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Formula: 24-hour range = (24-hour highest – 24-hour lowest) / Previous closing price * 100%</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Bid-Ask ratio</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The bid-ask ratio, also known as the order ratio, iis an indicator that measures the relative strength of buy and sell orders over a specific period.The ratio's value ranges from -100% to +100%. A value of +100% indicates that all outstanding orders are bids; a value of -100% indicates that all outstanding orders are asks. A ratio of 0 means the volume of bids and asks is equal. </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Formula: Bis-Ask ratio = (Buy order volume – Sell order volume) / (Buy order volume + Sell orders volume) * 100%</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Circulating volume</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The number of currencies or tokens issued by an asset.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Circulating market cap</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Formula: Circulating market cap = Circulating volume * Asset current price</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Maximum supply</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The maximum number of cryptocurrencies. Different cryptocurrencies have different supply limits. For example, Bitcoin has a maximum supply of 21 million coins, while Ether has no maximum supply limit.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Maximum supply market cap</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Formula: Maximum supply market cap = Maximum supply * Current asset price</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Note: For cryptocurrencies with no maximum supply limit, this field is left blank.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Circulation rate</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Formula: Circulation rate = Circulating volume / Maximum supply * 100%</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Note: For cryptocurrencies with no maximum supply limit, this field is left blank.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Key Takeaways: </strong>The virtual asset major indicators include: 24-hour highest/lowest, 24-hour quantity/volume, 24-hour range, Bid-Ask ratio, circulating volume, circulating market cap, maximum supply, maximum supply market cap, and circulation rate.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
