<p style="margin-left: 0cm">The process of transferring stocks from uSMART Securities to Longbridge mainly involves two steps: submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge and submitting a transfer-out request to uSMART Securities. Go to the Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>Transfer</i> to submit a transfer-in application. Confirm the information.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Enter the details of the stocks to be transferred into Longbridge. After confirming the details are correct, click <i>Next.</i></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Please note: Longbridge now supports entering the cost price per share (optional).</p><ul><li>If not entered: The cost price per share will be calculated based on the closing price on the day the transfer is completed.</li><li>If you need to enter: Please enter the cost price you actually want to display. After the transfer is completed, profits and losses will be calculated based on the cost price you entered.</li><li>The cost price cannot be modified once it is entered. If you have any questions, please contact customer service.</li><li>Confirm information and submit the request.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Check the information such as the transfer-out broker, stock code, and number of shares, and submit the application if correct.
