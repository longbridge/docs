<p><strong>The account cancellation process is as follows:</strong><br/><strong>Before proceeding with the cancellation, please take note of the following:</strong></p><ol><li><strong>Asset Settlement:</strong> Ensure that there are no pending assets to be settled, no outstanding liabilities, and that all third-party bindings have been removed.</li><li><strong>Data Deletion:</strong> Upon cancellation, your personal information, account details, and transaction records will be permanently deleted and cannot be recovered.</li><li><strong>Rights Forfeiture:</strong> Cancellation is considered a waiver of any future entitlements that may arise (e.g., IPO refunds, etc.).</li><li><strong>Registration Restriction:</strong> Within 90 days after account cancellation, the current phone number cannot be used to register a new account.</li></ol><ul><li><strong>Account Opening Completed:</strong> Please fill in the account cancellation application form: <a href="https://pub.lbkrs.com/files/202602/LS5FyJKJf5ZmQAVT/___20260225.pdf">https://pub.lbkrs.com/files/202602/LS5FyJKJf5ZmQAVT/___20260225.pdf</a> and send it to <a href="mailto:service@longbridge.hk">service@longbridge.hk</a>. Processing is expected within 7 business days. All assets must be settled and third-party bindings such as eDDA/bank-securities transfers must be removed prior to cancellation. Cash remaining in the account may be forfeited, however stocks cannot be forfeited — Hong Kong stocks may be withdrawn in physical form or transferred to another brokerage.</li><li><strong>Account Opening Not Completed:</strong> Please contact customer service for assistance.</li></ul><p> </p><p><i>This article is for reference only.</i></p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p><p> </p>
