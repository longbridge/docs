<p style="margin-left: 0cm">The process of transferring stocks from Stellar Securities to Longbridge mainly involves two steps: submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge and submitting a transfer-out request to Stellar Securities. Below are the detailed descriptions for these two steps.</p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">1. Submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Go to the Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>Transfer</i> to submit a transfer-in application. If you have already deposited funds, select <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>More</i> &gt; <i>Share Transfer-In</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4kx7lrsahy5vrhs1c_.jpeg"/></figure><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4kx2m8z8nlcyrtg3_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. After confirming the details are correct, click <i>Next.</i></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Please note: Longbridge now supports entering the cost price per share (optional).</p><ul><li>If not entered: The cost price per share will be calculated based on the closing price on the day the transfer is completed.</li><li>If you need to enter: Please enter the cost price you actually want to display. After the transfer is completed, profits and losses will be calculated based on the cost price you entered.</li><li>The cost price cannot be modified once it is entered. If you have any questions, please contact customer service.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 3. Confirm the information and submit the application. Check the information such as the transfer-out broker, stock code, and number of shares, and submit the application if correct.
