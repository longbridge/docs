<p style="margin-left: 0cm">This article explains the automatic investment/redemption mechanism, trading time, and cycle of Cash Plus.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>1. Automatic investment:</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">After you enable Cash Plus, idle funds in your Longbridge Securities account will be automatically invested into the corresponding money market fund. </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>2. Automatic redemption:</strong></p><ul><li>When deductions occur (e.g., for purchasing stocks or other products and withdrawals), holdings in the money market fund will be automatically redeemed as needed.</li><li>All holdings will be redeemed when the automatic investment agreement is terminated.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>3. Trading time:</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Automatic investment/redemption occurs at a fixed time every day, with no need to specify trading time.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>4. Cycle:</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Funds are automatically invested into the money market fund and begin accruing returns within one trading day after arriving in the account.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Note: Processing time is calculated based on Hong Kong trading days and will be postponed during holidays.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>5. Calculation of 7-day earnings and 7-day annualized yield:</strong></p><p>This metric is annualized based on the net return calculated from the most recent 7 calendar days and does not represent the actual one-year return.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Key points</strong>:</p><ul><li>Automatic investment: Idle funds automatically invested after signing agreement.</li><li>Automatic redemption: Automatic fund redemption for deductions; full redemption upon agreement cancellation.</li><li>Processing Time: Funds invested within 1 working day (postponed on holidays).</li><li>Earning calculation: 7-day earnings and annualized yield displayed based on calendar days.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
