<p>Wire transfer is a deposit method of Longbridge. You need to obtain beneficiary information from Longbridge App, transfer funds to your Longbridge bank account via mobile or online banking, and upload the proof of remittance. The following takes HSBC as an example to introduce the specific operation process, transfer fees, processing time, and related notes.</p><h2><strong>Wire transfer process</strong></h2><ul><li><strong>Mobile banking</strong></li></ul><p>Step 1. Obtain beneficiary information.</p><figure class="table" style="width: 100%"><table><colgroup><col style="width: 41.09%"/><col style="width: 58.91%"/></colgroup><thead><tr><th><strong>Beneficiary name</strong></th><th><strong>Long Bridge HK Limited</strong></th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Beneficiary account number (HKD)</td><td>741733224001</td></tr><tr><td>Beneficiary account number (USD)</td><td>741733224201</td></tr><tr><td>Beneficiary bank</td><td>The Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation Limited</td></tr><tr><td>Bank code</td><td>004</td></tr><tr><td>SWIFT code</td><td>HSBCHKHHHKH</td></tr><tr><td>Bank address</td><td>Level 3 &amp; BL1, HSBC Main Building, 1 Queen's Road Central, Central, Hong Kong</td></tr><tr><td>Transfer fee</td><td>Fees are determined by the bank. Longbridge charges no fees.</td></tr><tr><td>Processing time</td><td>Transfers within the same bank in Hong Kong are expected to arrive within 2 hours, while interbank transfers in Hong Kong may take 1–3 working days. The exact timeframe depends on the bank.</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p>Step 2. Go to the HSBC Bank App &gt; <i>Pay &amp; Transfer</i> &gt; <i>Other local payees/FPS</i> to select the beneficiary.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 50%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/eeb2b47d480855c5dc2557e078007cef"/></figure><p>Step 3. Select the bank account you wish to transfer from.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 50%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/f243e5cb0fcc80220482f2650a24531f"/></figure><p>Step 4. Click <i>Pay someone new</i> &gt; <i>Select a method</i>, and select <i>Account Number</i> in the drop-down list.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 50%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/566ea0777785b51b9fe5c1e923bff050"/></figure><p>Step 5. Enter the beneficiary account number. You can check <i>Add to the beneficiary list</i>. For specific beneficiary bank information, see the details provided at the beginning of this article. Click <i>continue</i> after filling in the details.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 52.04%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/082f71044d5bf9c5c277cf3b83142fe7"/></figure><p>Step 6. Enter the transfer amount and submit it. It is recommended to set the effective date to <i>now</i>.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 50%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/aa3b3aee47206ab5f9baebb4fabac11b"/></figure><p>Step 7. Confirm and submit the transfer application.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 50%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/11231735f8513a20aff4a2296d2bb055"/></figure><p>Step 8. [Important] <strong>To ensure timely processing, please save a screenshot (or take a photo) of the proof of remittance and upload it via the Longbridge App</strong>.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 50%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/563de52b047969bf1f3736f72ae7a7e9"/></figure><ul><li><strong>Online banking</strong></li></ul><p>Step 1. Go to <a href="https://www.hsbc.com.hk/zh-hk/ways-to-bank/internet/">the HSBC Hong Kong website</a> and click <i>Log on</i> &gt; <i>HSBC Online Banking</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/01c0729fa2c1fb0283339c5f219c72f9"/></figure><p>Step 2. After logging in, click <i>My banking - account dashboard &gt; Move money &gt; Pay or Transfer</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/d6bc70d7735f8b59ef0c52910f32623c"/></figure><p>Step 3. On the transfer page, for first-time transfers, select <i>New payee</i>. For subsequent transfers, select <i>My payees</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/d0625b1d1fd96dcbb2a911c259ba023e"/></figure><p>Step 4. Fill in the transfer currency and amount. In the remarks section, you can enter your Longbridge integrated account number and name, for example, ZHANGSAN H1234567.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/571ae4adfa29da6169cdda8cdc6e3391"/></figure><p>Step 5. After verifying the information, click <i>continue</i>, and complete the security verification. If a success message is displayed, the transfer is completed.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/d2fa5a615726f33da5b4e574052b180c"/></figure><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/2696000a27eb80ac3bcd4c0ce3bcdd4e"/></figure><p>Step 6. [Important] <strong>To ensure timely processing, please save a screenshot (or take a photo) of the proof of remittance and upload it via the Longbridge App</strong>.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 50%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/f05bf2b10388ab2ac85b4f467acb232b"/></figure><h2><strong>Notes</strong></h2><ol><li>You need to register important information such as a valid mobile phone number, email address, and up-to-date identity documents with your banks.</li><li>The name on the bank account used for the transfer must match the name on your Longbridge account. You cannot use another person's bank account for transfers. Any refund fees incurred due to non-compliance will be borne by you.</li><li>It takes time for the bank to process the transfer. Bank notifications stating &#34;transfer initiated&#34; do not indicate immediate receipt by Longbridge. Settlement and approval are required after the funds arrive in Longbridge.
