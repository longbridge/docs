<p style="margin-left: 0cm">This article explains Longbridge's currency exchange feature and its operational procedures.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>About currency exchange</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Currency exchange refers to the exchange of one currency for another within a securities account. To facilitate fund transfers between Hong Kong and US stock markets, Long Bridge (HK) Limited provides currency exchange services.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Currency exchange process</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Open Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio </i>&gt; <i>More </i>&gt; <i>Currency Exchange</i><strong>, </strong>where you can check the current exchange rate and convert currencies by entering the desired amount.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Key Takeaways:</strong></p><ul><li>Path: Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio </i>&gt;<i> More </i>&gt; <i>Currency Exchange</i></li><li>Real-time exchange rate: Displays the latest rates at the time of conversion</li><li>Applicable scenarios: Include fund allocation for Hong Kong/US stock investments, and multi-currency account management</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Notes</strong></p><ol><li>Exchange rates: The rates displayed on the exchange page are for reference only. The actual converted amount shall prevail.</li><li>Reminder: The currency exchange feature is solely for trade settlement purposes. Please refrain from using it for currency speculation. Requests unrelated to trading will be rejected. Proceed with caution.</li><li>Rounding: Due to system limitations, all currency exchange transactions will be rounded to two decimal places for cash balances.</li></ol><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>FAQs</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Q1: Which currencies are supported for exchange?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Currency exchange supports USD and HKD.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Q2: Are there any fees for currency exchange?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">No service fees are charged for currency exchange.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Q3: How is the exchange rate calculated?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Exchange rates fluctuate in real-time, and the rate applied will be the one in effect at the time of conversion.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Q4: How long does currency exchange take? </strong>(The following information is for reference only, and actual processing times may vary.)</p><ul><li>Real-time processing: Orders submitted between 08:00 on trading days and 05:00 the next day are typically completed in real-time (excluding December 25 and January 1).</li><li>Non-real-time processing: Orders submitted during non-trading days will be processed when exchange services reopen on the next trading day. For example, an order placed on Sunday will be completed after 08:00 on Monday.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Note: Longbridge provides advance notices for holiday schedules. Stay updated via official announcements.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
