<p>For users in Hong Kong, opening an account by submitting a check involves specific procedures and requirements. Here are the steps for account opening by check.</p><p>Step 1. Open the Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i>, enter the account opening application page, then click <i>Open an account</i>.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 80.16%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/4c603307bb1bdc7879a0b9f2ed047f6c"/></figure><p>Step 2. Upload your identification document. Once it is confirmed, click <i>Next, Personal Information</i>. Fill in the personal information, and upload proof of address, then click <i>Next, Employment.</i></p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/b79135361bf15303f1ddc42f651bc512"/></figure><blockquote><p>Tips: If you do not have your identification document with you at the moment, click the blue text labeled<i>Click to upload later</i>, as shown in the figure above. Fill in the other required information, and then upload your identification document after the <i>Risk Disclosure</i> page.</p></blockquote><p>Step 3. Enter your occupation information. The email address will be used to receive important information such as statements. Please ensure that the information you entered is correct before clicking <i>Confirm</i>.</p><p>Step 4. Fill in <i>Investment Experience</i>  and <i>Confirmation of  Compliance.</i></p><p>Step 5. Read and acknowledge the <i>Risk Disclosure</i>, click <i>Next, confirm</i>  to confirm that all the information you have entered is correct, and then click <i>Next.</i></p><p>Step 6. On the authentication page, locate the <i>Choose other account opening methods</i> option,  and select check verification.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/f5410438389338d9435f71d9a9732b8c"/></figure><p>Step 7. After mailing your documents to Longbridge, tap <i>sent</i> to confirm dispatch.</p><p>Step 8. Sign in the blank space, then click <i>Done</i> to submit your account opening application.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/51bc65a86b02aea6debef4bf5f721789"/></figure><p>Step 9. Please be patient after submitting your account opening application. We will review your application as soon as possible. You can check the review results on the Portfolio page of the Longbridge App, through App notifications, or via the email address you provided when opening your account.</p><p> </p><p><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p><i>The information provided herein is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
