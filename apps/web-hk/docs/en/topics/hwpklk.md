<p style="margin-left: 0cm">The process of transferring stocks from Tiger Brokers to Longbridge mainly involves two steps: submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge and submitting a transfer-out request to Tiger Brokers (integrated account). Below are the detailed descriptions for these two steps.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Note: The following guidelines are for reference only. For the specific transfer process, please contact your Tiger Brokers account manager or Tiger Brokers customer service to confirm. They will provide a corresponding transfer plan based on your account situation.</strong></p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">1. Submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Go to the Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>Transfer</i> to submit a transfer-in application. If you have already deposited funds, select <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>More</i> &gt; <i>Share Transfer-In</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4m8d5khhya4uxw9wu_.jpeg"/></figure><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4m8848mu4cny75bbb_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Fill in the transfer details and confirm transfer-out stock information. Select Tiger Brokers as the transfer-out broker, enter the account holder's name and account number, click <i>Next</i>, and fill in the stock information.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Please note: Longbridge now supports entering the cost price per share (optional).</p><ul><li>If not entered: The cost price per share will be calculated based on the closing price on the day the transfer is completed.</li><li>If you need to enter: Please enter the cost price you actually want to display. After the transfer is completed, profits and losses will be calculated based on the cost price you entered.</li><li>The cost price cannot be modified once it is entered. If you have any questions, please contact customer service.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 3. Confirm stock transfer details and submit the transfer application.</p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">2. Confirming the account type of Tiger Brokers</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Tiger Brokers has two account types: global accounts and integrated accounts. Their processes for submitting transfer-out requests are different. Please check which account type your stocks are held in.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Log in to Tiger Brokers, click <i>My</i> &gt; <i>Tiger Account</i>, and check the account type.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">If you have a global account, click <a href="https://support.longbridge.hk/topics/1npp30q/4c4yrgk">here</a> to view relevant instructions.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>3. Submitting a transfer-out request to Tiger Brokers (integrated account)</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Contact Tiger Brokers' telephone/online customer service to submit a stock transfer application. Since the process for submitting transfer-out requests may vary across brokers, please refer to the requirements of the other broker for details.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Click <a href="https://support.longbridge.hk/topics/1qawjoc/47azunp?locale=zh-CN">here</a> to see Longbridge information for stock transfer-in.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Notes:</strong></p><ol><li>Stock transfers between different brokers are limited to transfers between accounts under the same name.</li><li>Fees for stock transfer: Longbridge does not charge any fees. Fees may be charged by the broker who transfers the stock out.</li><li>Time required for stock transfer: The processing time depends on the other broker. Once the other broker transfers the stocks out, Longbridge will deposit them into your account within 1–2 working days.</li></ol><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only.
