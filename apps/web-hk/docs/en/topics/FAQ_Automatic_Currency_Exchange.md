<p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black">Here you'll find quick answers to common questions about </span> <span style="color: black">auto-repayment service.</span></p><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black">1. What is auto-repayment?</span></h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black">When your account has a settled debit balance and available funds in other currencies, the system will automatically convert the currency to lower interest charges on your debit balance.</span></p><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black">2. How do I enable auto-repayment service?</span></h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black">You can enable or disable this feature on App &gt;<i> Me &gt; Settings &gt; Account Preference &gt; Auto-repayment</i>.</span></p><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black">3. Under what circumstances will my auto-repayment be triggered?</span></h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="background-color: white; color: black">If you have enabled the auto-repayment feature, it will be triggered on Hong Kong business days at 07:00 HKT and 16:15 HKT, as long as both of the following conditions are met:</span></p><ul><li><span style="background-color: white; color: black">Your account has a settled debit balance of at least HKD 20,000 or its equivalent in other currencies.</span></li><li><span style="background-color: white; color: black">Your account balance in other currencies is higher than or equal to HKD 1,000.</span></li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="background-color: white; color: black">However, if you initiate any offset or conversion before the mentioned time, auto-repayment will not be triggered.</span><span style="color: black"> </span></p><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black">4. What exchange rate will be used for my auto-repayment?</span></h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="background-color: white; color: black">The prevailing exchange rate as of 07:00 HKT and 16:15 HKT on each business day will be used for auto-repayment. Please note that the exchange rate displayed is for reference only; you should refer to the actual converted amount after the transaction is completed.  </span></p><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black">5. When exactly will my auto-repayment be triggered? </span></h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="background-color: white; color: black">Longbridge HongKong will confirm and process settlement and delivery for your security account. This applies if it meets the conditions of service or the conditions of the cash plus. The process is carried out on each business day, as defined in the &#34;Client Agreement.&#34;</span></p><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black">6. Where can I find the conversion record?</span></h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black">You can check your conversion record on App <i>&gt; Portfolio &gt; More &gt; Fund Details</i>, or you may find more details of currency conversion in your daily statement.</span></p><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black"><strong>7.</strong> How do I disable auto-repayment?</span></h3><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black">You can disable this feature on App <i>&gt; Me</i></span><i> </i><span style="color: black"><i>&gt; Settings</i></span><i> </i><span style="color: black"><i>&gt; Account Preference &gt; Auto-repayment</i>.</span></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black"><strong>Disclosures</strong></span></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><span style="color: black"><i>This article is for informational purposes only and does not constitute financial advice.</i></span></p>
