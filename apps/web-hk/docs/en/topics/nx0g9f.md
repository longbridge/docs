<p style="margin-left: 0cm">The process of transferring stocks from Homaer to Longbridge mainly involves two steps: submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge and submitting a transfer-out request to Interactive Brokers (IB). Below are the detailed descriptions for these two steps.</p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">1. Submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Go to the Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>Transfer</i> to submit a transfer-in application. If you have already deposited funds, select <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>More</i> &gt; <i>Share Transfer-In</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4mqfool4uy2piqg3h_.jpeg"/></figure><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4mqatadaffko76ycp_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Fill in the transfer information (Below is an example for US stocks. If you want to transfer Hong Kong stocks, please fill in the Hong Kong stock information)<strong>. Select IB as the transfer-out broker, </strong>enter the account holder's name and account number, click <i>Next</i>, and fill in the stock information.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Please note: Longbridge now supports entering the cost price per share (optional).</p><ul><li>If not entered: The cost price per share will be calculated based on the closing price on the day the transfer is completed.</li><li>If you need to enter: Please enter the cost price you actually want to display. After the transfer is completed, profits and losses will be calculated based on the cost price you entered.</li><li>The cost price cannot be modified once it is entered. If you have any questions, please contact customer service.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 3. Confirm stock transfer details and submit the transfer application.</p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">2. Submitting a transfer-out request to IB</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Log in to the official website of IB to submit an application for transferring stocks out.</p><ul><li>Contact your Homaer account manager to obtain IB account information.</li><li>Log in to the IB official website: <a href="https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/cn/home.php">https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/en/home.php</a>.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Submit instructions for transferring stocks out.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Hong Kong stocks</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(1) Path: <i>Transfer &amp; Pay</i> &gt; <i>Transfer Positions</i> &gt; <i>Transfer Out</i> &gt; <i>Basic FOP Transfer</i></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(2) Broker information</p><figure class="table"><table><thead><tr><th><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: center"><strong>Financial institution</strong></p></th><th><p style="margin-left: 0cm;
