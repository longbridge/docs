<p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">This article will introduce the bank card verification process and an example of the relevant verification materials.</p><h4>1. Bank card verification operation</h4><p>（1）On the withdrawal page, if you are prompted that your bank card has not been verified, you need to click <i>Verify Now</i> to verify your bank card information. You can continue to perform withdrawal operations only after the verification is approved.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 80.36%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/fd8ad5d074f8a5bfd298da3cf3fa01f0"/></figure><p>（2）Click <i>Upload</i> to upload the bank card verification materials to Longbridge for review. A maximum of 3 images can be uploaded. If the review is passed, a push notification or email will be sent to notify you.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 80.36%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/b0c09e1ee979433f69b8c38c0c5b8662"/></figure><h4>2. Example of bank card verification materials</h4><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">The materials uploaded for bank card verification must include your name and bank account number. You can choose <strong>any</strong>of the following materials: bank statement, bank card, or account opening information.</p><p>（1）Monthly bank statement</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/16992cfa3b1dbd697e37b0c1010223ac"/></figure><p>（2）Bank Card</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Note: Some bank cards do not display the cardholder's name on the front. You need to provide a photo of a physical bank card with the cardholder's name on the front, that is, one photo containing both your name and bank account number. Otherwise, the review process may be delayed.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/6a74d1f611627017c361da04e034493f"/></figure><p>（3）Account opening information</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Note: Please provide the successful account opening receipt as proof. Otherwise, the bank card verification request may be rejected.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/2b3df93a3c46f2afd9ca443fa982e304"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Notes:</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">1. The uploaded verification materials must include your name and bank account number. Please ensure that the bank verification materials match the name registered in your Longbridge account.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">2. Please ensure the accuracy of the verification materials. Incorrect information may affect your withdrawal process.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">3. After you submit the bank card verification, it is expected to be processed within 1–3 working days.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
