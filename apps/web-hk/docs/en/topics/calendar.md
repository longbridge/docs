<p> </p><p>Calendar spreads cover several distinct strategies, each suited to a different view on price and volatility. They let you pursue the returns that time value can generate while keeping risk managed.<br/>This article walks through four of them in detail: Long Call Calendar Spread, Long Put Calendar Spread, Short Call Calendar Spread, and Short Put Calendar Spread.
