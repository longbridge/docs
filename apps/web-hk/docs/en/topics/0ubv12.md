<p style="margin-left: 0cm">Fund trading is based on the fund company's local trading day (T day), with a daily cutoff at 09:05 Hong Kong Time. Specific details are as follows.</p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">1. Subscription:</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">T day refers to a fund trading day, with 09:05 as the cutoff. Orders placed before 09:05 are considered as placed on T day. Orders placed at or after 09:05 are considered as placed on T+1 (the next trading day). Weekends and public holidays (subject to the country where the fund company is located) are non-trading days. For orders placed on T day, the units will be confirmed according to the net worth of the fund on T day. Returns can be viewed starting from the second working day after the units are confirmed.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Order cancellation: You may cancel a submitted order before 09:05 on T Day.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Note: All times mentioned above are in Hong Kong Time.</p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">2. Redemption:</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">T day refers to a fund trading day, with 09:05 as the cutoff. Orders placed before 09:05 are considered as placed on T day. Orders placed at or after 09:05 are considered as placed on T+1 (the next trading day). Weekends and public holidays (subject to the country where the fund company is located) are non-trading days. For orders placed on T day, the redemption amount will be confirmed according to the net worth of the fund on T day.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Order cancellation: You may cancel a submitted order before 09:05 on T Day.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Note: All times mentioned above are in Hong Kong Time.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Key takeaways:</p><ul><li><strong>Cut-off time:</strong> The recognition of the trading day (T day) uses <strong>09:05 Hong Kong Time</strong> as the cutoff (excluding 09:05). Orders placed before this time will be calculated based on the net worth of that day.</li><li><strong>Cancellation rule</strong>: Both subscription and redemption orders can be canceled before <strong>09:05 Hong Kong Time on T Day</strong>.</li><li><strong>Units and returns: </strong>Returns can be viewed starting from the second working day after the units are confirmed.</li><li><strong>Non-trading days</strong>: Weekends and public holidays in the country where the fund company is located are not considered as trading days.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
