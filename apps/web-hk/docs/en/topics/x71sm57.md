<p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Here you'll find quick answers to common questions about about funds.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><strong>1. How do I choose the fund dividend method?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">You can choose the dividend method (cash dividend or dividend reinvestment) when subscribing to the fund. Once chosen, it cannot be modified.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><strong>2. What is the deadline for subscribing to funds?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Orders submitted before 12:00 noon on each trading day will be processed on the same day. Orders submitted after 12:00 noon or on non-trading days (e.g., fund holidays and public holidays) will be processed on the next trading day.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><strong>3. Is financing supported for fund subscriptions?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Financing is not supported for fund subscriptions. If the account currency differs from the fund currency, please exchange the currency first before submitting a subscription order.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><strong>4. Is there a specific order for fund redemption?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">There is no specific order for fund redemption. You can go to the corresponding fund page &gt; <i>Salable Fund Units</i> to view redeemable funds. After successful redemption, the funds will be returned via the original payment method.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><strong>5. When will the funds arrive after redemption?</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">If the fund redemption request is submitted before 12:00 noon on T day, the fund company will calculate the asset value based on the net worth on T day. The funds will arrive within 7 trading days.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</p>
