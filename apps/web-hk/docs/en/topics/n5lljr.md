<p style="margin-left: 0cm">The process of transferring stocks from Snowball X to Longbridge mainly involves two steps: notifying Longbridge to receive the stocks and notifying Interactive Brokers (IB) to transfer the stocks out. Below are the detailed description for these two steps.</p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">1. Notifying Longbridge to receive stocks</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Go to the Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>Transfer</i> to submit a transfer-in application. If you have already deposited funds, select <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>More</i> &gt; <i>Share Transfer-In</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4nnxt4lfwnfmzzuf_.jpeg"/></figure><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4nnqstgb7z6z83le_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Fill in stock transfer details. Select <strong>Snowball X</strong> as the transfer-out broker, enter the account holder's name and account number, click <i>Next</i>, and fill in the stock information.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Please note: Longbridge now supports entering the cost price per share (optional).</p><ul><li>If not entered: The cost price per share will be calculated based on the closing price on the day the transfer is completed.</li><li>If you need to enter: Please enter the cost price you actually want to display. After the transfer is completed, profits and losses will be calculated based on the cost price you entered.</li><li>The cost price cannot be modified once it is entered. If you have any questions, please contact customer service.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 3. Confirm stock transfer details and submit the transfer.</p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">2. Notifying IB to transfer stocks out</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Log in to the official website of IB to submit an application for transferring stocks out.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(1) Obtain IB login account information from Snowball X</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Log in to Snowball X, select <i>My</i> &gt; <i>Account</i> &gt; <i>Snowball X Backend System Account/Initial Password</i> to query the Snowball X backend system account and initial password.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4nq7lq9qurn2q55qr_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(2) Log in to the IB official website: <a href="https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/cn/home.php">https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/en/home.php</a></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(3) Log in to your IB account</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Use the obtained Snowball X backend system account and initial password to log in. If you have set a new password, use the new password to log in.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">If the password is incorrect, you can click <i>Forgot Password</i> or call Snowball X/IB customer service to retrieve it. IB customer service phone: +86 (021) 60868586.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(4) Obtain IB account information</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">After logging in to the IB official website, the account number in the portfolio section is your IB account number. You can also obtain the U account number (this account information is required when submitting the transfer-in application to Longbridge) from the Snowball X app account.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4nquz5zphcu6av6zb_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Submit instructions for transferring stocks out.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Hong Kong stocks</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(1) Path: <i>Transfer &amp; Pay</i> &gt; <i>Transfer Positions</i> &gt; <i>Transfer Out</i> &gt; <i>Basic FOP Transfer</i></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(2) Broker information</p><figure class="table"><table><thead><tr><th><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: center"><strong>Financial institutions</strong></p></th><th><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: center"><strong>Long Bridge HK Limited</strong></p></th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Your or your broker's account number at the above financial institution:</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">B02195+ Your Longbridge account number (starting with H), such as: B02195+H1234567</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Account name</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Your account name (in English)</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Contact name</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Settlement Team</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Contact number</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(+852) 3585 8944 / (+852) 3585 8915</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Email</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><a href="mailto:settlement@longbridge.hk">settlement@longbridge.hk</a></p></td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Click <a href="https://support.longbridge.hk/topics/1qawjoc/47azunp?locale=zh-CN">here</a> to see the information required for stock transfer.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4nrm16ed8rg7x3as_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>US stocks</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Log in to the IB website: <a href="https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/cn/home.php">https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/en/home.php</a></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Submit the application via the Message Center on the IB website.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(1) In the IB account, select <i>Help</i> &gt;
