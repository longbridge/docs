<p>What is a Dormant Account?</p><p>A dormant account is an account that has not had any activity for an extended period. To safeguard your account and comply with relevant requirements, Longbridge HK Limited (&#34;LBHK&#34;) will temporarily suspend trading and fund movement on such accounts until you complete the reactivation process.</p><h2>1. How an account becomes dormant</h2><p>If your account has not had any &#34;activity&#34; (defined below) for 24 consecutive months, the system will automatically flag it as dormant.</p><h2>2. What counts as &#34;activity&#34;?</h2><p>Any of the following counts as activity:</p><ul><li>Trading: orders to buy or sell stocks, options, ETFs, virtual assets, funds, or Cash Plus</li><li>Fund movement: deposits, withdrawals, securities transfers in or out, foreign-exchange conversions, transfers between main and sub-accounts</li><li>Corporate actions: dividends, dividend reinvestments, stock splits, bonus shares, share consolidations, or other corporate actions that change your holdings or cash balance</li></ul><h3>The following do NOT count as activity</h3><ul><li>Merely logging in, viewing market data, holdings, or statements</li><li>Interest, margin interest, platform fees, or refunds posted automatically by the system</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0in"><span style="color: #1F3864"><i>💡 In short, as long as your account has had any of the activities above over the past 24 months — including passive ones such as dividends or stock splits — your account will not become dormant.</i></span></p><h2>3. What happens when your account becomes dormant</h2><p>Once your account is flagged as dormant:</p><ul><li>Trading is suspended: you cannot buy or sell stocks, options, ETFs, virtual assets, funds, or Cash Plus</li><li>Fund movement is suspended: no deposits, withdrawals, securities transfers, FX, or main-/sub-account transfers</li><li>Your holdings, cash balance, and trade history are not affected</li><li>Passive corporate actions (such as dividends and stock splits) continue to be processed normally</li><li>LBHK does not charge any monthly fee or dormancy fee while your account is dormant</li></ul><h2>4. How to reactivate your account</h2><p>You can use any of the following channels:</p><ul><li>[Fastest] Longbridge App: log in and go to &#34;Account Activation&#34;, then follow the prompts to update your information, complete the questionnaire, upload supporting documents, and submit</li><li>Email: service@longbridge.hk</li><li>Customer Service hotline: Hong Kong +852 3851 1777 / +852 2652 4496; Global 400-071-2688</li><li>WhatsApp: +852 5223 6373</li><li>Online chat (in-app)</li></ul><h3>Information you may need to update</h3><p>Depending on your circumstances, the App will list the documents you need. These may include proof of identity, proof of address, occupation details, tax residency declaration, and source of wealth.</p><h3>Processing time</h3><ul><li>If your information has not changed and your case meets the criteria, the system may approve reactivation instantly</li><li>If manual review is required: typically 1 to 3 business days, depending on your circumstances</li><li>If we need additional documents from you, the timeline restarts from the date we receive the complete supporting documents</li></ul><h2>5. Frequently Asked Questions</h2><h3>Q1. If I do not plan to trade for a long time, will my account be closed automatically?</h3><p>No. A dormant account will not be closed automatically. You may keep it indefinitely; whenever you wish to trade again, simply apply to reactivate.</p><h3>Q2. I log in to view market data regularly — why is my account still dormant?</h3><p>Logging in and viewing market data are passive actions and do not count as &#34;activity&#34;. To prevent dormancy, you need to place an actual trade, make a fund movement, or have a corporate action change your holdings.</p><h3>Q3. I receive dividends regularly — will my account go dormant?</h3><p>No. Dividends, stock splits, bonus shares, and other corporate actions count as account activity.</p><h3>Q4. My sub-account has activity — will my main account still go dormant?</h3><p>No. Longbridge looks at your overall account activity, including the main account and all sub-accounts.</p><h2>6.
