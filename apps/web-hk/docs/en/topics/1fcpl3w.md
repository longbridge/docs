Send an email from your Alibaba Health work email address to CS at AliHealthSharePlans@computershare.com.au to request a stock transfer-out.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Receive an email reply from CS, which will detail the transfer steps. Reply to the email from CS to confirm your intention to transfer the stocks and pay the transfer service fee (HKD 375) to the beneficiary account provided in the email from CS.</p><p>After completing the payment, reply to CS's email again and provide the payment receipt.
