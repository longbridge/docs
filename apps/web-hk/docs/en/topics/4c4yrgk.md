<p style="margin-left: 0cm">The process of transferring stocks from Tiger Brokers to Longbridge mainly involves two steps: submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge and submitting a transfer-out request to Tiger Brokers (global account, IB). Below are the detailed descriptions for these two steps.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Note: The following guidelines are for reference only. For the specific transfer process, please contact your Tiger Brokers account manager or Tiger Brokers customer service to confirm. They will provide a corresponding transfer plan based on your account situation.</strong></p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">1. Submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Go to the Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>Transfer</i> to submit a transfer-in application. If you have already deposited funds, select <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>More</i> &gt; <i>Share Transfer-In</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4mzkx0wwpxvq9ufsr_.jpeg"/></figure><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4mzebf5ehvxj4nr6y_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Fill in the transfer information (Below is an example for US stocks. If you want to transfer Hong Kong stocks, please fill in the Hong Kong stock information). Select Tiger Brokers as the transfer-out broker, enter the account holder's name and account number, click <i>Next</i>, and fill in the stock information.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Please note: Longbridge now supports entering the cost price per share (optional).</p><ul><li>If not entered: The cost price per share will be calculated based on the closing price on the day the transfer is completed.</li><li>If you need to enter: Please enter the cost price you actually want to display. After the transfer is completed, profits and losses will be calculated based on the cost price you entered.</li><li>The cost price cannot be modified once it is entered. If you have any questions, please contact customer service.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 3. Confirm stock transfer details and submit the transfer application.</p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">2. Confirming the account type of Tiger Brokers</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Tiger Brokers has two account types: global accounts and integrated accounts. Their processes for submitting transfer-out requests are different. Please check which account type your stocks are held in.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Log in to Tiger Brokers, click <i>My</i> &gt; <i>Tiger Account</i>, and check the account type.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">If you have an integrated account, click <a href="https://support.longbridge.hk/topics/1npp30q/3dp6swl">here</a> to view relevant instructions.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4n1enoq6httgz7jx_.jpeg"/></figure><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">3. Submitting a transfer-out request to Tiger Brokers (global account, IB)</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Obtain IB login account information from Tiger Brokers. Log in to Tiger Brokers, click <i>My</i> &gt; <i>Tiger Account</i>. The tiger account number in parentheses beside your global account is your username for IB website login.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4n2npev030lmmzwq_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Log in to the IB official website: <a href="https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/cn/home.php">https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/en/home.php</a></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 3. Log in to your IB account. Use the obtained tiger account as the username to log in. If the password is incorrect, you can click <i>Forgot Password</i> or call Tiger Brokers/IB customer service to retrieve it. IB customer service hotline: +86 (021) 60868586.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 4. Obtain IB account information. After you logging in to the IB official website, the account number in the portfolio section is your IB account number (this is required when submitting a transfer-in application at Longbridge).</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 5. Submit instructions for transferring stocks out.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Hong Kong stocks</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(1) Path: <i>Transfer &amp; Pay</i> &gt; <i>Transfer Positions</i> &gt;
