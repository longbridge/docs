<p>To transfer shares in, you'll need to submit a share transfer-in request and have your delivering broker confirm the share transfer-out. Follow the detailed steps below to submit your request and initiate the transfer-out with your broker.</p><h3>Step 1. Submit a stock transfer<strong> </strong>application<strong>.</strong></h3><p>1.1 Go to the Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>Transfer </i>to submit a transfer-in application.  </p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 100%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/0d65b86cc47487a05f7cdfeee76f4ea8"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">If you have already made a deposit, please select <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>More</i>  &gt; <i>Share Transfer-In.</i></p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/61cd5ff80f5787603277e57486a303f3"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">1.2 Please select your sending broker, provide the required broker details, and enter the stock code and quantity. (Currently, we support inbound transfers of HK and US stocks.) Double-check the transfer and stock details, then tap <i>Confirm and Submit</i> to receive sample email instructions at your registered Longbridge email address.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Note: Providing the cost price is optional. <br/>- If it is not filled in, the cost price per share will be calculated based on the closing price on the day when the transfer is completed<br/>- If it is filled in, after the transfer-in is completed, the P/L will be calculated based on the cost price provided. <br/>- The cost price cannot be changed once filled in. If you have any questions, please contact the customer service.</p><h3 style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Submit the stock transfer-out request to your delivering broker.</h3><p>2.1Take BOCI transfer-out as an example: After the information submitted on the Longbridge App is confirmed, the corresponding transfer request instructions will be sent to your registered e-mail address. Please follow the instructions in the e-mail.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/d2c7e21646a1bf9760e6f4f8aaa40712"/></figure><p>2.2 Follow the transfer instructions: Download, print, and sign the attachment (transfer application form). Finally, submit the transfer-out request to BOCI by e-mail with the signed application form.</p><p>2.3 Wait patiently for the email confirmation from BOCI.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Notes:</strong></p><p>After completing the above steps, your request will be confirmed by both parties. Under normal circumstances, the transfer can be completed within 3-5 working days<strong>.</strong></p><p> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclaimer:</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only.
