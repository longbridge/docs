<h2>IPO Subscription Portal</h2><p>You can initiate an IPO subscription in the Longbridge App. <a href="https://longbridge.hk/en/download">Download</a> Longbridge App</p><h2>IPO Subscription Process</h2><p>1. Launch Longbridge App, tap “Market” - “IPOs” either in the middle or at the top of the page to enter the IPO list page.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/02f29536d23127f213cc4d6ac9f25c27"/></figure><p>2. Tap the “Subscribe” button on the corresponding IPO list page or in the IPO calendar to enter the subscription page.</p><p>There are different subscription types according to the asset holdings at the time of application for subscription: as shown in ① and ②. You can select the number of shares you would like to subscribe.</p><p><strong>① Financing</strong></p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/97e0c010a0593c1e291863817636bae5"/></figure><p><strong>② Standard</strong></p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/79a9fe018ce0fa45b2c06521571947c5"/></figure><p>3. Tap “Confirm” at the bottom right corner to confirm and submit your IPO subscription order.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/4a8d9aec4547705d2e93b4ec2341c02f"/></figure><h2>View Subscription History</h2><p>You can tap “Orders” on the IPO page or tap “View Order” in the IPO calendar to view your subscription orders.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/28547877f09919a31ea3d2cf34672d0a"/></figure>
