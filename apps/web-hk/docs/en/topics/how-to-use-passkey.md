<h2>What is a passkey?</h2><p>A passkey is a password-free way to log in to the Longbridge app. Once added, you can verify your identity at login, or in other high-risk verification scenarios, by unlocking your device with Face ID, fingerprint or your device passcode. No password needed.</p><ul><li><strong>Log in without a password:</strong> face or fingerprint recognition replaces your password for faster verification.</li><li><strong>Use it across devices:</strong> a passkey can be used for quick login verification on your other devices.</li><li><strong>Stronger account security:</strong> your passkey is never sent over the network, which helps protect you against phishing.</li></ul><h2>How to create and manage a passkey</h2><h3>Before you start</h3><figure class="table"><table><thead><tr><th>Item</th><th>Requirement</th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Longbridge app version</td><td>5.4.0 or later</td></tr><tr><td>Operating system</td><td>iOS 16 or later, Android 9 or later, macOS 13 or later, Windows 11</td></tr><tr><td>Device settings</td><td>Screen lock turned on (passcode, fingerprint or face)</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><blockquote><p>HarmonyOS devices do not support passkeys yet. iPhone users should first turn on <strong>iCloud Keychain and AutoFill</strong> in system settings.</p></blockquote><h3>Steps</h3><p><strong>Step 1:</strong> Open the Longbridge app and go to <strong>Settings › Account security</strong>.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 35%"><img src="https://assets.lbkrs.com/uploads/1_19104_mwkgqhyr284kmjkofca_.jpeg"/><figcaption>Settings › Account security</figcaption></figure><p><strong>Step 2:</strong> Under Password, find <strong>Passkey</strong> and tap “Add” on the right.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 35%"><img src="https://assets.lbkrs.com/uploads/1_19104_mwkgqhz22sd8ps5drea_.jpeg"/><figcaption>Passkey › Add</figcaption></figure><p><strong>Step 3:</strong> On the Passkey page, tap <strong>Add passkey</strong>.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 35%"><img src="https://assets.lbkrs.com/uploads/1_19104_mwkgqhzbj7p07lrnp6_.jpeg"/><figcaption>Tap “Add passkey”</figcaption></figure><p><strong>Step 4:</strong> When your device asks whether to save the passkey, tap the blue button to save it and confirm with Face ID or Touch ID. To save it on another device, choose the option to save on another device.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 35%"><img src="https://assets.lbkrs.com/uploads/1_19104_mwkg9edmxo79rcgych9_.jpeg" alt="Save the passkey and verify"/><figcaption>Save the passkey and verify</figcaption></figure><p><strong>Step 5:</strong> Once added, the passkey appears in your passkey list with its update time and device model. Tap the ✎ icon to rename it so you can tell which device it belongs to.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 35%"><img src="https://assets.lbkrs.com/uploads/1_19104_mwkgqhzihllm49lr2n_.jpeg" alt="Passkey added. Rename or delete it here."/><figcaption>Passkey added. Rename or delete it here.</figcaption></figure><h3>Delete a passkey</h3><p>In the passkey list, tap the delete icon at the top right of the passkey. A deleted passkey stops working immediately and can no longer be used to log in to your Longbridge account. The matching entry on your device is not removed automatically. On iOS 18 or later, you can delete it manually in the Passwords app.</p><h3>Storage and sync by platform</h3><h4>iPhone / iPad</h4><ul><li>Verify with Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode.</li><li>Passkeys are saved to iCloud Keychain by default and work on your other devices signed in with the same Apple ID. On iOS 18 or later, you can view and manage them in the Passwords app.</li><li>You can also scan a QR code shown on another device, use a USB or NFC security key, or save the passkey in a third-party password manager.</li></ul><h4>Android</h4><ul><li>Verify with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.</li><li>If you are signed in to Google with sync turned on, passkeys are saved to Google Password Manager and work on devices using the same Google account.</li><li>Without Google sync, the passkey stays on this phone only and must be created again on a new phone. Some Android models support local storage only.</li></ul><h4>Mac</h4><ul><li>Verify with Touch ID or your Mac login password.</li><li>Passkeys sync through iCloud Keychain. Turn on Passwords &amp; Keychain sync under iCloud in System Settings.</li></ul><h4>Windows</h4><ul><li>Verify with Windows Hello (face, fingerprint or PIN).</li><li>Passkeys can be stored on this PC or synced to your Microsoft account.</li></ul><h2>How to log in or verify with a passkey</h2><p>Your account must already have a passkey, and your device and operating system must support passkeys.</p><h3>Option 1: Log in with a passkey directly</h3><p>On the login page, choose to log in with a passkey. Your device finds the passkey saved locally or in the cloud. Verify with your face or fingerprint and you are logged in, with no need to enter your account. If this device has no passkey, you can scan a QR code on another device or use a security key.</p><h3>Option 2: Enter your account first</h3><p>Enter your Longbridge account, phone number or email and tap Next. If the account has a passkey, you will be asked to verify with it first. The remaining steps are the same as Option 1.</p><blockquote><p>When you log in on a new device for the first time, you may still need to complete a one-time device verification to confirm it is you.</p></blockquote><h2>What if my passkey is lost or not working?</h2><ol><li>Close the passkey prompt and log in with your password or an SMS code instead.</li><li>If you are on a new device, follow the prompts to complete device verification.</li><li>After logging in, go to <strong>Settings › Account security › Passkey</strong>, delete the passkey that no longer works, and add a new one.</li></ol><p>If you think your device is lost or someone else has used it, delete that device’s passkey right away and change your login password. Contact Customer Service if you need help.</p><h2>FAQ</h2><h3>Why can’t I add a passkey?</h3><ul><li>Your device has no screen lock (passcode, fingerprint or face).</li><li>The third-party password manager you chose does not support passkeys.</li><li>Your device model or operating system is too old. See the requirements above.</li><li>Your Longbridge app is out of date. Version 5.4.0 or later is required.</li></ul><h3>I switched phones. Why doesn’t my old passkey work?</h3><p>To use a passkey across devices, turn on your system’s password sync: iCloud Keychain on iPhone, or Google Password Manager on Google Pixel and stock Android. Other brands put this setting in different places. Some Android models support local storage only, so you will need to create a new passkey on the new phone.</p><h3>Why don’t I see a passkey option when I log in?</h3><p>No passkey has been added for this account or device, or the passkey was deleted. Log in another way first, then add one in Settings.</p><h3>My USB security key asks me to set a PIN. What should I do?</h3><ul><li>Use the management software from your security key’s maker.</li><li>Some browsers or desktop apps ask you to set a PIN when adding a passkey, but you cannot change it there later.</li><li>In Google Chrome, go to Settings › Privacy and security › Security › Manage security keys to create or change a PIN.</li></ul><h3>Why does passkey verification fail?</h3><ul><li>This device has no passkey saved for this account.</li><li>The device was reset or the passkey was deleted from the system, so it no longer works.</li><li>The passkey was deleted in the Longbridge app, but an old entry is still on your device.</li><li>You picked a passkey that belongs to a different account.</li><li>A temporary network or service issue.</li></ul><p>In most cases, log in with your password or an SMS code, delete the old passkey, and add a new one.</p><h3>Can I still use my password after turning on passkeys?</h3><p>Yes. A passkey is an extra way to log in. Your login password and trading password stay the same, and you can log in with your password at any time.</p>
