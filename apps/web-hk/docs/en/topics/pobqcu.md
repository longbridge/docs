<p>Structured products mainly face the following risks: market risk arising from the fluctuation of the underlying benchmark, principal loss risk (for non-capital-protected products), liquidity risk where early redemption  is not possible during the product term, issuer credit risk, and understanding bias risk caused by complex product terms. In addition, for products denominated in foreign currencies, there is also exchange rate fluctuation risk that may affect the actual income.</p>
