<p>Faster Payment System (FPS) is a rapid payment system launched by the Hong Kong Monetary Authority (HKMA). It features instant interbank transfers and is mostly fee-free. Below is a guide on how to deposit funds into Longbridge App via FPS.</p><h2><strong>Process of deposits via FPS</strong></h2><p>Step 1. Go to the <strong>Longbridge App</strong> &gt; <i><strong>Portfolio</strong> &gt; <strong>Deposit</strong> &gt; <strong>FPS</strong></i>, and copy the FPS ID.</p><figure class="table"><table><thead><tr><th><strong>Longbridge FPS ID</strong></th><th><strong>169152691</strong></th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Recipient bank</td><td>Industrial and Commercial Bank of China (Asia)</td></tr><tr><td>Recipient name</td><td>LONG BRIDGE HK LIMITED-CLIENT'S A/C</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/a11630e6b2ce871c3d6c13d8f8e0d48f"/></figure><p>Step 2. Go to the <strong>ZA Bank App</strong> &gt; <i><strong>Transfer</strong></i> &gt; <i><strong>FPS ID</strong></i>.</p><p>Step 3. Enter Longbridge FPS ID 169152691, enable the <i>Register Payee</i> option, and click <i>Confirm.</i> In the next page, enter the transfer amount, complete swipe verification, and click <i>Submit</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/e59cc88e5cbad84c0adb9b6e66560a03"/></figure><p>Step 4. After successful submission, complete the transfer.</p><p>Step 5. Go to the <strong>Longbridge App</strong> &gt; <i><strong>Portfolio</strong></i> &gt; <i><strong>Deposit</strong></i>, select the deposit currency and bank information, choose <i>FPS</i>, and upload the <strong>proof of remittance</strong>.</p><blockquote><p>Note: Please upload the proof of remittance and transfer amount immediately after completing the transfer to avoid delays in deposit processing.</p></blockquote><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/9c6fadd6e4523da497b87ede46bad92b"/></figure><h2><strong>Notes</strong></h2><p><strong>1.Arrival time:</strong></p><p>FPS currently only supports HKD deposits. The arrival time is as follows:</p><figure class="table"><table><thead><tr><th><strong>Deposit initiation time</strong></th><th><strong>Expected arrival time</strong></th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td>Hong Kong stock trading days (Monday 09:00–Friday 23:59)</td><td>Within 2 hours</td></tr><tr><td>Other periods</td><td>Before 09:40 on the next Hong Kong stock trading day</td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p><strong>2.Transfer fee:</strong></p><p>Most banks and stored value payment tools currently do not charge fees, but some institutions may impose charges. Fees are subject to change, so please consult your bank for details. Longbridge does not charge any fees.</p><p><strong>3.Transfer limit</strong>: Longbridge does not impose any limits for deposits via FPS. Please note whether your bank has any transfer limits.</p><h2><strong>Notes</strong></h2><ol><li>Currently, Longbridge only supports <strong>HKD deposits via FPS</strong>.</li><li>The bank account name used for transfer shall be the same as the Longbridge account name. You cannot use other people's bank accounts (or joint accounts) for transfers; otherwise, the refund fees incurred will be borne by you.</li><li>After you complete the transfer, the funds will be instantly transferred to the Longbridge recipient account via FPS. After approval, the funds will be deposited into your Longbridge account as soon as possible.</li><li>It takes time for the bank to process the transfer. Bank notifications stating &#34;transfer initiated&#34; do not indicate immediate receipt by Longbridge. Settlement and approval are required after the funds arrive in Longbridge.
