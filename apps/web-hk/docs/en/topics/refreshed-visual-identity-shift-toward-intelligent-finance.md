<p>Longbridge Securities today announced the launch of a refreshed visual identity, featuring Olo Cyan— a color that becomes perceptible through cutting-edge scientific exploration of human vision beyond its natural limits. Just as Olo symbolizes technology’s power to expand what’s possible, this new visual identity reflects Longbridge’s ambition to move beyond the boundaries of traditional finance and build the next generation of intelligent investing experiences.</p><p>This marks a new chapter in the company’s evolution as it progresses past the realm of online finance towards pioneering intelligent financial systems.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 100%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/5de05221466b0ad31a7caccab0a0ccf6"/></figure><h2>What This Means for Users</h2><p>The refreshed visual identity will roll out progressively across Longbridge Securities’ website and mobile applications from 6 February.<strong> </strong>All updates will take effect automatically, and no action is required from users to experience the new interface and design.</p><p>The rebrand follows a series of major technological milestones, including the launch of the world’s first pre-market US options trading capability and the upcoming rollout of AI-powered investment intelligence. Together, these innovations reflect a shift from execution-focused tools to intelligence-driven support and from a standalone platform to a connected, global financial ecosystem.</p><p>The rebrand is visual in nature and does not affect users’ accounts or access to the platform. All accounts, features, and data will remain unchanged. Trading functionality and platform reliability also remain the same.</p><h2>A Strategic Evolution Reflected Visually</h2><p>This new visual chapter is designed to mirror three core shifts in Longbridge’s product and technology direction. Longbridge is growing, moving beyond execution to enhancement, where AI elevates every decision. The company is also turning new ideas into tangible market advantages, and connecting markets, data, and intelligence into a seamless, adaptive financial environment.</p><p>The refreshed palette includes Olo Cyan, representing innovation, Signal Orange, representing execution and Horizon Yellow, representing insight.</p><p>Longbridge Securities has also updated its logo to include Olo, to appear more dynamic and forward-leaning, while retaining the bridge motif that has represented connection, access, and opportunity across global markets. This refreshed palette is in line with Longbridge's priorities of ensuring that the platform is clear and easy to use, allowing users to interact with confidence and trust.</p><figure class="image image_resized" style="width: 100%"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2026/61cb57805a7b0369a87e4557d8443881"/></figure><h2>Continuing to Build What’s Next in Finance</h2><p>Longbridge's recent product launches and refreshed brand identity reflect its broader vision of redefining what is possible in investing. By extending U.S. options trading into the pre-market session and introducing AI-driven tools that make institutional-grade insights more accessible, the company continues to remove traditional barriers related to time, information, and complexity.</p><p>Through ongoing technological innovation, Longbridge Securities remains focused on enhancing investment efficiency and user experience while supporting investors across global markets.</p>
