<p style="margin-left: 0cm">Below is a detailed introduction and example regarding margin calls.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">When the equity in your account falls below the maintenance margin (MM) level, the company will issue a margin call notice. You must make up the shortfall within 3 trading days upon receiving the notice (including the date of receipt). <strong>The margin call amount will be higher than the difference between the MM and the equity.</strong></p><h4 style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Example: margin call case</strong></h4><p style="margin-left: 0cm">A client deposits USD 10,000 in cash and USD 5,000 worth of Stock A (initial margin (IM): 30%, MM: 25%), and purchases USD 25,000 worth of Stock B (IM: 50%, MM: 45%). Suppose the market value of Stock B drops to USD 19,500.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">After the purchase and the decline in the price of Stock B, the client's holdings are as follows:</p><figure class="table"><table><thead><tr><th><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: center"><strong>Cash deficit</strong></p></th><th><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: center"><strong>HKD 15,000</strong></p></th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Holdings IM</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">HKD 11,250 (HKD 5,000 X 30% + HKD 19,500 X 50%)</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Holdings MM</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">HKD 10,025 (HKD 5,000 X 25% + HKD 19,500 X 45%)</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Equity in account</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">HKD 9,500</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Margin call*</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">HKD 1,750</p></td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">The client must ensure that the equity in their account meets the margin requirement by the specified date.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">If the client chooses to sell securities, the required amount to be sold is no more than the difference between the IM and the equity, divided by the IM ratio.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>If the equity falls below the forced liquidation margin, the shortfall must be made up immediately. Otherwise, the positions may be subject to immediate forced liquidation.</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Note: The actual amount of margin call may be higher, depending on the nature of the stocks and the company's policies.</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Key takeaways</strong>:</p><ul><li>Triggering condition: The value of equity falls below the MM level.</li><li>Margin call amount: The margin call amount is generally higher than the difference between the MM and equity. **</li><li>Processing deadline: You must make up the shortfall or reduce your position within 3 trading days upon receiving the notice.</li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><i>This article is for reference only and does not constitute any investment advice.</i></p>
