<p style="margin-left: 0cm">The process of transferring stocks from Interactive Brokers (IB) to Longbridge mainly involves two steps: submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge and submitting a transfer-out request to IB (the processes of Hong Kong and US stock transfer-out are different). Below are the detailed descriptions for these two steps.</p><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">1. Submitting a transfer-in request to Longbridge</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Go to the Longbridge App &gt; <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>Transfer</i> to submit a transfer-in application. If you have already deposited funds, select <i>Portfolio</i> &gt; <i>More</i> &gt; <i>Share Transfer-In</i>.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4ndaz2s6huyk6rvie_.jpeg"/></figure><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4nd5f6wjwnuz8g4c_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Select IB as the transfer-out broker, enter the account holder's name and account number, click <i>Next</i>, and fill in the stock information.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Please note: Longbridge now supports entering the cost price per share (optional).</p><ul><li>If not entered: The cost price per share will be calculated based on the closing price on the day the transfer is completed.</li><li>If you need to enter: Please enter the cost price you actually want to display. After the transfer is completed, profits and losses will be calculated based on the cost price you entered.</li><li>The cost price cannot be modified once it is entered. If you have any questions, please contact customer service.</li></ul><h2 style="margin-left: 0cm">2. Submitting a transfer-out request to IB</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>Hong Kong stock transfer-out</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Note: This operation process only applies to personal accounts under IB. If you are a user of Snowball X, you can also follow this process; if you are a user of Tiger Brokers, you are advised to contact the customer service of Tiger Brokers to consult and confirm the transfer process.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Log in to the IB website: <a href="https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/cn/home.php">https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/en/home.php</a></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. Submit instructions for transferring stocks out.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(1) Path: <i>Transfer &amp; Pay</i> &gt; <i>Transfer Positions</i> &gt; <i>Transfer Out</i> &gt; <i>Basic FOP Transfer</i></p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4ngpqlkh3l9blxmu8_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(2) Broker information</p><figure class="table"><table><thead><tr><th><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: center"><strong>Financial institution</strong></p></th><th><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: center"><strong>Long Bridge HK Limited</strong></p></th></tr></thead><tbody><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Your or your broker's account number at the above financial institution:</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">B02195+ your Longbridge account number (starting with H), such as B02195+H1234567</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Account name</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Your account name (in English)</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Contact:</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Settlement Team</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Phone</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">(+852) 3585 8944 / (+852) 3585 8915</p></td></tr><tr><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Email</p></td><td><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><a href="mailto:settlement@longbridge.hk">settlement@longbridge.hk</a></p></td></tr></tbody></table></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">For details, see <a href="https://support.longbridgehk.com/topics/1qawjoc/47azunp">Transfer In Stock Details</a>.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">After completing the above process, please wait patiently. The two brokers will contact each other, and once everything is confirmed, the stock transfer process will begin.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Note: The required confirmation information shall be subject to the actual display. The information in the guide above is for illustrative purposes only.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm"><strong>US stock transfer-out</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Note: This operation process only applies to personal accounts under IB. If you are a user of Snowball X, you can also follow this process; if you are a user of Tiger Brokers, you are advised to contact the customer service of Tiger Brokers to consult and confirm the transfer process.</p><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 1. Log in to the IB website: <a href="https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/cn/home.php">https://www.interactivebrokers.com.hk/en/home.php</a></p><figure class="image"><img src="https://assets.lbctrl.com/uploads/1_20114_mh4nhicxqj9xxbros0q_.jpeg"/></figure><p style="margin-left: 0cm">Step 2. The two brokers will contact each other, and once everything is confirmed, the stock transfer process will begin. Once the other broker transfers the stocks out, Longbridge will deposit them into your account within 1–2 working days.</li><li>IB's processes for Hong Kong and US stock transfer-out are different. Please pay attention to the distinction. This operation process only applies to personal accounts under IB.
