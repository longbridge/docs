<p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Common types of funds include money market funds, bond funds, stock funds, commodity funds, and balanced (hybrid) funds. Each type differs significantly in investment scope, risk-return characteristics, and liquidity. Below is a detailed introduction.</p><h2>1. Types of funds</h2><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Funds can be broadly categorized into money market funds, bond funds, stock funds, commodity funds, and balanced (hybrid) funds based on the primary asset classes they invest in.</p><figure class="image"><img src="https://pub.pbkrs.com/uploads/2025/9df3c77b3af4c6274703e55d89abaac6"/></figure><h2>2. Investment scope and characteristics of different types</h2><h3>Money Market Funds</h3><ul><li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Investment scope</strong></li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Mainly invest in short-term monetary instruments such as bank deposits, certificates of deposit, short-term government bonds, treasury bills, commercial paper, corporate bonds, and other short-term securities with high safety and stable returns.</p><ul><li><strong>Characteristics：</strong>Suitable for risk-averse investors or managing short-term idle liquid assets.<ul><li>Safety: Generally lower risk compared to other types of funds.</li><li>Profitability: Generally lower returns compared to other types of funds.</li><li>Liquidity: Short subscription and redemption time, convenient for fund management.</li></ul></li></ul><h3>Bond Funds</h3><ul><li><strong>Investment scope</strong></li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Mainly invest in bonds, such as government bonds, financial bonds, corporate bonds, and other fixed-income products or debt instruments. Some bond funds may allocate a small portion to stocks.</p><ul><li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Characteristics</strong></li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Generally higher risk than money market funds but lower than other types of funds. Returns are moderate but subject to fluctuations.</p><h3>Stock Funds</h3><ul><li><strong>Investment scope</strong></li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Primarily invest in stocks. Based on the scope of stock investments, they can be further classified into sub-types such as country-specific stock funds, industry-specific stock funds, or style-specific stock funds (e.g., value and growth).</p><ul><li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Characteristics</strong></li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">High volatility, high risk, and potentially higher returns or losses compared to money market and bond funds.</p><h3>Commodity Funds</h3><ul><li><strong>Investment scope</strong></li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Primarily invest in commodities, including but not limited to commodity-related listed companies and commodity spot and futures. Common types of commodities include precious metals (e.g., gold and silver), crude oil, and agricultural products.</p><ul><li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Characteristics</strong></li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Higher risk, with returns closely related to the price trends of commodities.</p><h3>Balanced (Hybrid) Funds</h3><ul><li><strong>Investment scope</strong></li></ul><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify">Flexible investment scope, including stocks, bonds, money market instruments, and other financial products.</p><ul><li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Characteristics</strong></li></ul><p style="text-align: justify"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><strong>Key takeaways:</strong></p><ul><li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Money market funds</strong>: Low risk, low return. Invest in short-term deposits and bills. High liquidity. Suitable for conservative investors and short-term fund placement.</li><li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Bond funds</strong>: Low to medium risk, moderate return. Primarily invest in government and corporate bonds. Relatively stable returns with some volatility.</li><li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Stock funds</strong>: High risk, high return. Mainly invest in stocks (may target specific markets or industries). High volatility. Suitable for aggressive investors.</li><li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Commodity funds</strong>: High risk. Invest in commodities like gold and crude oil. High price volatility. Suitable for hedging or speculative purposes.</li><li style="text-align: justify"><strong>Balanced (hybrid) funds:</strong> Flexibly invest in stocks, bonds, and money market instruments. Aim to balance returns and risks.</li></ul><p style="text-align: justify"> </p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><strong>Disclosures</strong></p><p style="margin-left: 0cm; text-align: justify"><i>The market involves risks, and investing requires caution. The above introduction is intended to help you understand the potential characteristics of fund products and does not constitute investment advice. Before making any investment decisions, you should carefully review the information and relevant documents provided by fund sponsors and products to assess whether you are suitable for investing in such fund products and your ability to bear risks.</i></p>
