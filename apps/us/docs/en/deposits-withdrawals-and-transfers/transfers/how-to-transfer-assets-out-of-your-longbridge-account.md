The _Transfer Assets Out_ feature allows you to move eligible positions from your Longbridge account to an account at another brokerage firm. Because this is a brokerage-to-brokerage transfer, your cost basis is preserved.

**Before You Begin**

Before initiating a transfer-out, make sure:

-   Your Longbridge account has no restrictions that would block an outgoing transfer.

-   Your Longbridge account has sufficient cash balance to cover the USD 75 transfer fee.

-   The receiving brokerage account is held under the same name as your Longbridge account.

-   The positions you want to transfer are settled and not subject to pending trades.

-   The positions you want to transfer are eligible to be held at the receiving brokerage, as ineligible positions may cause the transfer to be rejected or returned, resulting in additional fees.

**How to Submit a Transfer-Out Request**

Transfers must be initiated from your receiving brokerage. Please log in to your receiving brokerage's platform and submit the transfer request there.

**Tracking Your Transfer**

To check the status of your transfer-out request:

1.  Go to the _Transfer_ page and tap the arrow next to _Transfer history_.

2.  Filter by _Transfer-out_ in the _Type & Status_ dropdown if needed.

3.  Tap any record to open the _Transfer-out Details_ page.

**FAQs**

Q1: Do I need to contact the receiving brokerage to start the transfer?

Yes. You should initiate the transfer request from your receiving brokerage firm.
