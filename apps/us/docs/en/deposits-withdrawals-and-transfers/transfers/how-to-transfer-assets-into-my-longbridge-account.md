The 「Transfer Assets In」 feature lets you move eligible stocks, ETFs, and USD cash from an account at another brokerage firm directly into your Longbridge account. Your cost basis is preserved throughout the transfer.

> **Note** 
>
> Transfers are processed through the DTCC system and coordinated between both brokerages. Processing typically takes up to 7 business days.

## **Before You Begin —the Eligibility Checklist**

To ensure a smooth transfer, confirm the following before submitting your request:

**Assets:**

-   No pending trades in the sending account

-   No pending deposits or withdrawals in the sending account

-   Only eligible stocks, ETFs, and cash can be transferred — options positions are not eligible for transfer

**Account:**

-   Your SSN or ITIN must match on both accounts

-   The account owner name must match on both accounts

-   Account types must be the same (e.g., both individual taxable accounts)

-   No transfer restrictions on either account

> **Important** 
>
> Options positions cannot be transferred. However, you can place new equity option trades with Longbridge after your transfer is complete.

## **How to Submit a Transfer-In Request**

**Step 1 — Open the Transfer page**

1.  Tap 「Transfer」 on the home screen.

2.  Tap 「Transfer assets」.

3.  On the 「What would you like to do?」 screen, tap 「Transfer assets in」.

**Step 2 — Enter your sending brokerage information**

On the 「Where are you transferring from?」 screen, fill in the details of the account you are transferring from:

-   Brokerage name — tap to select from the list. If your brokerage is not listed, select Other and type the name and DTC number manually.

-   Account number — enter your account number at the sending brokerage (letters, numbers, and hyphens only; up to 30 characters).

Tap 「Next」 once all fields are filled in and validated.

> **Note**  If a brokerage is shown as Ineligible in the list, it is currently not supported for transfer-in. 

**Step 3 — Confirm the transfer requirements**

Review the checklist on the Transfer into Longbridge screen. Check both the Assets and Account boxes to confirm you have read and verified the requirements, then tap 「Next」.

A pop-up will ask you to choose between two transfer types:

-   All assets — transfer your entire account balance (all eligible stocks, ETFs, and cash)

-   Partial assets — select specific stocks, ETFs, and/or cash to transfer

**Step 4A — All Assets Transfer**

If you select 「All assets」, you will go directly to the confirmation screen. Review the details and tap 「Confirm & submit」.

**Step 4B — Partial Assets Transfer**

If you select 「Partial assets」, you will see the 「Which assets would you like to transfer?」 screen. Add what you want to transfer:

**To add cash:**

1.  Tap 「+ Enter amount」 under the Cash section.

2.  Enter the USD amount you want to transfer and tap 「Next」.

**To add stocks or ETFs:**

1.  Tap 「+ Add a stock or ETF」 under the Stocks & ETFs section.

2.  Search by name or ticker symbol and select the stock or ETF you want.

3.  Enter the number of shares (whole numbers only) and tap 「Add」.

4.  Repeat to add more positions.

> **Note**
>
> You must add at least one stock or ETF to proceed with a partial transfer. A cash-only partial transfer is not allowed. You can transfer cash alongside stocks and ETFs at the same request.

When you have added all the assets you want to transfer, tap 「Next」 to go to the confirmation screen.

**Step 5 — Review and confirm**

The Confirm Your Transfer screen shows:

-   Sending brokerage name and account number

-   Transfer type (All assets or Partial assets)

-   For partial transfers: all selected assets — cash amount, then each stock/ETF with ticker and share count

Read the notices at the bottom of the screen carefully:

-   Your sending brokerage firm may charge fees. Make sure you have sufficient funds to complete the transfer.

-   Transfer is limited to eligible stocks, eligible ETFs, and cash. For questions, contact support@longbridge.com.

When everything looks correct, tap 「Confirm & submit」.

After submission, you will see a success screen showing the Transfer initiated date and an estimated completion date (up to 7 business days later). Tap 「OK」 to return to the 「Transfer」 page.

## **Tracking Your Transfer**

To check the status of your transfer-in request:

1.  Go to 「Transfer」 → tap the arrow next to Transfer history.

2.  Filter by 「Transfer-in」 in the 「Type & Status」 dropdown if needed.

3.  Tap any record to open the 「Transfer-in Details」 page, which shows the real-time progress timeline and all asset details.

## **Canceling a Transfer-In**

You can cancel a transfer-in only while it is in Initiated status — before the request has been picked up for processing.

1.  Open the 「Transfer-in Details」 page for the request you want to cancel.

2.  Tap 「Cancel transfer」 at the bottom.

3.  Tap 「Yes, cancel」 to confirm. The request will be withdrawn and the page will refresh.

> **Note** 
>
> Once the transfer moves to In progress or beyond, cancellation is no longer available. If you attempt to cancel and the status has already changed, you will see a message explaining why it cannot be canceled.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

How long does a transfer-in take?

Transfers typically take up to 7 business days. The timeline depends on both brokerages and the DTCC clearing process. You can track progress in your 「Transfer history」.

Will my cost basis be preserved?

Yes. One of the key advantages of a brokerage transfer (versus selling and re-buying) is that your original cost basis travels with your positions to Longbridge.

Can I transfer options positions?

No. Options positions are not eligible for transfer-in. Only stocks, ETFs, and USD cash can be transferred.

My brokerage is marked as Ineligible — what does this mean?

This means the brokerage is temporarily not supported for transfer-in. 

Can I submit a partial transfer with cash only?

No. A partial transfer must include at least one stock or ETF. You may include cash alongside your positions in the same request, but cash alone is not sufficient.

My transfer is partially completed — what happened?

Some of your requested positions were transferred successfully, while others could not be (for example, a stock may have been sold or become ineligible in the interim).
