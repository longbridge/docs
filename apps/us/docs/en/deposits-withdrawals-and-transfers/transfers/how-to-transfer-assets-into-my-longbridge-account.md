The free _Transfer Assets In_ feature lets you move eligible stocks, ETFs, and cash from an account at another brokerage firm directly into your Longbridge account. Cost basis information for eligible securities is generally transferred from the delivering brokerage when available.

**Before You Begin — Eligibility Checklist**

To ensure a smooth transfer, confirm the following before submitting your request:

**Assets:**

-   No pending trades in the sending account
-   No pending deposits or withdrawals in the sending account
-   Only eligible stocks, ETFs, and cash can be transferred — options positions are not eligible for transfer

**Account:**

-   Your SSN or ITIN must match on both accounts
-   The account owner name must match on both accounts
-   Account types must be the same (e.g., both are individual taxable accounts)
-   No transfer restrictions on either account

**How to Submit a Transfer-In Request**

**Step 1 — Open the** _**Transfer**_ **page**

1.  Tap _Transfer_ on the _Portfolio_ screen.
2.  Tap _Transfer assets_.
3.  On the _What would you like to do?_ screen, tap _Transfer assets in_.

**Step 2 — Enter your sending brokerage information**

On the _Where are you transferring from?_ screen, fill in the details of the account you are transferring from:

-   Brokerage name — tap to select from the list. If your brokerage is not listed, select _Other_ and enter the brokerage name and DTC number manually.
-   Account number — enter your account number at the sending brokerage (letters, numbers, and hyphens only; up to 30 characters).

Tap _Next_ once all fields are filled in and validated.

**Step 3 — Confirm the transfer requirements**

Review the checklist on the _Transfer into Longbridge_ screen. Check both the _Assets_ and _Account_ boxes to confirm you have read and verified the requirements, and then tap _Next_.

You will then be prompted to choose between two transfer types:

-   All assets — transfer your entire account balance (all eligible stocks, ETFs, and cash)
-   Partial assets — select specific stocks, ETFs, and/or cash to transfer

**Step 4A — Transfer all assets**

If you select _All assets,_ you will go directly to the confirmation screen. Review the details and tap _Confirm & submit._

**Step 4B — Transfer partial assets**

If you select _Partial assets_, you'll be taken to the _Which assets would you like to transfer?_ screen. From here, add the assets you'd like to transfer:

**To add cash:**

4.  Tap _\+ Enter amount_ under the _Cash_ section.
5.  Enter the USD amount you want to transfer and tap _Next_.

**To add stocks or ETFs:**

6.  Tap _\+ Add a stock or ETF_ under the _Stocks & ETFs_ section.
7.  Search by name or ticker symbol and select the stock or ETF you want.
8.  Enter the number of shares (whole numbers only) and tap _Add_.
9.  Repeat to add more positions.

When you have added all the assets you want to transfer, tap _Next_ to go to the confirmation screen.

**Step 5 — Review and confirm**

The _Confirm Your Transfer_ screen shows:

-   Sending brokerage name and account number
-   Transfer type (All assets or Partial assets)
-   For partial asset transfers: all selected assets — cash amount first, followed by each stock or ETF with its ticker symbol and share count

Read the notices at the bottom of the screen carefully:

-   Your sending brokerage firm may charge a fee for outgoing transfers. Make sure you have sufficient funds to cover any such charges.
-   Transfers are limited to eligible stocks, ETFs, and cash. For questions, contact support@longbridge.com, or reach out to live support in the app.

When everything looks correct, tap _Confirm & submit_.

After submission, you will see a success screen showing the transfer initiation date and an estimated completion date (up to 7 business days). Tap _OK_ to return to the _Transfer_ page.

**Tracking Your Transfer**

To check the status of your transfer-in request:

10.  Go to the _Transfer_ page and tap the arrow next to _Transfer history_.
11.  Filter by _Transfer-in_ in the _Type & Status_ dropdown if needed.
12.  Tap any record to open the _Transfer-in Details_ page, which shows the transfer progress timeline and all asset details.

**Canceling a Transfer-In**

You can cancel a transfer-in only while it is in the Initiated status — that is, before it has been picked up for processing.

13.  Open the _Transfer-in Details_ page for the request you want to cancel.
14.  Tap _Cancel transfer_ at the bottom.
15.  Tap _Yes, cancel_ to confirm. The request will be withdrawn and the page will refresh.

**FAQs**

**Q1: How long does a transfer-in take?**

Transfers typically take up to 7 business days, depending on the processing timelines of both brokerage firms and the DTCC clearing process. You can monitor progress under _Transfer history_.

**Q2: Will my cost basis information transfer?**

Cost basis information for eligible securities is generally transferred from the delivering brokerage. In some cases, cost basis information may arrive separately from the transferred securities or may require additional processing.

**Q3: Can I transfer options positions?**

No. Options positions are not eligible for transfer-in. Only stocks, ETFs, and USD cash can be transferred.

**Q4: My brokerage is marked as Ineligible — what does this mean?**

This means the brokerage is currently not supported for transfer-in.

**Q5: Can I submit a partial asset transfer with cash only?**

No. A partial asset transfer must include at least one stock or ETF. You may include cash alongside your positions in the same request, but cash alone does not qualify as a partial asset transfer.

**Q6: My transfer is partially completed — what happened?**

Some of your requested positions were transferred successfully, while others were not (for example, a position may have been sold or become ineligible in the interim).
