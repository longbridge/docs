**How to Submit a Withdrawal**

1.  Tap _Transfers_ on the _Portfolio_ screen, and then tap _Withdraw_.
2.  On the _How would you like to withdraw?_ screen, choose _ACH_ or _Wire_. The details for each method, such as the daily limit and fees, are shown under each option.
3.  Enter the amount you want to withdraw.
4.  Check the _To_ field below the amount. This shows the destination bank account. Tap it to open the account selector and choose a different bank if needed.

**Which bank accounts you'll see**

The account selector shows different accounts depending on the withdrawal method you chose:

-   **ACH:** Only bank accounts that are verified for ACH transfers.
-   **Wire:** All of your linked bank accounts.

To link a new bank account, tap _Add an account_ in the account selector.

1.  Tap _Next_ once the amount is valid and passes all checks.
2.  Review the confirmation screen. It shows your withdrawal amount (displayed as a negative figure), the destination bank, and any applicable wire transfer fee.
3.  Tap _Withdraw now_ to submit.

After submitting, you will see a success screen showing:

-   The withdrawal amount
-   The withdrawal initiation date
-   An estimated completion note (ACH: 1–3 business days; Wire: up to 1 business day)

Tap _OK_ to return to the _Transfers_ page.

**Understanding Withdrawal Limits**

**Available to withdraw**

This is the maximum you can currently withdraw, based on your available account balance. It is shown below the amount input on the withdrawal screen.

**ACH daily limit**

ACH withdrawals are subject to a daily limit of USD 50,000. The maximum amount you can withdraw is the lesser of:

-   Your available balance, and
-   USD 50,000 minus the total amount of ACH withdrawals submitted today with a status of _Initiated_, _In Progress_, or _Completed_.

Withdrawals submitted on non-working days count toward the next working day's limit. The limit resets at 15:30 ET on each working day.

To see a breakdown, tap the ⓘ icon next to _You can withdraw up to_ on the withdrawal screen.

**Minimum withdrawal amount**

A minimum withdrawal amount may apply, depending on your account configuration. If your entered amount is too low, the screen will show: You'll need to withdraw at least $XX.XX (this includes any applicable bank fee).

**Wire Withdrawals That Require Manual Review**

If you submit a wire withdrawal and Longbridge detects that within the past 60 days your account received an ACH deposit from a different bank account, the withdrawal will be flagged for manual review.

In this case, the confirmation screen will show:

-   This withdrawal requires manual review.

After submission, the success screen will display:

-   This withdrawal requires manual review. Once approved, the wire transfer will be processed within approximately 30 minutes.

**Canceling a Withdrawal**

You can cancel a withdrawal only while it is in the _Initiated_ status (the earliest stage, before it has been sent for processing).

4.  Go to _Transfers_ > _Transfer history_.
5.  Tap the withdrawal record you want to cancel.
6.  On the detail page, tap _Cancel withdrawal._
7.  Tap _Yes, cancel_ to confirm.
