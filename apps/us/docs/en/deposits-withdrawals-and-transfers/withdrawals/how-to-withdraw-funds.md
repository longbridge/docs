## **How to Submit a Withdrawal**

1.  Tap 「Transfer」 on the home screen, then tap 「Withdraw」.

2.  Enter the amount you want to withdraw.

3.  Check the 「To」 field below the amount. This shows the destination bank account. Tap it to open the account selector and choose a different bank if needed.

In the account selector, you will see:

-   ACH accounts — labeled Fast and free with a $50,000 daily limit shown

-   Wire accounts — labeled with the applicable fee (e.g., Fees: $25.00)

-   An Add an account option if you need to link a new bank

4.  Tap 「Next」 once the amount is valid and passes all checks.

5.  Review the confirmation screen. It shows your withdrawal amount (with a negative sign), the destination bank, and any applicable Wire fee.

6.  Tap 「Withdraw」 now to submit.

After submitting, you will see a success screen showing:

-   The withdrawal amount

-   Withdrawal initiated date

-   Estimated completion note (ACH: 1–3 business days; Wire: up to 1 business day)

Tap 「OK」 to return to the 「Transfer」 page.

## **Understanding Withdrawal Limits**

**Available to withdraw**

This is the maximum you can currently withdraw, based on your available account balance. It is shown below the amount input on the withdrawal screen.

**ACH daily limit**

For ACH withdrawals, there is an additional daily limit of $50,000. The applicable withdrawal amount is the smaller of:

-   Your available balance, and

-   $50,000 minus the total ACH withdrawals you have already submitted today with status Initiated, In progress, or Completed.

Non-business-day submissions count toward the next business day's limit. The limit resets at 3:30 PM ET on each business day.

To see a breakdown, tap the ⓘ icon next to You can withdraw up to on the withdrawal screen.

> **Over-limit Error**
>
> If the amount you enter exceeds your balance or daily limit, the input field turns red. Lower the amount and try again.

**Minimum withdrawal amount**

A minimum withdrawal amount may apply, depending on your account configuration. If your entered amount is too low, the screen will show: You'll need to withdraw at least $XX.XX (this includes any applicable bank fee).

## **Wire Withdrawals That Require Manual Review**

If you submit a Wire withdrawal and Longbridge detects that within the past 60 days your account received an ACH deposit from a different bank account, the withdrawal will be flagged for manual review.

In this case, the confirmation screen will show:

-   This withdrawal requires manual review.

After submission, the success screen will display:

-   This withdrawal requires manual review. Once approved, the wire transfer will be processed within approximately 30 minutes.

> **Note** 
>
> Manual review is a security measure to protect your account. No action is required from you beyond submitting the request. Our team will process it as quickly as possible.

## **Canceling a Withdrawal**

You can cancel a withdrawal only while it is in Initiated status (the earliest stage, before it has been sent for processing).

1.  Go to 「Transfer」 → 「Transfer history」.

2.  Tap the withdrawal record you want to cancel.

3.  On the detail page, tap 「Cancel withdrawal」.

4.  Tap 「Yes, cancel」 to confirm.

> **Note** 
>
> Once a withdrawal moves to In progress or beyond, it can no longer be canceled. You will see a message indicating the current status if cancellation is unavailable.
