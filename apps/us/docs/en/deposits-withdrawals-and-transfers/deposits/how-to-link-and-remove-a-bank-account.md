**How to Link a Bank Account**

You can add a bank account from the _Transfer_ page:

1.  Open the app and tap _Transfer_ from the home screen.
2.  On the _Transfer_ page, tap _Add_ to add an account under linked accounts.
3.  Choose a connection type: ACH or Wire.

**Option A — Linking via ACH**

ACH allows you to connect a personal checking or savings account, verified either instantly or through a small test deposit.

**Choose your verification method:**

Real-time verification (Real time verification is available if your bank is supported on Plaid)

-   Tap _Real-time verification_ to open the secure Plaid portal.
-   Enter your mobile number and the SMS verification code you receive.
-   Select your bank, log in with your online banking credentials, and choose the account to link.
-   Once confirmed, Plaid closes automatically and your account is linked instantly.

Micro-deposit verification (takes 1–2 business days)

-   Tap _Micro-deposit verification_.
-   Fill in your bank details: bank name, ABA routing number (9 digits), account type (checking or savings), account number, and an optional nickname.
-   Tap _Next_. Longbridge will send two small deposits (each under USD 1) to your bank account.
-   Once the deposits arrive, return to the _Transfer_ page and tap the pending verification.
-   Enter both deposit amounts in the order they were received, and tap Next to complete verification.

**Option B — Linking via Wire**

Wire accounts do not require verification. Fill in your bank details and tap _Add account_ to save.

You are responsible for confirming that your bank account details are correct and that your external bank account is eligible to receive wire transfers. Wires may not be able to be cancelled or reversed once initiated.

Required fields:

-   Bank name
-   ABA routing number (US banks)
-   Province / State, City, Postal code
-   Account number
-   Account owner (pre-filled from your Longbridge profile — cannot be changed)
-   Account nickname (optional)

**Reordering Your Linked Accounts**

The order of your linked accounts determines which account appears as the default when you deposit or withdraw.

4.  On the _Transfer_ page, find the _Linked accounts_ section.
5.  Long-press the drag handle (⠿ ) on the right side of any account.
6.  Drag it to your preferred position and release.

**How to Remove a Bank Account**

7.  On the _Transfer_ page, tap the bank account you want to remove.
8.  On the account detail page, tap _Remove_ at the bottom.
9.  Tap _Remove_ again in the confirmation pop-up. The account will be unlinked immediately.

**FAQs**

**Q1: Why can't I add a fourth ACH account?**

Longbridge supports a maximum of 3 linked ACH accounts. Please remove an existing ACH account first, and then try adding the new one.

**Q2: My micro-deposit verification has expired. What should I do?**

Verification must be completed within 10 days of the deposits being sent. If your verification has expired, tap _OK_ on the error message to request a new pair of deposits and restart the process.

**Q3: I entered the micro-deposit amounts wrong. How many attempts do I have?**

You have 3 attempts. After 3 failed tries, Longbridge will automatically send two new deposits and reset the verification. If the re-send limit is exceeded, you will need to restart the entire verification process.
