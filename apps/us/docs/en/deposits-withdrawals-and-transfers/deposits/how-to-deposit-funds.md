**Method 1: ACH Deposit (Recommended)**

ACH transfers are free and the easiest way to deposit. You must have at least one linked ACH bank account to use this method.

**Steps**

1.  Tap _Transfers_ on the home screen, then tap _Deposit_.
2.  On the _How would you like to deposit?_ screen, tap _ACH_.
3.  Enter the amount you wish to deposit. The minimum is $0.01 and the maximum is your remaining daily limit.
4.  Check the _From_ field below the amount. This shows your currently selected bank account. Tap it to switch to a different linked ACH account if needed.
5.  After a valid amount has been entered, tap _Next_.
6.  Review the summary screen — it shows your deposit amount and the source bank account. Please note that returned ACH deposits may be subject to a $30 fee.
7.  Tap _Deposit now_ to submit.

Once submitted, you will see a confirmation screen showing:

-   The deposit amount
-   Deposit initiated date
-   Estimated completion date (up to 5 business days)

Tap _OK_ to return to the _Transfers_ page.

**Understanding Your Daily Limit**

Your daily ACH deposit limit is $50,000, calculated based on all ACH deposits you submitted that day that are in progress or completed.

-   Deposits submitted on non-working days count toward the next working day's limit.
-   The limit resets every business day at 16:00 ET.
-   To check your remaining limit, tap the ⓘ icon next to _You can deposit up to_ on the deposit screen.

**Method 2: Wire Deposit**

Wire deposits are initiated on your end — either through your bank's online platform, in person at a branch, or in some cases over the phone. Longbridge does not process wires directly. You'll send the transfer using the beneficiary details provided in the app.

**Steps**

1.  Tap _Transfers_, and then tap _Deposit_.
2.  On the _How would you like to deposit?_ screen, tap _Wire_.
3.  The _How to Wire_ screen will display Longbridge's receiving bank details, including beneficiary name, bank name, account number, ABA routing number, bank address, currency (USD), and payee reference/FFC.
4.  Tap the copy icon next to each field to copy it, and then use those details to initiate a wire transfer through your bank's online platform or at a branch.

**Canceling a Deposit**

You can cancel an ACH deposit while it is still in progress.

1.  Go to _Transfers_ > _Transfer history_.
2.  Tap the deposit record you want to cancel.
3.  On the detail page, tap _Cancel deposit_.
4.  Tap _Yes, cancel_ to confirm. The deposit will be withdrawn and the page will refresh.

**FAQs**

**Q1: How long does an ACH deposit take?**

ACH deposits are typically credited to your account by the next business day (T+1). Once your deposit is successful, you'll receive instant buying power, so you can start trading right away. Learn more about [**Instant Buying Power**](/articles/17877868710415). You'll see an estimated completion date on the confirmation screen after submitting.

Please note:

-   ACH deposits are not eligible for withdrawal for 5 business days.
-   It may take several days for the debit to appear in your bank account. This depends on your bank and may vary.

**Q2: What happens if my deposit is returned?**

Returned deposits will appear as _Returned_ in your _Transfer history_, and a $30 return fee may be charged. The returned amount will be reflected as a fee on the deposit detail page.

**Q3: Why was my deposit rejected?**

Deposits can be rejected for several reasons, including mismatched account information or bank-side issues. Check the deposit detail page for the specific status. If you need further help, contact our support team at **support@longbridge.com**.

**Q4: I don't see the ACH option. What should I do?**

If you do not have a linked ACH bank account, you will be taken to the bank linking flow when you tap _ACH_. Follow the steps in the _How to Link a Bank Account_ guide to add an account first.
