### **Method 1: ACH Deposit (Recommended)**

ACH transfers are free and the easiest way to deposit. You must have at least one linked ACH bank account to use this method.

**Steps**

1.  Tap 「Transfer」 on the home screen, then tap 「Deposit」.

2.  On the 「How would you like to deposit?」 screen, tap 「ACH」.

3.  On the deposit amount screen, type in the amount you want to deposit. The minimum is $0.01 and the maximum is your remaining daily limit.

4.  Check the From field below the amount. This shows your currently selected bank account. Tap it to switch to a different linked ACH account if needed.

5.  Tap 「Next」 when the amount is valid.

6.  Review the summary screen — it shows your deposit amount and the source bank account. Read the notice: ACH deposit returns may be subject to a $30 fee.

7.  Tap 「Deposit now」 to submit.

Once submitted, you will see a confirmation screen showing:

-   The deposit amount

-   Deposit initiated date

-   Estimated completion date (up to 5 business days later)

Tap 「OK」 to return to the Transfer page.

> **Note** 
>
> If you have deposits already In progress, the total amount in progress is shown at the top of the deposit method screen. Tap it to view all pending deposits.

**Understanding Your Daily Limit**

Your daily ACH deposit limit is $50,000. The limit is calculated based on all ACH deposits you submitted today that are In progress or Completed.

-   Non-business-day submissions count toward the next business day's limit.

-   The limit resets every business day at 4:00 PM ET.

-   To check your remaining limit, tap the ⓘ icon next to 「You can deposit up to」 on the deposit screen.

> **Over-limit Error**
>
> If the amount you enter exceeds your remaining daily limit, the input field turns red. Lower the amount and try again.

### **Method 2: Wire Deposit**

Wire deposits are initiated from your bank — either online or in person at a branch. Longbridge does not process wire deposits directly; you send the wire using the beneficiary details below.

**Steps**

1.  Tap 「Transfer」, then tap 「Deposit」.

2.  Tap 「Wire」 on the 「How would you like to deposit?」 screen.

3.  The 「How to Wire」 screen displays Longbridge's receiving bank details. You will see: Beneficiary name, Bank name, Account number, ABA routing number, Bank address, Currency (USD), and Payee Reference / FFC.

4.  Tap the copy icon next to each field to copy it, then use those details to initiate a wire transfer through your own bank's online platform or at a branch.

> **Important**
>
> Your account name must match on both ends. Longbridge does not accept wires from third-party or business accounts.

## **Canceling a Deposit**

You can cancel an ACH deposit while it is still In progress.

1.  Go to Transfer → Transfer history.

2.  Tap the deposit record you want to cancel.

3.  On the detail page, tap 「Cancel deposit」.

4.  Tap 「Yes」, cancel to confirm. The deposit will be withdrawn and the page will refresh.

> **Note** 
>
> Once a deposit is completed, rejected, or returned, it can no longer be canceled. If cancelation is no longer available, you will see a message explaining the current status.

## **FAQS**

**How long does an ACH deposit take?**

ACH deposits are typically credited to your account by the next business day (T+1), though the funds will remain on hold for up to 4 additional business days before becoming available to use. You will see an estimated completion date on the confirmation screen after submitting.

**What happens if my deposit is returned?**

If a deposit is returned, it will appear as 「Returned」 in your Transfer history. A $30 return fee may be charged. The returned amount will be reflected as a fee on the deposit detail page.

**Why was my deposit rejected?**

Deposits can be rejected for several reasons, including mismatched account information or bank-side issues. Check the deposit detail page for the specific status, and contact our support team if you need help resolving it.

**I don't see the ACH option. What should I do?**

If you do not have a linked ACH bank account, you will be taken to the bank linking flow when you tap 「ACH」. Follow the steps in the 「How to Link a Bank Account」 guide to add one first.
