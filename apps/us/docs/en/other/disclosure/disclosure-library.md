Brokerage accounts are opened through the Longbridge mobile application. Disclosures, agreements, and risk documents required by **Apex Clearing Corporation and Bakkt Financial Solutions I, LLC** are presented within the Longbridge platform in the form supplied by those providers.

## Order Routing Disclosure – SEC Rule 606(a) & 606(b)

-   APEX Clearing Corporation routes all customer orders placed through the Longbridge platform.
-   Quarterly SEC Rule 606(a) reports are publicly available through the FINRA 606-NMS repository [https://www.finra.org/finra-data/606-nms-data](https://www.finra.org/finra-data/606-nms-data)
-   Customer-specific SEC Rule 606(b) routing information is available upon request by emailing [support@longbridge.com](mailto:support@longbridge.com).
