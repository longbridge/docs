## <span style="color: #2F3941;">Order Routing Disclosure – SEC Rule 606(a) & 606(b)</span>

-   <span style="color: #2F3941;">APEX Clearing Corporation routes all customer orders placed through the Longbridge platform.</span>
-   <span style="color: #2F3941;">Quarterly SEC Rule 606(a) reports are publicly available through the FINRA 606-NMS repository</span> [<span style="color: #096ECC;">https://www.finra.org/finra-data/606-nms-data</span>](https://www.finra.org/finra-data/606-nms-data)
-   <span style="color: #2F3941;">Customer-specific SEC Rule 606(b) routing information is available upon request by emailing</span> [<span style="color: #096ECC;">support@longbridge.com</span>](mailto:support@longbridge.com)<span style="color: #096ECC;">.</span>
