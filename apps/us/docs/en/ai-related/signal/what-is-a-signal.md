## What a Signal tells you

A Signal is an AI-generated trading alert for a specific stock. Longbridge continuously monitors market events — such as earnings releases, technical breakouts, and changes in a company's fundamentals — and generates a Signal when a stock newly meets the conditions of one of our investment Strategies.

Each Signal tells you four things at a glance:

-   **The stock** the Signal applies to

-   **The Strategy** that was triggered (for example, Innovation Growth or Quality Value)

-   **The direction** of the opportunity (such as a potential entry)

-   **The reason** the Signal was generated — the underlying event or factor change

## How Signals are generated

A Signal is produced when a _catalyst_ — a meaningful market event — **occurs for a stock**. **Longbridge checks whether this event causes the stock to satisfy the rules of one of our Strategies, based on** that Strategy's factor framework (growth, momentum, profitability, quality, valuation, and more). When the conditions are met, a Signal is created and shown to you with its supporting rationale.

## A Signal is a point-in-time snapshot

Each Signal reflects the exact moment it was created and **is never edited afterward**. If new developments change the picture, Longbridge generates a _new_ Signal rather than updating the old one. This keeps every Signal a faithful record of what was true when it was issued, so you can always trace the reasoning behind it.

## What you can do with a Signal

-   **Review** the reasoning to understand why the stock was flagged

-   **Act** on it — for example, add the stock to your watchlist, or place an order through a **Trade Plan**

-   **Ignore** it if it doesn't fit your plan

> Signals are research tools to help you discover opportunities. They are not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.
