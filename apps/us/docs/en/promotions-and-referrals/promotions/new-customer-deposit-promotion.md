Participation in this promotion ("Promotion") constitutes the participant's full and unconditional agreement to these Terms & Conditions. The Promotion is offered by Long Bridge Securities LLC ("Longbridge") to reward eligible customers who fund their Longbridge U.S. brokerage account for the first time during the Promotion Period.

This promotion is intended to encourage new account funding and is not intended to incentivize trading activity. Customers are not required to place any trades to qualify for this promotion.

## 1\. Promotion Period

This promotion is valid from <span style="background-color: #F5D5D8;">TBD, 2026, 12:00 AM ET – TBD, 2026, 11:59 PM ET</span> (the “Promotion Period”).

## 2\. Eligibility

-   All approved new customers (as determined by Tax ID) who fund and settle their Longbridge account during the Promotion Period

-   Customers (new or existing) who have not made any deposit or asset transfer into their Longbridge brokerage account **prior to \[Start date of the promotion\], 2026**, and who fund and settle their account during the Promotion Period.

-   Follow the steps of "How to Participate"

## 3\. Definitions

### 3.1 Eligible Account

A Longbridge brokerage account opened, approved, and in good standing during the Program Period.

### 3.2 Qualified Funding Amount

Total cash deposits and asset transfers in, minus any withdrawals or asset transfers out, measured during the Promotion Period and Holding Period.

### 3.3 Holding Period

The Holding Period is a continuous 60-calendar-day period. Day 1 of the Holding Period begins on the next calendar day following the settlement date of the deposit or transfer that establishes or re-establishes the customer's qualification for the applicable reward tier (the "Qualifying Settlement Date").

## 4\. How to Participate

To participate and receive a reward, an eligible customer must:

1.  Go to Account tab, My tab, or Promotion page to locate the New Customer Promotion.

2.  Open and fund a brokerage account by tapping **"Fund Your Account"**.

3.  Connect your bank and deposit funds (ACH/wire) or initiate an ACAT transfer-in.

4.  Complete funding (via cash deposit or asset transfer) that results in a **Qualified Funding Amount** meeting one of the reward tiers below; and

5.  Most approvals are completed within 1–2 business days; additional documentation may be required.

6.  Maintain the required Qualified Funding Amount through the applicable **Holding Period** checkpoint.

Please note, deposits must both be initiated and fully settled during the Promotion Period to qualify for the promotion.

### 5.2 Additional Funding and Withdrawal

-   The 60-day Holding Period begins on the calendar day immediately following (T+1) the Qualifying Settlement Date, which is defined as the settlement date of the last deposit or transfer that establishes or re-establishes the customer's qualification for the applicable reward tier.

-   Customers may add additional deposits or transfers during the Promotion Period.

    -   New deposits during the Holding Period:

        -   If a new deposit or transfer in during the Holding Period results in the Qualified Funding Amount reaching a higher reward tier, the Holding Period resets. Day 1 of the new Holding Period begins on the next calendar day following the settlement date of the deposit or transfer that establishes the higher tier.

        -   If a new deposit or transfer in during the Holding Period does not result in a higher reward tier, the Holding Period continues without interruption and the reward tier remains unchanged.

    -   Withdrawals during the Holding Period:

        -   If a withdrawal or transfer out during the Holding Period reduces the Qualified Funding Amount to a lower reward tier but the customer still qualifies for at least one tier, the Holding Period continues without interruption from the original Qualifying Settlement Date. The applicable reward will be adjusted to correspond to the lower tier at the time of the Day 60 eligibility snapshot.

        -   If a withdrawal or transfer out during the Holding Period reduces the Qualified Funding Amount below all reward tiers, the reward is forfeited and the Holding Period is terminated. Any days accumulated prior to such withdrawal will not be credited if the customer subsequently re-qualifies. The Holding Period will restart on the next calendar day following the new Qualifying Settlement Date.

-   Any previously satisfied lower-tier qualification will continue to be measured based on its original Holding Period timeline. However, regardless of how many tiers are satisfied, each eligible customer may redeem only one (1) reward under this Promotion. Lower-tier Holding Period days are tracked in parallel. If a customer’s Qualified Funding Amount is reduced due to a withdrawal, the system will apply the highest tier for which the full 60-day Holding Period has been completed as of the Day 60 eligibility snapshot.

Example:

-   Example 1 (Upgrade): A customer deposits $1,000 on April 3 (settles April 3) and later deposits an additional $4,000 on April 10 (settles April 10), qualifying for the $5,000 tier. The Qualifying Settlement Date is April 10, and Day 1 of the 60-day Holding Period is April 11.

-   Example 2 (Withdrawal with downgrade): A customer deposits $1,000 on April 3. On April 20, the customer withdraws $200, reducing the Qualified Funding Amount to $800. The reward tier adjusts to the $100 tier. The Holding Period continues from April 4 (Day 1) without interruption; only the reward amount changes.

-   Example 3 (Re-entry after full withdrawal): A customer deposits $1,000 on April 3, then withdraws the full $1,000 on April 20, forfeiting the reward. The customer re-deposits $1,000 on May 1 (settles May 1). A new Holding Period begins and Day 1 is May 2. Days accumulated prior to the full withdrawal are not carried forward.
