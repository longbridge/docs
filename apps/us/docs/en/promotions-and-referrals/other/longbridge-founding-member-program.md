## Terms & Conditions

Participation in this program ("Program") constitutes the participant's full and unconditional agreement to these Terms and Conditions. The Program is offered by Long Bridge Securities LLC ("Longbridge") to a limited group of eligible customers who are among the first to open and fund a Longbridge U.S. brokerage account during the Program Period.

This Program is intended to recognize early supporters and encourage new account opening. It is not intended to incentivize trading activity. Customers are not required to place any trades to qualify for this Program or to receive any of its benefits.

## 1\. Program Period

This Program is valid from TBD, 2026, 12:00 AM ET through TBD, 2026, 11:59 PM ET (the "Program Period"), or until all 200 available Founding Member spots have been filled, whichever occurs first.

## 2\. Eligibility

-   Founding Member status is available to a maximum of 200 eligible accounts on a first-come, first-served basis from the Longbridge waitlist.

-   Eligible participants are new customers (as determined by Tax ID) who receive an official Founding Member invitation from Longbridge by email, open and fund a Longbridge brokerage account during the Program Period, and complete account approval.

-   All participants must be legal U.S. residents and at least 18 years of age as of the date of participation.

-   Founding Member invitations are personal and non-transferable. An invitation received by email does not guarantee Founding Member status if the 200-spot limit has already been reached at the time of account funding.

## 3\. Definitions

### 3.1 Founding Member

A customer who has received an official invitation from Longbridge, opened an Eligible Account during the Program Period, and secured one of the 200 available Founding Member spots.

### 3.2 Eligible Account

A Longbridge brokerage account opened, approved, and in good standing during the Program Period.

### 3.3 Max Tier

The subscription tier offered on the Longbridge platform, providing access to the full suite of Longbridge features. The Max tier is ordinarily available on a paid subscription basis.

### 3.4 Qualified Funding Amount

Total cash deposits and asset transfers into the account, minus any withdrawals or asset transfers out of the account, measured during the Program Period and applicable Holding Period.

### 3.5 Holding Period

A continuous 60-calendar-day period beginning on the next calendar day following the settlement date of the deposit or transfer that establishes or re-establishes the customer's qualification for the applicable reward tier (the "Qualifying Settlement Date").

## 4\. How to Participate

Funding your account is not required to receive Founding Member Status or to activate the complimentary Alpha tier benefit.

However, customers who choose to fund their account during the Program Period may also be eligible for a deposit reward of up to $1,500 in stock or cryptocurrency under the separate Longbridge New Customer Promotion. To qualify for that reward:

1.  Connect your bank and deposit funds (ACH or wire transfer) or initiate an ACAT transfer.
2.  Complete funding sufficient to qualify for at least one reward tier described in Section 5.3.
3.  Maintain the required Qualified Funding Amount through the applicable Holding Period checkpoint.

Deposits must both be initiated and fully settled during the Program Period to qualify for the New Customer Promotion reward. See Section 5.3 and the full New Customer Promotion Terms and Conditions for complete details.

## 5\. Founding Member Benefits

Confirmed Founding Members are entitled to the following benefits, subject to the conditions described in each section below.

### 5.1 Complimentary Max Tier Access

Founding Members receive three (3) months of complimentary Max tier access beginning on the date their Founding Member status is confirmed. No deposit is required to activate or maintain this benefit.

-   The Max tier provides access to the full Longbridge feature set, including exclusive product capabilities not available on the Free tier.
-   The retail value of Max tier access is $299.97 per person for the three-month period.
-   Complimentary Max access will be credited to the customer's account upon confirmation of Founding Member status and will appear under My Rewards in the Longbridge app. No additional redemption action is required to activate it.
-   The benefit must be activated within 3 months of the date it is credited. If not activated within that period, the reward will expire automatically and will no longer be available. Expired rewards have no cash value and cannot be reinstated.
-   Upon expiration of the complimentary period, the account will revert to the Free tier unless the customer elects to subscribe to a paid plan.
-   The complimentary Max tier benefit is non-transferable and has no cash value.

### 5.2 Product Access and Engagement Benefits

Subject to availability and at Longbridge's discretion, Founding Members may be eligible for the following additional benefits during and after the Program Period:

-   Early access: Founding Members may receive access to new product features and updates before they are made available to the general user base, where such early access programs are offered by Longbridge. Availability of early access is not guaranteed and may vary by feature, timing, and operational readiness.

-   Feedback sessions: Founding Members may be invited to exclusive product previews and feedback sessions where Longbridge solicits input from early users prior to broader feature releases. Such invitations, if extended, will be communicated by email or through the Longbridge app. Participation in any feedback session is voluntary and does not affect account standing or any other benefit.

-   Priority event invitations: Founding Members may receive priority invitations to special events, promotions, or community programs as Longbridge expands in the U.S. market. The availability, format, and frequency of such events are subject to change and are not guaranteed.

The benefits described in this Section 5.2 are offered as good-faith expressions of Longbridge's commitment to early supporters and do not constitute contractual obligations. Longbridge reserves the right to modify, delay, or discontinue any of these benefits at any time without prior notice, subject to applicable regulatory requirements.

## 6\. How to Confirm Founding Member Status

Longbridge will notify customers by email and in-app message at each key step of the process, including when Founding Member status is confirmed and when a reward is credited to the account.

Founding Member status is confirmed upon delivery of the complimentary Max tier benefit. Once a customer's eligibility is verified, Longbridge will credit the complimentary Max tier benefit to the customer's account. Receipt of this reward serves as confirmation of Founding Member status. If no reward has been received, the customer has not been granted Founding Member status.

The complimentary Max tier benefit will appear under My Rewards in the Longbridge app. Customers must navigate to My Rewards and tap Use to activate the benefit. The benefit will not be applied automatically.

Founding Member status will also be reflected in the Longbridge app under the My tab or Account tab.

To claim the New Customer Promotion reward (if applicable), please follow the steps outlined in the separate New Customer Promotion Terms and Conditions.
