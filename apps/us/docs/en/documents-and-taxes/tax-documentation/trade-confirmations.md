Trade confirmations document the details of the trades executed in your account on a given day. This article explains how to find, filter, view, and download your trade confirmations.

## Where to find trade confirmations

1.  From your account's home page, open the _Docs & statements_ menu.
2.  Tap _Trade confirmations_.

You'll see a list of dates on which trades were confirmed for your account, sorted with the most recent date at the top. Trade confirmations are organized by day — if there were no trades confirmed on a given day, that day won't appear in the list.

## Filtering by date

At the top of the list is a _Date range_ filter, which defaults to _All dates_. Tap it to open a selection menu with the following options:

-   _This month_
-   _Past 3 months_
-   _Past 6 months_
-   _Past year_
-   _Custom range_

If you choose _Custom range_, you'll be asked to enter a start date and an end date. The _OK_ button only becomes active once both fields are filled in. Once you confirm, the filter will display the range you selected (for example, _01/15/2022-01/30/2025_). If you choose one of the preset options instead, the filter will display that option's name (for example, _This month_).

## Viewing a trade confirmation

Tap any date in the list to open the trade confirmation for that day. It will load as a PDF directly in the app.

## Downloading or sharing a trade confirmation

While viewing a trade confirmation, tap the download button at the top of the page to download or share the PDF.
