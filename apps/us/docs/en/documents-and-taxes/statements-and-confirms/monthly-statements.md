Monthly statements provide a detailed record of your account activity, holdings, and balances for each calendar month. This article explains how to find, view, and download your monthly statements.

## Where to find monthly statements

1.  From your account's home page, open the _Docs & statements_ menu.
2.  Tap _Monthly statements_.

You'll see a list of your available statements, sorted with the most recent month at the top (for example, _Dec 2025_). If a particular month has no statement available, it won't appear in the list.

## Viewing a statement

Tap any month in the list to open that statement. It will load as a PDF directly in the app so you can review it on-screen.

## Downloading a statement

While viewing a statement, tap _Download_ in the top-right corner to save the PDF to your device.
