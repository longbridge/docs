## Overview

Longbridge offers two levels of options trading access for all clients, Level 1 or Level 2 strategies. All options trading requires prior approval and is subject to eligibility and risk review.

## Options Trading Levels

### Level 1 — Covered and Income Strategies

These are conservative strategies designed to generate income or enter stock positions at favorable prices.

**Available strategies:**

-   **Covered Calls:** Sell call options against stocks you already own.

-   **Buy-Writes:** Simultaneously buy stock and sell a call option on that stock.

-   **Cash-Secured Puts:** Sell puts while holding enough cash to buy the shares if assigned.

### Level 2 — Long and Protective Strategies

Includes all Level 1 strategies plus long (directional) and hedging strategies.

**Available strategies:**

-   **Long Calls:** Buy calls to benefit from a rise in the stock price.

-   **Long Puts:** Buy puts to benefit from a decline in the stock price or to hedge existing holdings.

-   **Protective Puts:** Purchase puts to limit downside risk on shares you already own.

-   **Long Straddles:** Buy a call and a put with the same strike price and expiration to benefit from large price moves in either direction.

-   **Long Strangles:** Buy a call and a put with different strike prices but the same expiration to capture potential volatility with less upfront cost than a straddle.

## Risk Considerations

Options involve significant risk and are not appropriate for all investors.

-   Option buyers risk the **entire premium** paid if the option expires worthless.

-   Option sellers face potential **assignment risk** and must be prepared to buy or sell shares if assigned.

-   Leverage can magnify both gains and losses.

Please review the [**Characteristics and Risks of Standardized Options (ODD)**](https://www.theocc.com/getmedia/a151a9ae-d784-4a15-bdeb-23a029f50b70/riskstoc.pdf) before trading.
