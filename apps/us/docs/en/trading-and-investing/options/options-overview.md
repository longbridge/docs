## **Options Trading Help Center**

Discover how options work, understand the risks involved, and learn how to trade options confidently with Longbridge.

## **1\. Overview of Options Trading**

Options are financial contracts that give you the right, but not the obligation, to buy or sell an underlying asset (like a stock or ETF) at a specific price before a certain date. Options can be used to manage risk, generate income, or speculate on price movement.

We support Options Trading up to Level 2:

-   **Level 1:** Covered Calls, Protective Puts
-   **Level 2:** Buying Calls and Puts

We do not support complex strategies such as spreads, straddles, or naked short options.

## **2\. Option Basics**

-   **Call Option:** Right to buy the underlying asset at the strike price before expiration.
-   **Put Option:** Right to sell the underlying asset at the strike price before expiration.
-   **Strike Price:** The agreed price for the transaction.
-   **Expiration Date:** The last day the option can be exercised.
-   **Premium:** The cost to buy the option, paid upfront.

## **3\. Benefits and Risks**

**Benefits:**

-   **Leverage:** Control more shares with less capital.
-   **Flexibility:** Profit from both upward and downward movements.
-   **Risk Management:** Hedge against losses in stock positions.

**Risks:**

-   **Potential for Total Loss:** Entire premium can be lost if option expires worthless.
-   **Time Decay:** Options lose value as expiration approaches.
-   **Volatility Risk:** Market volatility can affect pricing.

\*\*\* Options are not suitable for all investors. Understand the risks before trading.

## **4\. Approval Levels**

To trade options, you must apply for approval. Approval is based on your experience, financial profile, and risk tolerance.

**Level 1 – Covered Calls & Protective Puts:**

-   **Covered Call:** Sell a call against stock you already own.
-   **Protective Put:** Buy a put to hedge a stock position.

**Level 2 – Buying Calls & Buying Puts:**

-   **Buy Call:** Bullish strategy.
-   **Buy Put:** Bearish strategy.

We do not offer: naked short options, vertical spreads, straddles, strangles, or multi-leg strategies.

## **5\. Placing an Options Trade**

1.  Search for the underlying asset.
2.  Open the options chain.
3.  Select expiration date and strike price.
4.  Choose order type (Market, Limit).
5.  Confirm order details and submit.

## **6\. Trading Hours for Options**

-   **Regular Market:** 9:30 AM – 4:00 PM ET
-   Options cannot be traded in pre-market or after-hours sessions.
-   Crypto options are not available.
