## What is fractional share trading?

Fractional share trading allows you to buy or sell less than one whole share of a stock or ETF. Instead of transacting a full share, you can trade the exact dollar amount or share quantity you choose. This makes it easier to:

-   Start investing with a smaller amount of money

-   Diversify across more companies

-   Align your investments with your budget or strategy

## Which stocks will be eligible for fractional share trading?

Fractional share trading will be offered on selected US-listed stocks and ETFs. Eligible securities will be clearly marked in the trading platform so you can easily identify them.

## How will I trade fractional shares?

You can place fractional share orders — both buys and sells — in two ways:

1.  By share quantity – enter the fraction of a share you want to buy or sell (e.g., 0.25 shares). When selling, you may sell your full fractional position or any portion of it.

2.  **By dollar amount** – enter the amount you want to invest or receive from a sale, and the system will calculate the equivalent share quantity based on the current market price. Minimum amount is $5.00 for both buys and sells.

## How will corporate actions work with fractional shares?

-   **Included:** Fractional shares will participate in mandatory corporate actions, such as dividends and stock splits.

-   **Not included:** Fractional shares generally will not be eligible for voluntary corporate actions, such as rights offerings or tender offers.

## Trading Hours for Fractional Shares

-   Fractional share trading will only be available during **regular market hours**: 9:30 a.m. – 4:00 p.m. (ET)

-   On **half-day sessions** (such as before certain holidays), trading hours will be 9:30 a.m. – 1:00 p.m. (ET)

## Order Types Supported

-   **Market orders** and **limit orders** will be supported.

-   Conditional orders (such as stop or trailing orders) and advanced order types will not be supported for fractional shares.

## Margin and Short Selling

-   Fractional shares cannot be sold short.

-   They will be supported in margin accounts for buying purposes, with the same margin interest rate that applies to whole shares.

## Order Modifications

-   Fractional share orders cannot be modified after submission.

-   If you need to make changes, you’ll need to cancel the order and submit a new one.

## Minimum Order Requirement

-   **Minimum share quantity:** 0.0001 shares

-   **Minimum order value:** $5 per trade

## Account Transfers

-   Fractional share positions **cannot be transferred** to another brokerage via ACATS (Automated Customer Account Transfer Service).

-   To avoid unexpected liquidation, consider selling fractional positions manually before initiating a transfer.
