## **US Stock Market Trading Hours**

-   **Regular Trading Hours (ET):** 9:30 a.m. – 4:00 p.m.

## **US Stock Trading and Settlement**

### **Day Trading**

Day trading refers to buying and selling the same security within a single trading day.

-   Following FINRA Rule 4210 (Regulatory Notice 26-10) (effective June 4, 2026), the Pattern Day Trader (PDT) framework has been removed. All margin account holders may day trade freely — there is no longer a PDT designation or a minimum equity requirement based on the number of day trades made.
-   For margin accounts, day trading buying power is determined by your Intraday Margin Buying Power (IMBP) at the start of the day.
-   Exceeding your IMBP may trigger an Intraday Margin Call (IM Call). You must resolve it by depositing funds or reducing your position before the deadline. If unresolved, your account may be restricted to close-only for 90 days.

### **Clearing Rules**

US stocks follow a **T+1 settlement cycle**, meaning trades are cleared and settled on the **next business day** after the transaction.

## **Extended Hours Trading**

Longbridge supports **Pre-Market** and **After-Hours Trading** for US stocks in addition to standard market hours.

-   **Pre-Market:** 4:00 a.m. – 9:30 a.m. (ET)
-   **After-Hours:** 4:00 p.m. – 8:00 p.m. (ET)
-   **Half-Day Sessions (e.g., before holidays):** Regular market closes at 1:00 p.m. (ET) After-hours runs 1:00 p.m. – 5:00 p.m. (ET)

## **Overnight Trading**

Longbridge also supports **Overnight Trading**, giving clients the ability to trade US stocks nearly 24 hours a day.

-   **Overnight Session:** 8:00 p.m. – 4:00 a.m. (ET, next day)

### **Key Considerations for Overnight Trading**

-   **Liquidity:** Trading volume is typically lower, which may result in wider bid/ask spreads.
-   **Volatility:** Prices can move sharply in response to news and events released outside regular market hours.
-   [**Order Types**](https://longbridgeus.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/15955750716303-Order-Types)**:** Only limit orders are accepted during overnight sessions.
