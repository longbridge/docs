## What fees apply when trading US stocks, ETFs, and options?

When you trade US-listed stocks, ETFs, and options, the following fees may apply. For crypto trading fees, see the section below.

### 1\. Regulatory & Exchange Fees

These are charged by regulators and exchanges and passed directly to investors:

-   **SEC Transaction Fee** – applies to sell transactions.

-   **FINRA Trading Activity Fee (TAF)** – applies to sell transactions, charged on a per-share basis with a capped maximum.

-   **Exchange Fees** – certain exchanges may charge fees or provide rebates depending on how your order interacts with liquidity.

-   **OCC Regulatory Fee** – applies to options contracts only. Charged by the Options Clearing Corporation (OCC) on a per-contract basis and passed directly to investors.

### 2\. Trading Fees

-   **Stocks & ETFs** – $0 commission on all trades.

-   **Equity Options (Stock Options)** – $0 commission, $0 contract fee per leg. Longbridge does not charge a contract fee on equity options.

-   **Index Options (SPX, NDX, and others)** – $0 commission, $0.50 per contract. This fee applies to broad-based index options such as SPX and NDX.

### 3\. Other Possible Costs

-   **Margin Interest** – applies when trading on borrowed funds. Rates vary and accrue daily.

-   **Corporate Action Processing Fees** – may apply for certain voluntary corporate actions (e.g., tender offers).

## What fees apply when trading crypto?

When you buy or sell cryptocurrency on Longbridge, the following fees apply:

### 1\. Trading Fee

-   **Commission** – 0.75% per trade, applied to both buy and sell transactions. The fee is calculated on the notional amount of each trade. For example, a $1,000 crypto purchase incurs a $7.50 trading fee.

### 2\. Other Notes

-   Crypto transactions are not subject to SEC or FINRA regulatory fees.

-   Margin trading and short selling are not currently supported for crypto assets.

## Why does my trade show a fee?

Even though commissions may not apply, trades can still reflect small **regulatory or exchange fees**. These charges are mandated by the SEC, FINRA, and exchanges and are not set by Longbridge.
