To place a trade for Stocks or ETFs, review the following steps:

1\. Go to the stock quote page, tap **Trade** button, select **Buy** or **Sell** for the order direction.

2\. Input all required order parameters, like order type, price, quantity, time in force etc., then tap **Review order** to double confirm. Once order information is confirmed, tap **Confirm & submit**, the order will be submitted to upstream broker.

3\. If order is in a pending status, and you want to modify or cancel it, just go to Portfolio page, tap the Orders button to navigate to order page.
