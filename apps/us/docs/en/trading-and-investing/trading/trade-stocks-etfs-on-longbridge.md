To place a trade for Stocks or ETFs, review the following steps:

1\. Go to the stock quote page, tap **Trade** button, select **Buy** or **Sell** for the order direction.

![1.png](https://longbridgeus.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17967491558287)![2.png](https://longbridgeus.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17967491558799)

2\. Input all required order parameters, like order type, price, quantity, time in force, etc., then tap **Review order** to review the order details. Once order information is confirmed, tap **Confirm & submit**, the order will be submitted to upstream broker.

![3.png](https://longbridgeus.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17967491559439)![4.png](https://longbridgeus.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17967491564047)![5.png](https://longbridgeus.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17967512897807)

3\. If the order is in a pending status and you want to modify or cancel it, go to Portfolio page and tap the **Orders** button.

![6.png](https://longbridgeus.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17967491566479)![7.png](https://longbridgeus.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17967512898831)![8.png](https://longbridgeus.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/17967512900367)

4\. Tap **Edit button**, change order parameters and resubmit for order modification, or you can tap **Cancel order** to directly cancel the order.
