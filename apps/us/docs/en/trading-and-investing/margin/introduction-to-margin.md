## About Close Only

Currently, there are only four scenarios that will cause an account to trigger "Close Only" mode:

-   Outstanding required maintenance call
-   Day trades placed while the account was under an equity maintenance call
-   4 Reg T call violations in the past 12 months
-   Overdue Intraday margin call（Triggered by unresolved Intraday Margin Call after T+5.）
