### Starter (Free)

Get started with Longbridge AI's core AI capabilities at no cost.

**Features:**

-   **AI Chat**: Chat with AI agents, ask follow-ups, and get deeper market insights

-   **Basic Models**: Access to standard AI models

-   **Limited Usage**: Suitable for occasional and exploratory use

-   **AI Recommendations:** AI-generated investment analysis with Reg BI disclosures

-   **AI Trade Plans:** AI-generated trade plans with entry, stop-loss, and target prices

-   **AI Portfolio P&L Analysis**: AI analysis of your portfolio performance

**Limitations**:

-   Limited monthly usage quota

-   Custom strategies not supported

### Plus

The plan for investors who rely on AI insights in their day-to-day investing

**Includes all Starter features, plus**:

-   **Higher Usage Limits:** Increased monthly quota for frequent use

-   **Custom Strategies**: Up to 20 personalized investment strategies

### Pro

The plan for active investors who need more advanced analysis tools and greater flexibility.

**Includes all Plus features, plus**:

-   **2.5x Plus Quota:** More quota for intensive research and analysis

-   **More Custom Strategies**: Up to 50 personalized investment strategies

-   **Third-Party Tools**: Access to third-party Connector tools for extended capabilities

### Max

The ultimate plan for professional investors and power users who demand the highest quotas and priority

**Includes all Pro features, plus**:

-   **5x Plus Quota:** The highest usage quotas that support professional analysis

-   **Most Custom Strategies**: Up to 100 personalized investment strategies

-   **Priority Model Access**: Priority access to AI models during peak times

## Usage Limits

-   Your monthly usage quota is distributed evenly across four weeks and refreshes every week.

-   Unused weekly quota does not roll over to the next week.

-   All AI activities consume usage quotas, including AI chat, tool calls, and analysis features.

## Upgrade & Downgrade

-   **Upgrade**: Upgrade at any time. The new plan takes effect immediately, and you pay only the prorated price difference.

-   **Downgrade**: The downgrade takes effect at the start of your next billing cycle. You keep your current plan benefits until then.

-   **Cancel**: Your current plan remains active until the end of the billing period. After expiration, your account automatically downgrades to the Starter plan.

## FAQs

**Q1: Does the Starter plan expire?**

A: No. Starter is permanently free and automatically activated upon registration.

**Q2: Will my usage quota refresh immediately after upgrading?**

A: Yes. Your remaining weekly usage is recalculated immediately based on the quota of your new plan.

**Q3: What is Reg BI disclosure?**

A: Regulation Best Interest (Reg BI) requires broker-dealers to provide important disclosures when making investment recommendations to retail customers. When applicable, AI-generated investment recommendations include disclosures regarding risks, fees, and potential conflicts of interest, consistent with applicable regulatory requirements.

**Q4: Will I lose my data after cancellation?**

A: No. After your account downgrades to Starter, your chat history and account data are retained. However, your future usage will be subject to the Starter plan's limits.

**Q5: Where can I view my AI Subscription plan?**

A: Click on the notifications bell icon on the home page > select **Longbridge AI** > click the Settings icon > select **Upgrade Plan** > toggle between plans until you find the one labeled "Current Plan".
