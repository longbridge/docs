Purchases made on an Apple device are billed by Apple, so refunds can't be issued directly by us. To request a refund, please visit Apple's support page [here](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118223). Once Apple processes your request, your access to the purchased item will be updated automatically.

For additional questions, please reach out to our customer support team by emailing support@longbridge.com or by leveraging the live-chat feature in the mobile app.
