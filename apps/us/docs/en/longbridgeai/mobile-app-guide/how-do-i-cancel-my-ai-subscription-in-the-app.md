You can cancel your AI subscription directly in the Longbridge app.

## Steps to Cancel

1.  Open the Longbridge app.

2.  Go to Longbridge **AI** section.

3.  Open the **Settings** widget.

4.  Select **Billing**.

5.  Select **Cancel**.

6.  Follow the on-screen prompts to complete the cancellation.

## How to Confirm Your Subscription Was Canceled

Before cancellation, the Billing page displays **Cancel**.

After the subscription has been canceled, the option changes to **Renew**, indicating that the subscription is no longer set to renew.

If you would like to subscribe again, select **Renew** from the **Billing** section. You can also select **Upgrade plan** from **Settings** if you would like to choose a different AI subscription plan.
