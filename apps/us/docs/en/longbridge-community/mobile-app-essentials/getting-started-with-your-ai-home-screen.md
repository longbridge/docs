# Your AI Home Screen: See What Matters, Ask Anything

Your Longbridge home screen works two ways. **Tap** the cards (we call them **widgets**) to catch up on market news, check your portfolio and watchlist, and discover investment ideas and opportunities you can act on. **Ask** a question in plain words using the **Ask me anything** bar at the bottom, and Longbridge AI will explain it or help you dig deeper.

You can add, remove and rearrange cards at any time, so the screen can grow with you.

## 1\. Set your investor profile

Take a quick quiz to find your **investor portrait**, such as Maverick, Keeper, Protector or Pioneer. It describes how you like to invest. Your **risk profile** is separate. It comes from the questions you answered when you opened your account and decides which **strategies** and **signals** you can unlock. Together, they help Longbridge AI show you what matters most to you.

## 2\. Explore your home screen

### AI widgets

![](https://longbridge-group.jp.larksuite.com/space/api/box/stream/download/asynccode/?code=Mjk3MjNlNzJmMDQzOGZkNmFiMTllZDkzMzAwZGY0YjBfTDBINm1GQUJWUlVXYk16RWZMdjFlbXR4Z00xYzlic0hfVG9rZW46UHZSeGJuaUVMb0JPNEJ4TGQwT2pjTThUcGpkXzE3OTEzOTEzOTI6MTc5MTM5NDk5Ml9WNA&add_watermark=true&scene_type=CCM)

These widgets form **your investing loop**, bringing every step of investing into one place, from spotting news to reviewing results: **Catalyst → Signal → Trade Plan → Profit & Loss**. Your **Strategies** are the lens that turns Catalyst news into Signals and Trade Plans made for you. Each step leads naturally to the next, but you're free to start wherever you like. If you're not sure where to begin, here's an easy way to draft your first trade plan:

1.  **See what's happening · Catalysts:** news and events about the stocks in your watchlist and holdings.

2.  **Choose your style · Strategies:** pick a ready-made strategy that fits you, like Quality Value, or describe your own in plain words. Those matching your risk profile unlock automatically, others with a tap on **Confirm and unlock**.

3.  **Spot an opportunity · Signals:** when the news matches your strategy, it shows up here as a signal. Tap **Analyze with AI** to learn more, or **Draft trade plan** to take the next step.

4.  **Make a plan · Trade Plan:** created from a Signal, your draft shows when to buy, when to sell and how much to invest. If you want to go ahead, **confirm it in the chat**. Nothing is placed until you do.

After you trade, **Profit & Loss** shows how your trades performed. Longbridge AI remembers your decisions and results, so each round of your investing loop is more tailored to you.

### Classic widgets

Need the everyday essentials? A range of classic widgets, such as **Markets**, **Watchlist** and **Portfolio**, keeps quotes, holdings and trading one tap away. Browse the full selection in the widget manager.

### Make it yours

Scroll to the bottom of the home screen and tap **Widgets** to add or remove cards.

> **Prefer the Classic layout?**
>
> Tap the **grid icon** in the top-right corner to switch back to the classic page at any time.

## 3\. Ask Longbridge AI anything

Have a question along the way? Type or speak it in the **Ask me anything** bar at the bottom of the screen. Longbridge AI chats with you in plain words and understands what you are looking at, so you can ask about a stock, a news or a term at any time.

> Longbridge AI provides market information and analysis to support your research. Signals, strategies and trade plans are for information only and are not personalized investment advice. Investing involves risk, including possible loss of principal, and all investment decisions are yours to make.
