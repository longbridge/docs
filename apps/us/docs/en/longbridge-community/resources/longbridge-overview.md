## **Who We Are**

Long Bridge Securities LLC (Longbridge) is a U.S. broker-dealer registered with the Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and a member of the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA), offering an online brokerage platform for trading in U.S. securities. 

Cryptocurrency services are offered separately through Longbridge Crypto LLC, with transactions processed through Bakkt Financial Solutions I, LLC.

Our platform combines advanced trading capabilities with an AI-enabled assistant that helps you ask smarter questions, uncover market trends, and deepen your investment knowledge—while you stay fully in control of every decision.

## **Regulations and Protections**

### **Regulated Broker**

We are a U.S. broker-dealer registered with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC) and a member of the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA).

We are committed to maintaining full compliance with all applicable securities laws and regulations, and we encourage customers to review our regulatory status and membership at: [FINRA BrokerCheck](https://brokercheck.finra.org/).

### **SIPC Protection**

Longbridge is a member of the Securities Investor Protection Corporation (SIPC). SIPC protects securities customers of its members up to $500,000 (including $250,000 for claims for cash).

Customer brokerage accounts introduced by Longbridge are carried and custodied by Apex Clearing Corporation, a registered broker-dealer and member of FINRA and SIPC. SIPC coverage for customer accounts applies at Apex, where your securities and cash are held. SIPC protection does not cover losses due to market fluctuations in the value of securities.

For more information about SIPC coverage, including answers to frequently asked questions and eligibility details, please visit: [www.sipc.org](https://www.sipc.org).

### **Custody of Customer Assets**

Longbridge does not directly hold or safeguard customer securities or funds. All customer securities and cash held in brokerage accounts opened through Longbridge are custodied by Apex Clearing Corporation, which performs custody, clearing, trade settlement, and account recordkeeping functions.

You will receive account statements, trade confirmations, and other required account documentation from Apex, which reflect the official books and records of your account. Longbridge provides the trading platform, account interface, and customer support services, while Apex performs all clearing and custodial functions.

#### _**Important Notes**_

-   SIPC protection applies only to the custody and safekeeping of securities and cash held in a brokerage account, not to investment performance or market losses.
-   Cryptocurrency assets are not securities held in a Long Bridge Securities LLC brokerage account and are not protected by SIPC.
