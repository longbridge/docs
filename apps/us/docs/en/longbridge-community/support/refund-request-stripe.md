If you made your purchase on an Android device through Stripe (most purchases made in our mobile app on an Android device are processed through Stripe), please contact our Customer Support team to request a refund. Our Customer Support team can be contacted at support@longbridge.com or by using the live-chat feature in the mobile app. Include the email address used for the purchase, the purchase date, and the amount charged, and we'll review your request.

**Note:** not all purchases are eligible for a refund
