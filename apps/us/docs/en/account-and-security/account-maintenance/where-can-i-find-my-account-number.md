You can find your 8 digit account number on an account statement or a trade confirmation. If you have not received an account statement or trade confirmation, you can also find your account number by navigating to the transfers screen in the mobile app.

### **Finding your account number on a statement or trade confirmation**

1.  Login to the Longbridge app

2.  Navigate to the **portfolio** page

3.  Scroll until you find the **Docs & Statements** button and click to open

    ![IMG\_1269.jpg](https://longbridgeus.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/18025840995215)

4.  You will see a menu showing monthly statements, trade confirmations, and tax documents. Your account number will appear on page 1 of the account statement and page 1 of the trade confirmation.

### **Finding your account number through the transfers screen**

You do not need to process a transfer to view your account number, the following steps will simply show you how to locate the account number.

1.  Login to the Longbridge app

2.  Navigate to the **portfolio** page

3.  Scroll until you find the **Transfers** button and click to open

    ![IMG\_1270.jpg](https://longbridgeus.zendesk.com/hc/article_attachments/18025840995471)

4.  Click the **deposit** option

5.  Click the **wire** option

6.  Under the **Payee Reference/ FFC** heading, you will find your account number.

If you are unable to locate your account number, please [contact customer support](/articles/17350120095503).
