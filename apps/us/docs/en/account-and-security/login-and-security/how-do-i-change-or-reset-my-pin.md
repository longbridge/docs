Your PIN helps keep your account secure. You use it to view your portfolio, place trades, manage your account settings, and more. You can change your PIN at any time in the Longbridge app. If you’ve forgotten it, you can reset it.

### **Change your PIN**

1.  Log in to the Longbridge app.

2.  Tap your **profile icon**.

3.  Tap **Security and Privacy**.

4.  Tap **Change PIN**.

5.  Enter your current PIN

6.  Enter your new PIN and click **confirm**

### **Forgot your PIN? Reset it**

1.  Log in to the Longbridge app.

2.  Tap your **profile icon**.

3.  Tap **Security and Privacy**.

4.  Tap **Change PIN**, then tap **Reset PIN**.

5.  Enter the verification code we send to your phone number or email

6.  Enter your new PIN and click **confirm**

### **Tips for choosing a PIN**

-   Avoid PINs that are easy to guess, such as 1234, 0000, or your birth year.

-   Don’t use the same PIN as your phone, bank card, or other accounts.

-   Never share your PIN with anyone. Longbridge will never ask for your PIN by phone, email, or text.

**Still need help?** If you can’t change or reset your PIN, contact our customer service team through **Contact us** in the app or on our website.
