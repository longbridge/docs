We send verification codes by SMS or email to help keep your account secure. If your code hasn't arrived, the steps below resolve most cases. Find the method you use below.

> **Keep your code private.** Longbridge will never ask you for your verification code, and our customer support team can't see, send, or read it out. Don't share it with anyone, including anyone who says they're from Longbridge.

# SMS

If you didn't receive your SMS verification code by text message, work through the steps below.

## **Before you start: Confirm your phone number**

-   **Opening an account:** make sure the phone number you entered is correct, including the right country code. A mistyped number is the most common cause.

-   **Logging in:** the code is sent to the phone number on your account. Make sure you still have access to that number.

### **Step 1. Refresh your mobile network signal**

-   Turn on airplane mode, wait about 10 seconds, then turn it off to reconnect to the network. Then request the code again.

### **Step 2. Check SMS blocking settings**

Some security apps or phone systems mistakenly block verification messages. Check:

-   The spam or junk message folder on your phone.

-   The blocked message records of any third-party security apps.

-   Whether you ever replied STOP to a text from us. If you did, your carrier may block further messages, and replying START can turn them back on.

### **Step 3. Check your phone's SMS receiving function**

-   Make sure you haven't accidentally turned off text message notifications. Confirm that text notifications are turned on in the settings on your mobile device.

-   Do Not Disturb or Focus mode can silence or hold message alerts, so you may not notice the code arriving. Turn it off, or allow messages as an exception, and check again.

### **Step 4. Restart your device and check your SIM card**

-   Restart your phone: power off, wait a minute, then power on.

-   Reinsert your SIM card: power off, remove the SIM, reinstall it securely, then power on again. If you use an eSIM, skip this and simply restart your device.

### **Step 5. Confirm carrier and plan status**

-   **Plan status:** check that your mobile plan is active and your carrier account is in good standing, with your line fully working and not temporarily restricted.

-   **Message filters:** some plans, such as corporate lines, certain prepaid plans, or family plans, block automated or commercial text messages by default. If needed, contact your carrier and ask them to make sure your number can receive these messages.

### **Step 6. Confirm local network and carrier status**

In rare cases, your carrier or network may have a temporary problem in your area. Wait a few minutes and try again, or check whether others on the same carrier nearby are having the same trouble.

**Tip:** Request one code at a time and wait a few minutes before requesting another. Too many requests in a short time may temporarily stop new codes from being sent.

#### **Still not working?**

If you need urgent help, or the issue continues after these steps, contact our customer support team. Select Contact us in our app or on our website to reach us by live chat or email at support@longbridge.com.

Our support team can't send or read out your code. We can help you troubleshoot and, after we verify your identity, help you switch to another verification method if one is available for your account. If you're opening an account and SMS still doesn't arrive, you can choose email verification instead.

If you no longer have access to your phone number, see “If you no longer have access to your phone number or email address” below.

# Email

If you didn't receive your verification code by email, work through the steps below.

## **Before you start: Confirm your email address** 

If this happens while you're opening an account, first make sure the email address you entered is correct. A small typo, such as a missing letter or the wrong domain (for example, “gmial.com” instead of “gmail.com”), is a common cause. If you're logging in, the code is sent to the email address on your account, so make sure you still have access to it.

### **Step 1. Wait a moment and request a new code** 

Most verification emails arrive within seconds, but delivery can sometimes take a few minutes. If the code still hasn't arrived, request a new one. If you receive more than one email, use the code from the most recent one.

### **Step 2. Check your spam, junk, and other folders**

Your email provider may have filtered the message out of your main inbox. Check:

-   Your spam or junk folder.

-   Other inbox tabs or folders, such as Promotions or Updates in Gmail, or Other in Outlook's Focused Inbox.

-   Any filters or rules you've set up that automatically move, archive, or delete certain emails.

If you find the email, mark it as “Not spam” so future emails from us reach your inbox.

### **Step 3. Add Longbridge to your safe senders list** 

To help our emails reach you, add noreply@longbridge.com to your contacts or safe senders list, and check that you haven't previously blocked this address.

### **Step 4. Check your email app and mailbox**

-   **Refresh your inbox:** If you’re using an email app, make sure it’s connected to the internet and syncing. Try checking your email in a web browser instead.

-   **Check your storage:** If your mailbox is full, new emails may not be delivered. Delete some emails to free up space, then request a new code.

-   **Check forwarding settings:** If your email automatically forwards to another address, check that inbox too.

### **Step 5. Using a work or school email?**

Company and school email systems often have strict security filters that hold or quarantine automated emails, including verification codes. These emails may not appear in your spam folder at all. You can:

-   Check your email quarantine, if your organization provides one.

-   Ask your IT team to release the email and allow messages from noreply@longbridge.com.

-   If you're opening an account, use a personal email address or a U.S. (+1) mobile phone number instead.

#### **Still not working?** 

If you need urgent help, or the issue continues after these steps, contact our customer support team. Select Contact us in our app or on our website to reach us by live chat or email at support@longbridge.com.

Our support team can't send or read out your code. We can help you troubleshoot and, after we verify your identity, help you switch to another verification method if one is available for your account. If you're opening an account and email still doesn't arrive, you can use a U.S. (+1) mobile phone number to receive your code by text instead

## If you no longer have access to your phone number or email address

If you've changed your phone number, lost your phone, or can no longer open the email address on your account, contact our customer support team. Select Contact us in our app or on our website.

-   We'll need to verify your identity before making any changes to your account. This may involve extra steps, and it helps protect you from anyone trying to take over your account.

-   We can't send or read out a verification code, and we will never ask you for one.

-   For your security, please don't send passwords, verification codes, or images of your ID by email or chat unless we've given you a secure way to do so.

> **Tip:** In most cases, confirming your phone number or email address, checking for blocked messages or your spam folder, and allowing messages from us resolves the issue. If you still can't receive your code, our customer support team can help troubleshoot further.
