Typically, individual accounts can be completed in just a few minutes. However, occasionally, we may need to ask for some additional information in order to open your account. For example, if you have recently moved and changed your address, we may need to verify your new address with supporting documents such as a recent utility bill or bank statement showing your current address. Please feel free to reach out to [support@longbridge.com](mailto:support@longbridge.com) for additional information or if you have any questions on the account opening process.
