Typically, opening an individual account takes just a few minutes.

Occasionally, we may need more information before we can open your account. For example, if you've recently moved, we may ask for a document that shows your current address, such as a recent utility bill or bank statement.

If you have any questions about opening an account, contact us at **support@longbridge.com** or **through the in-app live chat.**
