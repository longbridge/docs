At this time, Individual U.S. persons are eligible to open an account with Long Bridge Securities. U.S. persons include United States citizens and permanent residents of the United States. Currently we are offering individual accounts. Please check back in with us as we will be supporting more account types in the future!

## **What Information Do I need to Provide to Open an Account?**

Account opening can be completed in just a few minutes by providing your name, address, social security number or Tax ID and answering a few short questions.
